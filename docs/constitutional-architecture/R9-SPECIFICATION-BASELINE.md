## PART 11 — D-SERIES CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS

### 11.1 D-2 v1.2 — Epistemic Chain (§IV)

The D-2 Epistemic Chain establishes the philosophical foundation for RT-09:

**Chain:** Reality → Observation → **Evidence** → **Interpretation** → **Belief** → **Knowledge** → Understanding → Decision → Action

RT-09 realizes stages: Evidence, Interpretation, Belief, Knowledge (stages 2–5 of D3's 10-stage chain).

D-2 defines:
- Evidence: "observation that has been evaluated as relevant and sufficiently reliable to bear on a specific claim... not raw observation — it is observation assessed against a standard"
- Interpretation: "assigning meaning to evidence by relating it to existing knowledge, existing models, and background understanding"
- Belief: "a held position about what is the case, formed through the interpretation of evidence, maintained with explicit confidence"
- Knowledge: "belief that has been validated — subjected to scrutiny, tested for coherence with the established body of knowledge, and found to hold... always provisional"

### 11.2 D3 v1.0 — Reality Fabric (§6.2 Epistemic Chain Stage Specifications)

RT-09 manages Stages 2-5 of the D3 Epistemic Chain:

- Forbidden: Direct grounding of Beliefs, KnowledgeClaims without passing through Interpretation

**Stage 3 — Interpretation:**
- Must cite at least one EvidenceRecord
- Must declare InterpretiveFrame explicitly
- May NOT reduce uncertainty below minimum of cited EvidenceRecords

**Stage 4 — Belief:**
- Must cite at least one Interpretation

**Stage 5 — KnowledgeClaim (Validation Gate — EP-T4):**
- Must cite at least one Belief AND a completed ValidationProcess
- Dual requirement: justification AND validation both required
- KnowledgeStatus: {validating, validated, contested, superseded}

## PART 13 — OPEN CONSTITUTIONAL QUESTIONS

### 13.1 EpistemicProtocol Registration Authority

A0 §3.10 lists "EpistemicProtocol (registered interpretation protocols)" as an owned object. The constitution establishes that interpretation protocols must be registered (D6 §4.3, D-2 §XI). However, the specific constitutional actor with authority to register new EpistemicProtocols is not explicitly assigned in A0 §3.10 or A1. The registration process appears to require Amendment Process (D7 §6.1) or at minimum RT-16 authorization given that new protocols change what RT-09 may constitutionally do. 

**Risk to specification:** RS-10 (Managed Objects) must characterize EpistemicProtocol lifecycle including registration; the specifier must determine from D6 §4.3 and the amendment architecture whether protocol registration requires RT-16 or whether a lower-authority process suffices.

### 13.2 DKS Transition Authority

A0 §3.10 INV-4 establishes that Knowledge States transition constitutionally. The specific authority required to transition a Knowledge State from DKS-1 to DKS-3 (Contested) or DKS-4 (Degraded) is not explicitly characterized in A0 §3.10. D6 §5.2 defines the states but does not specify the gating process.

**Risk to specification:** RS-15 (State Machine) must characterize all KnowledgeState transitions. The specifier must determine from D6, D4, and A0 what gate processing is required for each DKS transition.

### 13.3 Contradiction Register Resolution Protocol

A0 §3.10 R6 requires RT-09 to maintain the Contradiction Register. D5 §3.2 Stage 5 states contradictions must produce a Contradiction Record, not silent overwrite. The constitutional resolution protocol (how contradictions are resolved and ContradictionRecords closed) is distributed across RT-09, RT-10, RT-11 (deliberation), and RT-15 (domain authority). The RS-12 (Internal Processes) for RT-09 must document how RT-09's role in contradiction detection ends and RT-10/RT-11's role in resolution begins.

### 13.4 Reality Gap Register Class B vs. Class A

A0 §3.10 states RealityGapEntry is produced "to RT-03 as Class B Kernel Manifest output." D4 KI-023 mandates Reality Gap entries for undetected observations. The classification as Class B (not requiring full gate processing) is constitutionally established, but RS-12 must carefully document the distinction between Evidence Object formation (Class A through RT-03) and RealityGapEntry creation (Class B Manifest).

### 13.5 Temporal Validity Tracking Scope

A0 §3.10 R8 requires RT-09 to "track temporal validity of all epistemic objects — flag expired knowledge states." The scope of "all epistemic objects" may include objects owned by other runtimes (RT-10's Domain Understanding Models, RT-11's CUM). RT-09's direct tracking obligation is for its owned objects (EvidenceObjects, InterpretationRecords, BeliefObjects, KnowledgeClaims, KnowledgeStates). RS-05 and RS-12 must be precise about whether RT-09 tracks only its owned objects or whether it also monitors upstream/downstream objects for temporal validity.
