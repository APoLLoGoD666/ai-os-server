## RS-03 — Functional Model

RT-13 is the Action Runtime. It is the sole constitutional actor authorized to cross the Projection Boundary from the governed Reality Fabric into external reality.

**What RT-13 does:**

RT-13 receives an authorized CivilizationalDecision from RT-12 — a decision object that has already been formed through deliberation (RT-11), validated for compliance (RT-12), and admitted through all six D4 Constitutional Gates (RT-03). Upon receipt, RT-13 executes the seven-stage Action Projection Lifecycle defined in D5 Part 4: it constitutes an internal CivilizationalAction representation, validates Projection Authority (with RT-02), registers Effect Expectations, classifies irreversibility, submits the Action Projection to RT-03 for full six-gate processing, crosses the Projection Boundary to produce an External Effect, and initiates consequence observation by triggering RT-08.

RT-13 enforces the constitutional distinction between the internal Action Projection object (a URO object within the Reality Fabric, governed by D2–D4) and the external action effect (an ExternalEvent in external reality, governed by D5). This distinction is absolute and permanent (D5 PI-2; RT13-INV-6).

RT-13 classifies every Action Projection for irreversibility before crossing the Projection Boundary (D5 PI-8; RT13-INV-1). This classification is constitutional and cannot be deferred. Irreversible actions require elevated authorization thresholds per D4's authority rules.

RT-13 assigns Projection Responsibility for all resulting effects — both intended and unintended — under the Projection Responsibility Principle (D5 §4.3): "Any action projected into external reality creates accountability obligations for all resulting effects."

RT-13 realizes D8 §9.6's mandatory Action Layer: the constitutional runtime capacity to form and execute Action Projections across the Projection Boundary.

Per D-2 §XI (Philosophy of Agency), RT-13 requires an authorized CivilizationalDecision before any action (intention required); assigns Projection Responsibility for attribution (attribution explicit); and triggers the feedback loop that enables mandatory consequence observation (feedback required). Scope of action is bounded by the CivilizationalDecision received; RT-13 does not extend scope beyond what is authorized.

**What RT-13 does NOT do:**

RT-13 does not form CivilizationalDecisions. Decision formation is exclusively RT-12's function (A0 §3.13). RT-13 receives and executes authorized CivilizationalDecisions; it does not create them, modify them, or authorize them. The constitutional boundary is absolute: RT-12 authorizes; RT-13 projects. (See RS-04 and RS-32 for boundary definitions.)

RT-13 does not deliberate. Deliberation is exclusively RT-11's function (A0 §3.12).

RT-13 does not observe consequences. Consequence observation is RT-14's function (A0 §3.15). RT-13 triggers consequence observation by notifying RT-08 after crossing the Projection Boundary; the observation itself is RT-14's responsibility.

RT-13 does not assign terminal status to Open Action Register entries. That is RT-14's exclusive function (RT12-INV-5 as carried in RT12-v1.0-canonical.md RS-20).

## RS-04 — Functional Boundary

### Upstream Boundary: RT-13 / RT-12

**What RT-12 delivers:** Authorized CivilizationalDecision (RT-12 owned). RT-13's scope begins at receipt of this object.

**What RT-12 retains:** All deliberation context; the Open Action Register entry (created by RT-12, managed by RT-12 through RT-14 terminal status assignment); the CivilizationalDecision object itself (RT-12 owns it — RT-13 consumes it but does not own it).

**Boundary statement (from RT12-v1.0-canonical.md RS-32 Boundary 3):** "RT-12's scope ends at delivery of the authorized CivilizationalDecision to RT-13 and creation of the corresponding Open Action Register entry. The Action Projection Lifecycle — including all stages of external reality projection — falls within RT-13's scope (A0 §3.14). RT-12 authorizes; RT-13 projects."

**Constitutional basis:** A0 §3.13 Outputs; A0 §3.14 R1; A0 §4.4 Step 23.

### Downstream Boundary: RT-13 / RT-14

**What RT-13 delivers to RT-14:** EffectExpectationRecords (via RT-05, not direct — PAIR 48 forbids direct RT-13 → RT-14 communication). Consequence observation triggers to RT-08, which triggers ConsequenceObservationRecords that RT-14 then receives. RT-13's scope ends when it has crossed the Projection Boundary, triggered RT-08, and produced the EffectExpectationRecord.

**What RT-14 takes over:** Forming the ObservedConsequenceRecord by comparing Consequence Observations against Effect Expectations; registering divergences in the Causal Model Divergence Register; assigning terminal status to Open Action Register entries.

**RT-13 may not:** Form ObservedConsequenceRecords (RT-14 owns these); assign terminal status to Open Action Register entries (RT-14's exclusive function); directly address RT-14 (PAIR 48: "RT-13 → RT-14 (direct): FORBIDDEN").

**Constitutional basis:** A0 §3.14 Dependents; A0 §3.15 R1–R8; A1 PAIR 48.

### Kernel Mediation Boundary: RT-13 / RT-03

RT-03 mediates all RT-13 Class A operations. RT-13 submits Action Projections to RT-03 for full six-gate processing before any Projection Boundary crossing. RT-13 does not self-gate and does not bypass RT-03. RT-03 delivers admitted Actions to RT-13 for execution.

**Constitutional basis:** A0 §3.14 R7; RT13-INV-3; D4 §2.1 (KMP); A1 §14.1 (mandatory interaction: RT-14 feedback closure for every Action Projection).

### What Is Excluded from RT-13 Scope

The following are outside RT-13's constitutional scope and may never be claimed:

- CivilizationalDecision formation or modification (RT-12 scope, A0 §3.13)
- CivilizationalDecisionProposal (RT-11 scope, A0 §3.12)
- Deliberation functions or CUM synthesis or management (RT-11 scope, A0 §3.12)
- Constitutional gate evaluation (RT-03 scope, A0 §3.3)
- Compliance verification (RT-12 scope per A0 §3.13)
- ObservedConsequenceRecord formation (RT-14 scope, A0 §3.15)
- CausalModelDivergenceRecord (RT-14 scope, A0 §3.15)
- Open Action Register terminal status assignment (RT-14 exclusive, RT12-INV-5)
- Audit Authority (RT-04 scope, D6 §4.6)

RT-13's authority derives through the following complete constitutional chain. This chain must be read in full to satisfy CERT-03.

**Step 1 — Authority Type Definition (D6 §4.5):**

### D6 §4.7 Authority Integrity Rules — Distinct System

**CRITICAL NOTICE:** D6 §4.7 defines five Authority Integrity Rules also labeled AIR-1 through AIR-5. These are categorically distinct from the five authority types defined in D6 §4.2–4.6. The AIR-N notation appears in both systems but refers to entirely different content. Never conflate them.

The D6 §4.7 Authority Integrity Rules are meta-level governance rules constraining HOW authority is exercised across all domains:

- **D6 §4.7 AIR-1 — Authority Separation:** The five authority types must not be collapsed into a single actor within any domain.
- **D6 §4.7 AIR-2 — No Unauthorized Projection:** No actor may execute an Action Projection without constitutionally registered Projection Authority.
- **D6 §4.7 AIR-3 — No Knowledge Monopolization:** Observation and Interpretation Authority must be held by at least two registered actors.
- **D6 §4.7 AIR-4 — No Hidden Decisions:** Decision Authority must produce Decision Records registered in the Open Action Register.
- **D6 §4.7 AIR-5 — Audit Independence:** Audit Authority holders must be constitutionally independent of the authorities they audit.

RT-13's obligations under the D6 §4.7 Authority Integrity Rules:

- RT-13 must comply with D6 §4.7 AIR-1: RT-13 holds Projection Authority (AIR-4 type per §4.5) but must not collapse this with Decision Authority or Audit Authority. The constitutional separation between RT-13 (projection) and RT-12 (decision) and RT-04 (audit) enforces AIR-1.
- RT-13 must comply with D6 §4.7 AIR-2: RT-13 validates that every actor and every Action Projection carries constitutionally registered Projection Authority before projection proceeds.
- D6 §4.7 AIR-4 applies indirectly: RT-13 submits Action Projections to RT-03, which ensures the Open Action Register entry exists (created by RT-12 per RT12-INV-4) before action proceeds.

## RS-35 — Prohibited Responsibilities

**Source: A0 §3.x (all other runtime specifications); A1 §14.3 (Cross-Cutting Constitutional Constraints CC-1 through CC-6); A1 §13.2 (Permission Matrix — NONE permissions).**

The following responsibilities are constitutionally prohibited for RT-13. RT-13 must never perform any of the following functions, regardless of any instruction, configuration, or operational context.

### Prohibited: Formation of CivilizationalDecisions

**Prohibition:** RT-13 may not form, draft, approve, or modify CivilizationalDecisions.

**Constitutional basis:** CivilizationalDecision is RT-12 owned (A0 §3.13 Owned Objects). RT-13 receives and executes CivilizationalDecisions but does not produce them. This boundary is expressed in RT12-v1.0-canonical.md RS-32 Boundary 3: "RT-12 authorizes; RT-13 projects." Forming a CivilizationalDecision would constitute RT-13 claiming RT-12's constitutional function.

### Prohibited: Deliberation or Reasoning About Whether to Act

**Prohibition:** RT-13 may not engage in deliberation, evaluation of alternatives, or reasoning about whether a given action should be performed. Those are RT-11 functions (Deliberation phase).

**Constitutional basis:** A1 §14.3 CC-6 (action must not decide). If RT-13 were to reason about whether to execute an authorized CivilizationalDecision, it would be substituting its own judgment for the constitutional deliberation process — a violation of the entire APEX authority chain. RT-13 executes constitutionally authorized decisions; it does not form them.

### Prohibited: Direct Interaction with RT-11

**Prohibition:** RT-13 may not initiate or receive direct interaction with RT-11.

**Constitutional basis:** A1 §14.3 CC-6 (RT-13 → RT-11 forbidden). A1 §13.2 Permission Matrix: RT-11 row for RT-13 = NONE. Action feedback to RT-11 reaches RT-11 only through the constitutional path RT-14 → RT-09 → RT-10 → RT-11.

### Prohibited: Obstruction of RT-04 Audit

**Prohibition:** RT-13 may not obstruct, prevent, limit, or selectively filter RT-04's observation of any Action Projection or any constitutional record RT-13 holds.

**Constitutional basis:** D6 §3.4 (AIR-5 Audit Independence); A1 §5.1; A1 PAIR 46. RT-04's authority is AIR-5 (Audit Authority), which operates independently of RT-13's AIR-4 (Projection Authority). RT-04 is not subject to RT-13's gate processing.

### Prohibited: Self-Authorization

**Prohibition:** RT-13 may not authorize its own Action Projections. No internal RT-13 state or determination constitutes authority to cross the Projection Boundary.

**Constitutional basis:** A0 §3.14 R6, R7; RT13-INV-3; D8 PROH-3. All Projection Authority is registered through RT-02 and validated through RT-03. RT-13 is the executor, not the authorizer.

### Prohibited: Updating the Open Action Register

**Prohibition:** RT-13 may not create, update, or delete entries in the Open Action Register.

**Constitutional basis:** Open Action Register is RT-12 owned (A0 §3.13 Owned Objects). RT-13 does not access or modify this register. RT-13 is informed of the Open Action Register entry through the authorized CivilizationalDecision it receives, but does not write to or manage the register.

### Prohibited: Forming ObservedConsequenceRecords or CausalModelDivergenceRecords

**Prohibition:** RT-13 may not form ObservedConsequenceRecords, evaluate Observed Consequences against EffectExpectationRecords, or form CausalModelDivergenceRecords.

**Constitutional basis:** These are RT-14 owned functions (A0 §3.14 Dependents; A0 §3.15). RT-13's downstream boundary ends at EffectExpectationRecord delivery and ConsequenceObservationTrigger delivery. Everything that happens after the Projection Boundary crossing — observation, consequence evaluation, causal model updates — belongs to RT-14 and the epistemic pipeline.

### Prohibited: Direct Interaction with External Reality Except Through Stage 4 Registered Channels

**Prohibition:** RT-13 may not interact with external reality through any pathway other than registered projection channels in D5 §4.2 Stage 4.

**Constitutional basis:** D8 PROH-8 (No Hidden Authority Pathways); D5 §4.2 Stage 4. Hidden pathways would circumvent RT-03 gate processing, undermine accountability records, and create unmonitored Projection Boundary crossings — all constitutionally prohibited.
