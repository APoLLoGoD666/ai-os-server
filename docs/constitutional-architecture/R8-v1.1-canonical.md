### RS-03 — Purpose

RT-08 (Observation Runtime) receives all signals from external reality, maintains the Observer Register, forms constitutionally valid Observation Records with required uncertainty and provenance attributes, and routes them into the Knowledge Runtime — serving as the sole constitutional entry point for external reality information into the Reality Fabric.

RT-08 is the constitutional realization of D5's Observation Projection Lifecycle Stages 1–3 and the "Reality → Observation" transition in the D-2 Epistemic Chain. RT-08 does not interpret, judge, authorize, enforce, remember, predict, plan, or govern. RT-08 observes. All RT-08 Observation Records are immutable after formation. All RT-08 outputs enter the fabric exclusively through RT-03 gate processing. The Observation Boundary maintained by RT-08 is the only constitutional entry point for external reality information (D3 RF-A6; RT08-INV-1). A system without RT-08 operational is not a constitutionally valid APEX instance (D8 §9.7).

### RS-05 — Responsibilities and Obligations

The twelve obligations of RT-08, derived in full from A0-v1.1.1 §3.9:

**O8-1 — Observer Register Maintenance:** RT-08 shall maintain the Observer Register — the complete, current record of all active observation sources and their constitutional parameters. The Observer Register records for each registered observer: constitutional actor standing per RT-01 ActorProfile, observation scope specifying which ExternalRealitySegments and ExternalState types may be observed, calibration record constituting a CompetenceDemonstration with calibration history per D3 §5.6 requirement 3, health assessment per D3 §5.6 requirement 4, and authority chain traceable to FoundingRatification per D3 §5.6 requirement 5. The Observer Register is the constitutional precondition for all observation — no observation channel may activate unless its observer appears in the Register with a valid, current registration. The Observer Register is RT-08's operational realization of the five D3 §5.6 Observer Infrastructure requirements. An observer whose registration has lapsed, whose calibration record has expired, or whose authority chain is broken may not participate in observation until the deficiency is remediated. (Constitutional source: A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 R1; D3 §5.6)

**O8-2 — Observation Channel Registration and Signal Reception:** RT-08 shall receive signals from external reality exclusively through registered observation channels. No external reality signal may enter the fabric through an unregistered channel. Before activating any observation channel, RT-08 must verify that the observer is registered in the Observer Register, confirm observation authority from RT-02 per O8-9, and confirm the channel scope corresponds to the observer's registered ObservationScope. Signals arriving through channels that fail these preconditions are rejected and do not form Observation Records. This obligation is the operational enforcement of D3 RF-A6 (Observation Boundary as the sole constitutional entry point) and RT08-INV-5 (every channel registered before activation). A channel that has never been registered is not an observation channel in the constitutional sense — it is an unauthorized input. RT-08 shall not treat unauthorized inputs as observation events. (Constitutional source: A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 R2; D3 RF-A6; RT08-INV-5)

**O8-3 — Observation Record Formation:** RT-08 shall form Observation Records with all constitutionally required attributes: provenance, observation source, confidence, temporal position, relationship to internal objects, and uncertainty acknowledgment per D5 PI-10. Every Observation Record must carry the five D5 §3.4 uncertainty attributes: Source (instrument and method permanently recorded), Confidence (epistemic confidence derived from instrument calibration state), Limitations (known limitations of instrument and method), Timestamp (time of external contact and time of Record formation), and Observer Capability (constitutional scope of the observer's registered observation authority). An Observation Record missing any of these attributes is constitutionally incomplete and must not be submitted to RT-03. The Observation Record is the atomic unit of the fabric's contact with external reality — it is the formal result of OPL Stage 3. The Observation Record is immutable after formation (D5 §3.2): subsequent interpretation produces new objects; the Observation Record itself must never be altered. RT-08 ensures Observation Record completeness before closing the ObservationFormationWindow. (Constitutional source: A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 R3; D5 §3.2; D5 §3.4; D4 KI-016)

**O8-4 — Observation Boundary Enforcement:** RT-08 shall enforce the Observation Boundary: no external reality information enters the fabric except through a constitutionally valid Observation Record (RT08-INV-1; D3 RF-A6). This enforcement is absolute and admits no exceptions. No external reality information may enter through direct injection, side channels, or any bypass of the Observation Record formation process. RT-08 is the sole constitutional mechanism through which external reality information crosses the Observation Boundary from External Reality into the Reality Fabric. Any external reality information that does not pass through RT-08's Observation Record formation process is constitutionally prohibited from entering the fabric. This obligation operationalizes D3 RF-A6 ("The Observation Boundary is the only constitutional entry point for information from external reality into the Reality Fabric") and D3 RF-A10 (the fabric's grounding in external reality is maintained exclusively through the Observation Boundary). RT-08 failure to enforce this boundary is the root cause of CIF-6 (D7 §13). (Constitutional source: A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 R4; D3 RF-A6; D3 RF-A10; RT08-INV-1)

**O8-5 — Internal/External Distinction Maintenance:** RT-08 shall maintain the distinction between the Observation Record (the internal constitutional object) and the external referent (the observed external state) — never collapsing them per D5 PI-2 and D-2 §XVIII Forbidden Assumption 1. The Observation Record is a representation; the external state is reality. These must never be treated as equivalent. RT-08 must ensure that Observation Records never assert identity with the observed External State. Observation Records describe observations of external states; they do not assert that the internal record is the external state. Violations of this distinction constitute the fundamental epistemic failure that D-2 §XVIII prohibits: treating a representation as equivalent to reality. This distinction is enforced at the invariant level by RT08-INV-4 (derived from D5 PI-2 and D-2 §XVIII). The distinction protects the entire downstream epistemic chain — if RT-08 collapses this distinction, all subsequent Evidence, Knowledge, and Understanding built on that Observation Record inherits the error. (Constitutional source: A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 R5; D5 PI-2; D-2 §XVIII FA-1; RT08-INV-4)

**O8-6 — Observation Record Routing to RT-03:** RT-08 shall route all completed Observation Records to RT-03 for gate processing as Class A operations. No Observation Record may enter the fabric without passing through RT-03's six gates (D4 §2.2). RT-08 submits Observation Records to RT-03 as Class A immediately upon ObservationFormationWindow close — when OPL Stage 3 is complete and the Observation Record carries all required attributes. RT-08 does not bypass RT-03, does not directly write to RT-05, and does not directly write to RT-07. The routing through RT-03 is mandatory and admits no exception. An Observation Record that has not passed through RT-03 gate processing has not been constitutionally admitted to the fabric and may not be treated as a fabric object. Gate processing is RT-03's constitutional obligation; RT-08's obligation is complete submission — delivering the complete, valid Observation Record to RT-03 as Class A. (Constitutional source: A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 R6; D4 §2.2; A1 PAIR 18)

**O8-7 — Consequence Observation Record Formation:** RT-08 shall form Consequence Observation Records when RT-14 triggers observation of action consequences (A0 §4.4 Step 27; D8 INV-6). When RT-14 sends a ConsequenceObservationTrigger to RT-08, RT-08 applies the full Observation Record formation protocol to the consequence observation context: observer identity per O8-8, observation authority per O8-9, temporal positioning per O8-10, observer limitation acknowledgment per O8-11, and domain attribution per O8-12. Consequence observations follow the same constitutional requirements as primary observations — they are not exempt from any Observation Record formation obligation. ConsequenceObservationRecords are the mechanism through which action consequences re-enter the fabric through the Projection Boundary, closing the Constitutional Loop's feedback structure (PAIR 49; D8 INV-6 Feedback Requirement). Consequence Observation Records must pass through RT-03 gate processing as Class A before entering the fabric. (Constitutional source: A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 R7; A0 §4.4 Step 27; A1 PAIR 49; D8 INV-6)

**O8-8 — Observer Identity Assignment:** RT-08 shall assign observer identity from RT-01 to every Observation Record. No Observation Record may lack a resolvable observer identity (RT08-INV-2; D3 §5.6 Observer Infrastructure R1). Observer identity is obtained through PAIR 10: RT-08 passes external identity claims to RT-01 for evaluation; RT-01 provides ActorProfile validation results. The observer identity embedded in every Observation Record must be a constitutionally valid ActorProfile with ObserverCapability claim. Observation Records with unresolved or missing observer identity are constitutionally incomplete and must not be submitted to RT-03. Observer identity assignment is the constitutional mechanism that makes Observation Records attributable — traceable to a specific registered observer with a specific calibration record, at a specific time, through a specific registered channel. Attribution enables constitutional accountability for all observation claims entering the epistemic chain. (Constitutional source: A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 R8; A1 PAIR 10; D3 §5.6; RT08-INV-2)

**O8-9 — Observation Authority Registration:** RT-08 shall register observation authority from RT-02 before every observation channel activation. Authority registration is obtained through PAIR 14: RT-08 passes external authority claims to RT-02 for evaluation; RT-02 provides AuthorityResolutionResult. A channel may not be activated without a valid AuthorityResolutionResult confirming that the requesting observer holds AIR-1 (Observation Authority) for the domain and scope of the intended observation. RT-08 holds AIR-1 authority itself (D6 §4.2; RS-06), but AIR-1 is domain-specific — each observation channel activation requires authority confirmation for its specific domain and ExternalRealitySegment scope. Cross-domain observations require explicit cross-domain authorization. This obligation implements D6 §4.2's requirement that AIR-1 holders use registered instruments with known calibration states and report to the Domain Coherence registers. (Constitutional source: A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 R9; D6 §4.2; A1 PAIR 14)

**O8-10 — Temporal Positioning:** RT-08 shall maintain temporal positioning of all Observation Records per D3 RF-A4 temporal integrity constraints and D4 Gate 6 requirements. Every Observation Record must carry constitutionally accurate timestamps: the time of external contact (OPL Stage 2 initiation — when instrumental contact with External Reality occurred) and the time of Observation Record formation (OPL Stage 3 completion). These timestamps must be causally consistent with the established causal ordering of the fabric (D4 Gate 6 Condition (a); D4 KI-026 Causal Ordering). RT-08 must not assign timestamps that predate established causal ordering, that are internally inconsistent, or where the formation timestamp precedes the contact timestamp. Observation Record timestamps pass through RT-03 Gate 6 for verification. RT-08 does not perform Gate 6 — that is RT-03's function — but RT-08 must supply accurate, causally consistent timestamps for Gate 6 verification to succeed. (Constitutional source: A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 R10; D4 §4.6; D4 KI-026)

**O8-11 — Observer Limitation Acknowledgment:** RT-08 shall detect and acknowledge observer limitation in every Observation Record per D5 PI-10 (Observer Non-Contamination). Every Observation Record must include an explicit acknowledgment of the observer's known limitations: the instrument's calibration state, known limitations of the observation method, scope limitations (what the observer cannot observe from its registered position and orientation), and any degraded observer capability at the time of observation. If an observer is in a degraded state, the Observation Record's Confidence attribute must reflect the degraded status — a degraded observer must not generate Observations at full confidence (D4 Gate 4 Condition (h); D4 KI-017; RT08-INV-3). The ObserverLimitationRecord is the constitutional mechanism for capturing acknowledged limitations in a separately auditable object. This obligation is RT-08's operational realization of D5 PI-10's prohibition on observer contamination — the requirement that observers not suppress limitation acknowledgment and thereby contaminate downstream epistemic chain objects with false confidence. (Constitutional source: A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 R11; D5 PI-10; D4 KI-017; D4 Gate 4 Condition (h); RT08-INV-3)

**O8-12 — Domain Attribution:** RT-08 shall register domain attribution in every Observation Record: every Observation must be attributed to the domain of the observed ExternalRealitySegment per D5 PI-11 (Civilizational Scope). Domain attribution is non-optional — no Observation Record may lack attribution to the domain of the ExternalRealitySegment being observed (RT08-INV-6). Domain attribution answers the constitutional question: which civilizational domain does this observation concern? Attribution is determined by the ExternalRealitySegment observed, not by the observer's own domain registration. Cross-domain observations — where an observer registered in one domain observes an ExternalRealitySegment in another domain — require explicit cross-domain attribution and must carry evidence of cross-domain observation authority from RT-02 per D6 §4.2. Domain attribution enables downstream runtimes (RT-09, RT-10, RT-11) to route epistemic chain objects to the correct domain knowledge infrastructure and is required for civilizational-scope intelligence synthesis by RT-11. (Constitutional source: A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 R12; D5 PI-11; RT08-INV-6)

### RS-06 — Authority

#### 6.1 Authority Class

RT-08 holds two D6 authority types:

**AIR-1 — Observation Authority (D6 §4.2):** RT-08 holds Observation Authority — "the constitutional right to initiate Observation Projections (D5 Part 3) within a domain and to register their results as constitutionally valid Observation Records in that domain's knowledge base" (D6 §4.2 verbatim). RT-08 holds AIR-1 at civilizational scope: RT-08 is authorized to receive and form Observation Records across all domain ExternalRealitySegments registered in the Observer Register. AIR-1 is domain-specific within its scope — observation authority in one domain does not automatically extend to another; each domain observation channel activation requires registered authorization per O8-9.

Constitutional obligations of RT-08 as AIR-1 holder (D6 §4.2):
- Must use registered instruments with known calibration states
- Must preserve all five D5 §3.4 uncertainty attributes in every Observation Record
- Must not suppress Observations that contradict existing domain beliefs
- Must report to the Domain Coherence registers

Constitutional prohibitions on RT-08's AIR-1 use (D6 §4.2):
- AIR-1 may not be used to manufacture Observations — RT-08 may not create Observations of External States that were not actually observed
- AIR-1 may not be delegated below the registered minimum authority level
- AIR-1 holders may not share raw observation data across the Projection Boundary without D5 lifecycle compliance

**AIR-4 — Projection Authority (D6 §4.5) — Inbound Direction Only:** RT-08 holds Projection Authority for the inbound crossing of the Projection Boundary — the constitutional right to receive External Reality signals across the Projection Boundary and transform them through OPL Stages 1–3. For RT-08, AIR-4 applies exclusively to the inbound direction. RT-08 is one of exactly three runtimes authorized to interact with the Projection Boundary (R0 Part 3): RT-08 (inbound Observation Projection), RT-13 (outbound Action Projection), RT-14 (consequence capture). RT-08 does not hold outbound Projection Authority.

Constitutional obligations of RT-08 as AIR-4 holder for inbound crossing (D6 §4.5):
- All D5 OPL Stages 1–3 must complete before the Observation Record is submitted to RT-03
- Consequence Observations (triggered by RT-14) must be captured as ConsequenceObservationRecords
- All inbound projection channels registered in the Observer Register

**D6 §4.7 Authority Integrity Rule AIR-3 (No Knowledge Monopolization — Observation/Interpretation Separation):** "Observation and Interpretation Authority in any domain must be held by at least two registered actors." RT-08 (authority type AIR-1 — Observation) and RT-09 (authority type AIR-2 — Interpretation) are architecturally separated. RT-08 observes; RT-09 interprets. This separation is constitutionally mandatory. RT-08 may not hold authority type AIR-2 (Interpretation Authority); the combination would collapse the constitutionally required separation of observation and interpretation.

**Authority Separation Principle (D6 §4.7):** "Observation, Interpretation, Decision, Projection, and Audit Authority must not be collapsed into a single actor." ## PART 5 — LIFECYCLE AND STATE

### RS-14 — Lifecycle

#### 14.1 Initialization Phase

1. Load Observer Register from stable storage: verify all registered observers have valid constitutional parameters (D3 §5.6 requirements R1–R5 for each observer)
2. Load Channel Registry from stable storage: verify all registered channels have Active or valid Suspended authorization status; load ObservationChannelRecords
3. Validate RT-01 identity service availability: confirm ActorProfile service is operational
4. Validate RT-02 authority service availability: confirm AuthorityResolutionResult service is operational
5. Validate RT-03 submission service availability: confirm Class A submission service is operational
6. Confirm no ObservationFormationWindows were left Open at prior shutdown; if any incomplete observation contexts found from prior session, log as RT08-FAIL-05 and clear context — do not attempt to resume incomplete observations
7. Initialize Consequence Observation Queue as empty
8. Transition to ACTIVE state

#### 14.2 Active Operation

Once ACTIVE, RT-08 operates three concurrent services:
- **Signal Reception Service** (RS-12 §12.1–12.3): Continuously monitors registered observation channels for external signals; processes each signal through OPL Stages 1–3; event-driven
- **Consequence Observation Service** (RS-12 §12.4): Monitors Consequence Observation Queue; processes ConsequenceObservationTriggers from RT-14 through the full OPL cycle; event-driven
- **Observer Register Maintenance Service** (RS-12 §12.5): Processes observer registration requests, calibration updates, authority renewals, and lapse detections; ongoing

All three services must be simultaneously available in ACTIVE state. Failure of any service without recovery is RT08-FAIL-07.

#### 14.3 Consequence Observation Processing

When ConsequenceObservationTriggers are received from RT-14, RT-08 processes them per RS-12 §12.4 without interrupting the Signal Reception or Observer Register Maintenance services. Multiple triggers may be queued in the Consequence Observation Queue; they are processed in constitutional arrival order.

#### 14.4 Recovery

On restart after failure:
1. Re-enter Initialization Phase
2. Load Observer Register and Channel Registry from stable storage
3. Validate all observers per D3 §5.6 requirements
4. Log any incomplete Observation Contexts from prior session as RT08-FAIL-05 events (for RT-04 audit); do not attempt to resume incomplete observations
5. Validate RT-01, RT-02, RT-03 availability
6. If RT-01 or RT-02 unavailable: enter RT08-FAIL-07; do not transition to ACTIVE; wait for dependency restoration
7. Once all validations complete: transition to ACTIVE state

#### 14.5 Termination

RT-08 does not self-terminate. Shutdown is constitutional only, initiated through the founding termination protocol. Prior to shutdown:
1. Complete all in-progress Observation Record formations (close all Open ObservationFormationWindows) or record exact observation context for recovery log
2. Process all queued ConsequenceObservationTriggers or document their status in the recovery log
3. Write all Observer Register and Channel Registry state to stable storage
4. Confirm RT-04 audit access is preserved to all RT-08 constitutional objects
5. Record shutdown event for RT-04 audit

### RS-20 — Invariants

The following invariants are derived from A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 (RT08-INV-1 through RT08-INV-6) and constitutional D-series sources:

**RT08-INV-1 — Observation Boundary Sole Entry (A0-v1.1.1 §3.9; D3 RF-A6)**
No information from external reality enters the fabric except through a constitutionally valid Observation Record formed by RT-08. This invariant cannot be waived. Any pathway for external reality information to enter the fabric that bypasses ObservationRecord formation by RT-08 constitutes a constitutional violation. RT-08 is the sole constitutional mechanism for the Reality-to-Fabric transition.

**RT08-INV-2 — Observer Identity Required (A0-v1.1.1 §3.9; D3 §5.6 Observer Infrastructure R1)**
Every Observation Record has a resolvable observer identity — a constitutionally valid ActorProfile from RT-01 with ObserverCapability claim. No Observation Record may be submitted to RT-03 without embedded observer identity. An Observation Record without resolvable observer identity is constitutionally invalid regardless of the completeness of its other attributes.

**RT08-INV-3 — Observer Limitation Acknowledgment Required (A0-v1.1.1 §3.9; D5 PI-10; D4 KI-017)**
Every Observation Record explicitly acknowledges observer limitations through: (a) the Limitations attribute in the five D5 §3.4 uncertainty attributes, (b) the accompanying ObserverLimitationRecord, and (c) a Confidence attribute that reflects actual observer calibration state — not exceeding the calibration ceiling. A degraded observer must not generate Observations at full confidence (D4 Gate 4 Condition (h); D4 KI-017). This invariant is not waivable.

**RT08-INV-4 — Internal/External Distinction (A0-v1.1.1 §3.9; D5 PI-2; D-2 §XVIII FA-1)**
The internal Observation Record and the external referent (the observed External State) are never conflated. Observation Records describe observations; they are never asserted to be identical to the External State they describe. This invariant protects the entire downstream epistemic chain from the fundamental epistemic failure of representation/reality conflation. No Observation Record may assert identity with its referent.

**RT08-INV-5 — Channel Registration Required (A0-v1.1.1 §3.9; D3 §5.6 Observer Infrastructure R2)**
Every observation channel is registered in the Observer Register and Channel Registry before activation. No external reality signal received through an unregistered channel may produce an Observation Record. Signals arriving through unregistered channels are rejected and do not enter the ObservationFormationWindow. This invariant is the operational enforcement of D3 §5.6 Observer Infrastructure requirement R2.

**RT08-INV-6 — Domain Attribution Non-Optional (A0-v1.1.1 §3.9; D5 PI-11)**
Domain attribution is non-optional: no Observation Record may lack attribution to the domain of the observed ExternalRealitySegment. Every Observation Record carries a domain attribution identifying which civilizational domain the observation concerns. Absence of domain attribution is a constitutional incompleteness that prevents Gate 4 passage.

**D5 PI-2 — Representation Non-Identity**
The Observation Record is not identical to the External State it represents. RT-08 enforces D5 PI-2 through RT08-INV-4. All Observation Records formed by RT-08 must be marked as representations of observed external states, not as identical to those states.

**D5 PI-10 — Observer Non-Contamination**
Observer limitations must be acknowledged; observations must not be contaminated by suppression of limitation acknowledgment. RT-08 enforces D5 PI-10 through RT08-INV-3 and O8-11. Observer calibration state is embedded in every Observation Record's Confidence and Limitations attributes.

**D5 PI-11 — Civilizational Scope (Domain Attribution)**
Every Observation must be attributed to the domain of the observed ExternalRealitySegment. RT-08 enforces D5 PI-11 through RT08-INV-6 and O8-12.

**D4 KI-007 — Epistemic Chain Stage Sequence**
The epistemic chain must follow the constitutionally specified stage sequence; no stage may be skipped. RT-08 Observation Records must open the chain at Stage 1 (OPL Stage 1: External State) through Stage 3 (Observation Record formation). RT-08 may not skip to Stage 4 (Evidence Assessment) — that transition belongs to RT-09.

**D4 KI-010 — Epistemic Chain Traceability**
Every KnowledgeClaim must be traceable to at least one Observation through an unbroken epistemic chain. RT-08 Observation Records are the constitutional roots of all epistemic validity. RT-08 must ensure every Observation Record is a complete, valid epistemic chain root — carrying all required attributes for Gate 4 passage and RT-09 Evidence formation.

**D4 KI-016 — Prior Chain Objects Required**
Every epistemic stage transition must have required prior chain objects present and accessible. RT-08 must ensure Observation Records carry all required attributes before submission to RT-03 — prior chain objects (observer identity, authority, calibration state) must be present and valid.

**D4 KI-017 — UncertaintyDeclaration Required**
Every Observation must include an UncertaintyDeclaration; degraded Observer must not generate Observations at full confidence. RT-08 enforces KI-017 through RT08-INV-3 and O8-11. Observer calibration state determines the Confidence ceiling.

**D4 KI-023 — ObservationFormationWindow**

RT-08 opens the ObservationFormationWindow at OPL Stage 2 and closes it at OPL Stage 3 completion. ## PART 7 — COMPLIANCE AND AUDIT

### RS-23 — Audit Requirements

**RT-04 access:** RT-04 holds unrestricted AIR-5 observation access to all RT-08 owned objects and operations at all times (PAIR 22; R0 ADR-3; D6 §4.7 (AIR-5)). This access is non-delegable and may not be restricted by RT-08 under any circumstances.

**Audit-addressable objects:** Every ObservationRecord, ConsequenceObservationRecord, ObserverLimitationRecord, ObservationChannelRecord, and ObserverRegister entry created or maintained by RT-08 must be audit-addressable by RT-04. "Audit-addressable" means: identifiable by stable reference; readable in full; not modifiable after formation (for ObservationRecords and ConsequenceObservationRecords) or after creation (for other objects).

**Observation operation records:** Every Observation Record formation attempt must produce an audit-addressable record: the external signal received, the observer and channel involved, the OPL stage reached, the outcome (Observation Record formed and submitted to RT-03, or RT08-FAIL-NN event), and the timestamp. RT-04 must be able to reconstruct the complete observation history of RT-08 from these records.

**Dependency interaction records:** Every RT-01 identity resolution, RT-02 authority resolution, and RT-03 submission by RT-08 must be audit-addressable with the request sent, the response received, and the timestamp.

**Failure and recovery records:** All RT08-FAIL-NN events and their recovery actions are permanently accessible to RT-04.

**Observer Register change records:** Every Observer Register and Channel Registry update must be audit-addressable: what changed, the authorizing source, and the timestamp.

**Retention:** RT-08 does not define retention periods — those are founding matters. RT-08 obligations: (a) never modify ObservationRecords after formation; (b) maintain audit access for all records in the retention window established at founding.

### RS-24 — Validation Criteria

**ObservationRecord Validity:**
- Observer identity resolvable (RT-01 ActorProfile with ObserverCapability) — RT08-INV-2
- All five D5 §3.4 uncertainty attributes present: Source, Confidence, Limitations, Timestamp, Observer Capability
- Domain attribution present for the observed ExternalRealitySegment — RT08-INV-6
- Internal/external distinction marker present — RT08-INV-4 (representation, not identity)
- ObservationFormationWindow was opened (OPL Stage 2) before Record formation
- ObservationFormationWindow closed concurrent with Record formation (OPL Stage 3)
- Confidence attribute does not exceed observer calibration ceiling — RT08-INV-3; D4 KI-017
- Timestamps are causally consistent: contact timestamp precedes formation timestamp; both are within the current civilizational era — O8-10; D4 KI-026
- Record is immutable after formation — D5 §3.2

**ObservationChannelRecord Validity:**
- References a registered observer with Active status in ObserverRegister
- Contains current AuthorityResolutionResult from RT-02 (or reference to current result)
- ExternalRealitySegment scope corresponds to observer's registered ObservationScope
