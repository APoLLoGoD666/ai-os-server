### CH-005 — RS-06 §6.1 AIR Integrity Rule Heading (FIND-001)

**Location:** RS-06 §6.1 Authority Class, AIR integrity rule heading paragraph
**Finding reference:** FIND-001
**Previous text:**
```
**AIR-1 Authority Integrity Rule (D6 §4.7 AIR-3):** "Observation and Interpretation Authority in any domain must be held by at least two registered actors." RT-08 (AIR-1 — Observation) and RT-09 (AIR-2 — Interpretation) are architecturally separated. RT-08 observes; RT-09 interprets. This separation is constitutionally mandatory. RT-08 may not hold AIR-2 (Interpretation Authority); the combination would collapse the constitutionally required separation of observation and interpretation.
```
**Replacement text:**
```
**D6 §4.7 Authority Integrity Rule AIR-3 (No Knowledge Monopolization — Observation/Interpretation Separation):** "Observation and Interpretation Authority in any domain must be held by at least two registered actors." RT-08 (authority type AIR-1 — Observation) and RT-09 (authority type AIR-2 — Interpretation) are architecturally separated. RT-08 observes; RT-09 interprets. This separation is constitutionally mandatory. RT-08 may not hold authority type AIR-2 (Interpretation Authority); the combination would collapse the constitutionally required separation of observation and interpretation.
```
**Reason:** The previous heading "AIR-1 Authority Integrity Rule (D6 §4.7 AIR-3)" conflated two independent "AIR-N" numbering systems: R0 §3.5 defines authority types (AIR-1 = Observation Authority, AIR-2 = Interpretation Authority, AIR-3 = Decision Authority, AIR-4 = Projection Authority, AIR-5 = Audit Authority) while D6 §4.7 defines Authority Integrity Rules also numbered AIR-1 through AIR-5 (AIR-1 = Authority Separation, AIR-2 = No Unauthorized Projection, AIR-3 = No Knowledge Monopolization, AIR-4 = No Hidden Decisions, AIR-5 = Audit Independence). The heading "AIR-1 Authority Integrity Rule" implied D6 §4.7 AIR-1 (Authority Separation) while the parenthetical "(D6 §4.7 AIR-3)" correctly identified the rule as D6 §4.7 AIR-3 (No Knowledge Monopolization). Within the body text, "AIR-1" and "AIR-2" now use the qualifier "authority type" to disambiguate them from D6 §4.7 AIR-N rule numbering.
**Constitutional basis:** D6 §4.7 (AIR-3 = No Knowledge Monopolization rule); R0 §3.5 (AIR-1 through AIR-5 = authority type designations); FIND-001 remediation directive

**Location:** RS-29 Constitutional Loop Participation, Loop 2 — Consequence Observation Loop, RT-08 position and Constitutional basis lines
**Finding reference:** FIND-002
**Previous text:**

**Loop type:** Feedback loop — triggered by RT-13 consequence observation pathway via RT-14
