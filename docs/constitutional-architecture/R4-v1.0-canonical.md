## RS-02: Constitutional Basis

**Section Title (R0 §4.1):** Constitutional Basis

**Identifier:** RS-02

**Why Constitutional Audit Is Required**

No runtime within the APEX constitutional fabric can reliably verify its own constitutional compliance. Self-assessment by operational runtimes produces circular authority: the entity being evaluated applies the evaluation criteria and reports the result without independent verification. Three constitutional failures arise from audit absence:

**P-AUD-1 — Self-Assessment Circularity:** RT-03 can detect gate violations at the moment of operation but cannot verify that its own gate evaluation logic is constitutionally correct. RT-02 can record authority grants but cannot verify its own AIR compliance. Constitutional compliance cannot be self-certified by any operational runtime.

**P-AUD-2 — Accumulation Without Detection:** Constitutional violations that do not trigger immediate failure modes accumulate over time. Without continuous audit, the gap between actual constitutional state and required constitutional state widens without correction signals.

**P-AUD-3 — Constitutional Feedback Necessity:** D7 requires intelligence systems to operate within constitutional bounds. The constitutional feedback mechanism that keeps intelligence operations within those bounds is RT-04. Without audit, the constitutional loop lacks its verification and correction phase.

## RS-03: Purpose

**Section Title (R0 §4.1):** Purpose

**Identifier:** RS-03

**Primary Purpose**

RT-04 exists to maintain continuous visibility into the constitutional compliance of APEX and to detect constitutional deviation before it progresses to constitutional failure.

**Four Constitutional Audit Guarantees**

| Guarantee | Description |
|---|---|
| AUD-GUAR-1: Compliance Visibility | At all times, constitutional authorities have access to a current compliance assessment of all runtimes and constitutional objects; no constitutional domain is opaque |
| AUD-GUAR-2: Deviation Detection | Constitutional deviations are detected and recorded before they accumulate into constitutional failure; RT-04 provides early warning of constitutional drift |
| AUD-GUAR-3: Evidence-Grounded Findings | All audit findings are traceable to specific constitutional evidence; no finding is asserted without supporting evidence in the constitutional fabric |
| AUD-GUAR-4: Independent Verification | Constitutional compliance assessments are produced by an entity (RT-04) that is structurally independent from the entities being assessed; self-assessment is constitutionally excluded |

**Constitutional Self-Observation**

RT-04 is the constitutional self-observation capability of APEX. Just as D-1 establishes that epistemic claims must be grounded, RT-04 ensures that constitutional compliance claims are grounded in evidence. RT-04 transforms the constitutional fabric from an assertion-based system into a verification-based system.

**AIR-5 Structural Independence**

RT-04 Audit Authority is constitutionally separate from all operational and enforcement authority. This separation is not a procedural preference; it is a structural constitutional requirement established by D6 AIR-5. An audit function that derives its authority from, reports to, or is scoped by an enforcement function cannot be constitutionally independent. RT-04's authority derives directly from A0 §3.6 and D6, not from RT-03 (Enforcement) or RT-02 (Authority Management).

## RS-12: Internal Processes

**Section Title (R0 §4.1):** Internal Processes

**Identifier:** RS-12

**Naming Convention:** RT04-PROC-NN

---

### RT04-PROC-01: Audit Initiation

**Trigger:** Audit cycle initiation signal, KernelFailureLog entry (immediate audit trigger), ConstitutionalDeviationSignal, or scheduled continuous audit cycle.

**Constitutional steps:**
1. Consult AuditManifest Registry (RT04-STATE-10) to determine audit scope for this cycle
2. Verify Constitutional Requirements Corpus (RT04-STATE-07) is current; update if constitutional documents have been amended
3. Create audit cycle entry in AuditCycleLog (RT04-STATE-08)
4. Populate Active Audit Queue (RT04-STATE-01) with audit targets for this cycle
5. Confirm RT03-INV-6 channel is active for RT-03 record access
6. Begin RT04-PROC-02 (Evidence Collection) for first queued target

**Output:** Audit cycle initiated; Active Audit Queue populated

---

### RT04-PROC-02: Evidence Collection

**Trigger:** Active Audit Queue entry dequeued for processing.

**Constitutional steps:**
1. Access all relevant constitutional records for the audit target: KernelOperationLog (RT04-IN-01), ProvenanceRecord (RT04-IN-02), RejectionRecord (RT04-IN-03), AuthorityRecord (RT04-IN-04), IdentityRecord (RT04-IN-05) as applicable to the target
2. Access KernelFailureLog (RT04-IN-06) for any failures involving the target
3. Access RuntimeStateReport (RT04-IN-08) for the target's current lifecycle state
4. Record all collected evidence artifacts in Evidence Repository (RT04-STATE-03) with provenance references
5. Verify evidence completeness: if evidence is insufficient, record gap and initiate RT04-FAIL-02 condition
6. Proceed to RT04-PROC-03 (Constitutional Requirement Comparison)

**Constitutional invariant:** RT04-INV-03 — no evidence artifact is modified during collection; all access is read-only.

**Output:** Evidence artifacts in Evidence Repository; RT04-STATE-03 updated

---

### RT04-PROC-03: Constitutional Requirement Comparison

**Trigger:** Evidence collection complete for audit target.

**Constitutional steps:**
1. Identify the applicable constitutional requirements for the audit target from Constitutional Requirements Corpus (RT04-STATE-07)
2. Map each collected evidence artifact to its corresponding constitutional requirement
3. For each requirement: determine whether collected evidence demonstrates compliance, non-compliance, or indeterminate status
4. Record comparison result for each requirement in the audit working record
5. If any requirement yields indeterminate status due to evidence gap, trigger RT04-FAIL-02
6. Proceed to RT04-PROC-04 (Compliance Assessment)

**Output:** Per-requirement comparison results; audit working record updated

---

### RT04-PROC-04: Compliance Assessment

**Trigger:** Constitutional requirement comparison complete.

**Constitutional steps:**
1. Aggregate per-requirement comparison results into overall compliance determination: COMPLIANT (all requirements met), NON-COMPLIANT (one or more requirements failed), or CONDITIONALLY-COMPLIANT (minor deficiencies, no systemic failure)
2. Create ComplianceAssessment (RT04-OWN-02) recording the determination with evidence basis
3. Update Compliance Status Map (RT04-STATE-09) for the audit target
4. If NON-COMPLIANT or CONDITIONALLY-COMPLIANT: proceed to RT04-PROC-05 (Finding Generation)
5. If COMPLIANT: proceed directly to RT04-PROC-07 (Certification Determination)
6. Form AuditRecord (RT04-OWN-01) for this audit operation

**Constitutional invariant:** RT04-INV-04 — compliance determination must trace to specific evidence; no determination without evidence.

**Output:** ComplianceAssessment; AuditRecord; Compliance Status Map updated

---

### RT04-PROC-05: Finding Generation

**Trigger:** Non-compliant or conditionally compliant determination from RT04-PROC-04.

**Constitutional steps:**
1. For each failed or partially satisfied constitutional requirement: create a DeficiencyFinding (RT04-OWN-03)
2. Each DeficiencyFinding must specify: constitutional requirement violated, evidence of violation, severity (MINOR/MODERATE/HIGH/CRITICAL), and affected runtime or domain
3. If the deficiency indicates a systemic or acute constitutional deviation: initiate RT04-PROC-08 (Deviation Reporting) in parallel
4. Proceed to RT04-PROC-06 (Deficiency Recording)

**Output:** DeficiencyFindings created

---

### RT04-PROC-06: Deficiency Recording

**Trigger:** DeficiencyFindings created by RT04-PROC-05.

**Constitutional steps:**
1. Record each DeficiencyFinding in the DeficiencyRegister (RT04-OWN-04) with status OPEN
2. Issue RT04-OUT-03 (DeficiencyFinding) to the affected runtime's constitutional authority
3. Update Open Deficiency Register (RT04-STATE-02) and DeficiencyRegisterUpdate (RT04-OUT-10)
4. Proceed to RT04-PROC-07 (Certification Determination)

**Constitutional invariant:** RT04-INV-06 — DeficiencyFindings are immutable records; once created they cannot be deleted, only resolved with evidence.

**Output:** DeficiencyRegister updated; constitutional authority notified

---

### RT04-PROC-07: Certification Determination

**Trigger:** Compliance Assessment complete (COMPLIANT path) or Deficiency Recording complete (NON-COMPLIANT path).

**Constitutional steps:**
1. If COMPLIANT and no open DeficiencyFindings: issue CertificationOutput (RT04-OWN-05) with determination PASS
2. If CONDITIONALLY-COMPLIANT with only MINOR or MODERATE open findings: issue CertificationOutput with determination CONDITIONAL-PASS and list open findings
3. If NON-COMPLIANT or any HIGH/CRITICAL DeficiencyFindings open: issue CertificationOutput with determination FAIL and enumerate all open findings
4. Record CertificationOutput in Certification Registry (RT04-STATE-04)
5. Issue RT04-OUT-04 (CertificationOutput) to constitutional authorities
6. Proceed to RT04-PROC-10 (Audit Cycle Completion) unless escalation is required

**Constitutional invariant:** RT04-INV-04 — CertificationOutput must trace entirely to evidence; RT04-PROH-08 prohibits unsupported certification.

**Output:** CertificationOutput; Certification Registry updated

---

### RT04-PROC-08: Deviation Reporting

**Trigger:** RT04-PROC-05 detects systemic or acute constitutional deviation (HIGH or CRITICAL DeficiencyFinding, or multiple concurrent violations indicating systemic failure).

**Constitutional steps:**
1. Create ConstitutionalDeviationReport (RT04-OWN-08) describing the deviation, affected constitutional provisions, evidence chain, and severity
2. Issue RT04-OUT-06 (ConstitutionalDeviationReport) as broadcast to all constitutional authorities
3. Record in DeficiencyRegister with status ESCALATED
4. Proceed to RT04-PROC-09 (Escalation Processing) for CRITICAL deviations
5. Continue normal audit cycle processing in parallel

**Output:** ConstitutionalDeviationReport; broadcast issued

---

### RT04-PROC-09: Escalation Processing

**Trigger:** Unresolved DeficiencyFinding exceeding the constitutional resolution window, or CRITICAL ConstitutionalDeviationReport.

**Constitutional steps:**
1. Create EscalationRecord (RT04-OWN-06) referencing the DeficiencyFinding or ConstitutionalDeviationReport
2. Identify the constitutional authority responsible for the escalation target
3. Issue RT04-OUT-05 (EscalationRecord) to the designated constitutional authority
4. Update Escalation Register (RT04-STATE-05) with OPEN status
5. Update DeficiencyRegister entry to status ESCALATED
6. Monitor for constitutional authority response within the escalation response window
7. If response received and deficiency resolved: update DeficiencyRegister to RESOLVED, Escalation Register to CLOSED
8. If response not received within response window: issue second escalation at higher constitutional authority level

**Output:** EscalationRecord; Escalation Register updated; constitutional authority notified

---

### RT04-PROC-10: Audit Cycle Completion

**Trigger:** All audit targets in the Active Audit Queue for the current cycle have been assessed (RT04-PROC-04 through RT04-PROC-07 complete for all targets).

**Constitutional steps:**
1. Update AuditCoverageMap (RT04-STATE-06) to reflect coverage achieved in this cycle
2. Verify coverage against AuditManifest (RT04-STATE-10): if any AuditManifest target was not audited this cycle, record as RT04-FAIL-09 (Coverage Gap)
3. Issue AuditCycleReport (RT04-OUT-07) summarizing the cycle: targets audited, compliance results, open findings, new escalations, certifications issued
4. Issue ComplianceStatusBroadcast (RT04-OUT-08) to all constitutional authorities
5. Issue AuditCoverageReport (RT04-OUT-09) showing current coverage state
6. Close the current audit cycle entry in AuditCycleLog (RT04-STATE-08)
7. Clear the Active Audit Queue (RT04-STATE-01) for the next cycle
8. Transition to Idle state, ready for next audit cycle initiation (RT04-PROC-01)

**Output:** AuditCycleReport; ComplianceStatusBroadcast; AuditCoverageReport; AuditCycleLog updated

## RS-29: Constitutional Loop Participation

**Section Title (R0 §4.1):** Constitutional Loop Participation

**Identifier:** RS-29

**Description:** RT-04's position and role within the constitutional processing loop — the cyclic sequence through which APEX maintains constitutional compliance over time.

---

### Constitutional Loop Position

RT-04 occupies the Audit phase of the constitutional loop, immediately following the Enforcement phase (RT-03):

```
Identity (RT-01)
      ↓
Authority (RT-02)
      ↓
Enforcement (RT-03)
      ↓
Audit (RT-04)          ← RT-04 constitutional loop position
      ↓
Reality Fabric (RT-05)
      ↓
Observation (RT-08)
      ↓
Knowledge (RT-09)
      ↓
Intelligence (D7)
      ↓
[feedback to constitutional authorities]
```

**Why Audit Follows Enforcement**

RT-04 evaluates the constitutional compliance of enforcement operations. It can only audit records that already exist. Enforcement (RT-03) generates the gate evaluation records, provenance records, and KernelOperationLog entries that constitute RT-04's primary evidence. Audit must follow enforcement because:

1. There is nothing to audit before enforcement has processed operations
2. The sequence preserves D6 AIR-5: RT-04 evaluates RT-03 outputs, not RT-03 inputs, so RT-04 cannot interfere with enforcement decision-making
3. Audit in the enforcement feedback loop provides systemic compliance signals before the next operational phase (Reality Fabric, Observation, Knowledge) processes further operations on the basis of potentially non-compliant enforcement decisions

---
