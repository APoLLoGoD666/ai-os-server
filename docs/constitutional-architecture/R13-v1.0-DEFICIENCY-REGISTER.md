### FAA-13-001

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Deficiency ID | FAA-13-001 |
| Class | IV — Editorial / Traceability |
| Location | RS-20 (Invariants), RT13-INV-5 verification table; secondarily RS-05 R9 |
| Audit Areas | CERT-08 (Translation); V-3 (PI Citation Accuracy) |
| Verdict Impact | DOES NOT BLOCK |

**Finding:**

RT13-INV-5 in A0 §3.14 cites "D5 PI-6" as its constitutional basis:

> "RT13-INV-5: Consequence signals are always generated after Projection Boundary crossing — RT-08 is always notified to enable RT-14 consequence observation (D5 PI-6)"

Similarly, A0 §3.14 Responsibility 9 cites "D5 PI-6: consequence recording is mandatory."

The specification reproduces these citations verbatim in RS-20 and RS-05 R9, as constitutionally required.

**Constitutional standard:**

Per D5-v1.0-canonical.md Part 6 (independently verified):

- **D5 PI-5 — Consequence Recording:** "External effects must generate historical records. Every ExternalState change produced by an Action Projection must be recorded as an Observed Consequence and integrated into the Reality Fabric through Stage 7 of the Action Projection Lifecycle."

- **D5 PI-6 — Boundary Integrity:** "Internal authority cannot automatically become external authority. Constitutional authorization within the Reality Fabric does not constitute authorization in External Reality."

The content cited in RT13-INV-5 (consequence signals must be generated; consequence recording is mandatory) corresponds semantically to D5 PI-5 (Consequence Recording), not D5 PI-6 (Boundary Integrity).

**Constitutional source of discrepancy:** A0-v1.1.1-canonical.md §3.14. The citation error originates in A0, not in R13-v1.0-canonical.md.

**Specification conduct:**

The specification handles this correctly:
1. RS-05 R9 reproduces the A0 text verbatim (including the "D5 PI-6" citation), as CERT-08 verbatim requirements demand.
2. RS-20 reproduces RT13-INV-5 verbatim from A0 (including the "D5 PI-6" citation).
3. The RS-20 verification table explicitly discloses the discrepancy: "PI-6 (Boundary Integrity) in D5 governs Boundary Integrity... the specific consequence recording obligation is addressed in D5 PI-5 (Consequence Recording)... RT13-INV-5 is reproduced verbatim from A0 §3.14 with the citation as A0 states it."

The specification cannot and should not correct an upstream A0 error through its own text — verbatim reproduction of A0 §3.14 is constitutionally required. Any correction would require an A0 amendment.

**Nature of deficiency:**

This is a traceability imprecision in the upstream constitutional source (A0 §3.14). The specification's handling is constitutionally correct. The Class IV observation is recorded here solely for constitutional record completeness and to flag the upstream error for future A0 amendment consideration.

**Minimum surgical change required:**

None in R13-v1.0-canonical.md. If remediation is desired at the source, it requires an A0 amendment correcting §3.14 RT13-INV-5's citation from "D5 PI-6" to "D5 PI-5" and Responsibility 9's citation from "D5 PI-6" to "D5 PI-5." Such an A0 amendment would automatically propagate to R13's next version.

**Verdict impact:** DOES NOT BLOCK. This deficiency does not affect certification status.
