## FAA-10R-001

**Finding ID:** FAA-10R-001
**Class:** IV (Editorial / Traceability Defect)
**Certification Impact:** Advisory — does not block certification

### Section

RS-02 (Constitutional Basis)

### Location

Two distinct errors, both in RS-02:
1. RS-02 §2.1 Primary Constitutional Authority table — D-2 row citation field
2. RS-02 correction note (FAA-10-003 Citation Correction Note) — one explanatory sentence

### Constitutional Basis

D-2-v1.2-canonical.md (Section Index — authoritative section titles)

### Description

**Sub-finding A — Residual §XI citation in RS-02 §2.1 table:**

The RS-02 §2.1 Primary Constitutional Authority table contains the following row:
> `| D-2-v1.2-canonical.md | §XI (Philosophy of Intelligence) | Intelligence is instrumental, fallible, context-dependent, and interpretable... |`

The section number "§XI" is the uncorrected pre-remediation citation. D-2 §VII is "Philosophy of Intelligence" (independently verified from D-2-v1.2 section index); D-2 §XI is "Philosophy of Agency" (verified). The correct entry should read "§VII (Philosophy of Intelligence)." The CHANGELOG states "Zero occurrences of 'D-2 §XI' remain in R10-v1.1-canonical.md" — this claim is factually incorrect, as this one occurrence remains. FAA-10-003 required replacement of all "D-2 §XI" occurrences with "D-2 §VII"; this specific occurrence in the RS-02 §2.1 table was missed.

**Sub-finding B — Inverted explanation in RS-02 correction note:**

The RS-02 correction note (FAA-10-003 Citation Correction Note) states:
> "D-2 §VII is 'Philosophy of Agency' — a different section with different content."

This statement is factually inverted. D-2 §VII is "Philosophy of Intelligence" (not Philosophy of Agency). D-2 §XI is "Philosophy of Agency." The correction note has transposed the two section titles while attempting to explain the correction. The intended meaning (that D-2 §XI is a different section than the one containing the intelligence principles) is inferrable from context, but the literal text as written contains a false statement.

### Evidence

**R10-v1.1 RS-02 §2.1 table (as written):**
> `§XI (Philosophy of Intelligence)`

**D-2-v1.2 Section Index (verified):**
> Section VII — Philosophy of Intelligence
> Section XI — Philosophy of Agency

**R10-v1.1 RS-02 correction note (as written):**
> "D-2 §VII is 'Philosophy of Agency'"

**D-2-v1.2 §VII (verified):** Contains the four intelligence principles (instrumental, fallible, context-dependent, interpretable) — it is Philosophy of Intelligence, not Philosophy of Agency.

**Impact:** All other D-2 citations throughout R10-v1.1 (in RS-04, RS-05, RS-07, RS-10, RS-12, RS-15, RS-18, RS-19, RS-20, RS-22, RS-24, RS-25, RS-26, RS-27, RS-34, RS-35, RS-36) correctly use "D-2 §VII." The intelligence principles themselves are correctly characterized throughout. This error is isolated to the RS-02 correction note machinery and does not propagate into any substantive obligation, invariant, or constitutional claim.

### Remediation

In next revision:
1. RS-02 §2.1 table: change "§XI (Philosophy of Intelligence)" to "§VII (Philosophy of Intelligence)"
2. RS-02 correction note: correct the inverted sentence to read "D-2 §XI is 'Philosophy of Agency' — a different section with different content"

### Certification Impact Detail

The four intelligence principles (instrumental, fallible, context-dependent, interpretable) are substantively correct and correctly cited as "D-2 §VII" throughout all operational sections. Only the RS-02 correction note metadata contains the residual error. No constitutional claim is misstated as a result of this error. Classified Class IV.

## FAA-10R-002

**Finding ID:** FAA-10R-002
**Class:** IV (Editorial / Traceability Defect)
**Certification Impact:** Advisory — does not block certification

### Section

RS-29 (Constitutional Loop Participation), RS-31 (Phase Ownership)

### Location

RS-29.2 (Phase Support — SUPPORTING) Decision row; RS-31 Phase Ownership table Decision row

### Constitutional Basis

A1-v1.2 §15.2 (Runtime-to-Loop-Phase Mapping canonical table)

### Description

R10-v1.1 characterizes RT-10 as "SUPPORTING" for the Decision phase in RS-29.2 and RS-31:

**RS-29.2 Decision row:**
> "Decision | Valid CUM state at point of Decision; CUM invalidation notification to RT-11 | RT-11 | A1 §15.2; A1 PAIR 32 P6, P8"

**RS-31 Phase Ownership table:**
> "| Decision | SUPPORTING | See above |"

Independent verification of A1-v1.2 §15.2 shows:
- Decision phase: Primary Runtime = RT-11; Supporting Runtimes = RT-12, RT-03

RT-10 is not listed in the A1 §15.2 Decision row at all — neither as Primary nor as Supporting. The A1 §15.2 "Updated Understanding" row lists RT-09 → RT-10 → RT-11 as primary, and RT-15 as supporting. RT-10's Decision-phase activities are real and constitutionally grounded in PAIR 32 P6 (CUM state must be valid and non-stale at point of Decision) and P8 (RT-10 notifies RT-11 of CUM invalidation), but these obligations do not translate to "Supporting" status in the A1 §15.2 strict table.

This is a characterization that slightly exceeds what A1 §15.2 strictly assigns. RT-10's Decision-phase participation is better characterized as a passive obligation (maintain CUM validity; notify RT-11 of changes) rather than an active Supporting role per the A1 §15.2 framework.

### Evidence

**A1 §15.2 Decision row (verified):**
> "| Decision | RT-11 | RT-12, RT-03 |"

**R10-v1.1 RS-29.2 (as written):**
> "| Decision | Valid CUM state at point of Decision; CUM invalidation notification to RT-11 | RT-11 | A1 §15.2; A1 PAIR 32 P6, P8 |"

**R10-v1.1 RS-31 (as written):**
> "| Decision | SUPPORTING | See above |"

**PAIR 32 P6 (verified in A1):** "RT-11 must complete deliberation within constitutionally bounded window; Decisions formed after CUM state change exceeding threshold are stale and constitutionally void" — this obligation on CUM state implies RT-10 must maintain valid CUM state, but does not assign RT-10 a "Supporting" role designation.

### Remediation

In next revision: Revise RS-29.2 and RS-31 to either:
(a) Remove Decision from the SUPPORTING table and explain RT-10's PAIR 32 P6/P8 obligations as passive constraints during the Decision phase (not a phase Support role); or
(b) Add a note that RT-10's "Decision" characterization as SUPPORTING reflects PAIR 32 P6/P8 obligations, which are not captured in the A1 §15.2 table but are constitutionally real obligations derived from A1 PAIR 32.

### Certification Impact Detail

RT-10 does not claim Decision authority (AIR-3). RT-10 does not participate in deliberation formation or Decision formation. The SUPPORTING characterization does not expand RT-10's authority or responsibility beyond what PAIR 32 P6/P8 require. No boundary violation. No unconstitutional operation would result from implementing RT-10 per this specification. Classified Class IV.
