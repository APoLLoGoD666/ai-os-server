## 1. Remediation Summary by Finding

### FAA-11RF-002 (Class I — Critical Constitutional Defect)

**Section:** RS-10 — Managed Objects, Item 3

**Deficiency:** RS-10 Item 3 listed "CivilizationalDecision (CD)" as a constitutional object produced by RT-11 and delivered to RT-12. This is a Class I boundary violation against A0 §3.12 Produced Objects (which assigns "CivilizationalDecisionProposal (for RT-12)" to RT-11) and A0 §3.13 Owned Objects (which assigns "CivilizationalDecision" to RT-12). The item also internally contradicted RS-07, RS-09, and RS-20 within the same specification.

**Correction implemented:** RS-10 Item 3 heading and body replaced. "CivilizationalDecision (CD)" replaced with "CivilizationalDecisionProposal (CDP)" throughout Item 3. The replacement body accurately describes: (a) CDP as RT-11's produced and managed artifact; (b) RT-12's ownership of the CivilizationalDecision formed from this proposal; (c) explicit statement that RT-11 does not own or produce CivilizationalDecision. All DA field requirements preserved unchanged.

**Constitutional basis confirmed:** A0-v1.1.1 §3.12 Produced Objects; A0-v1.1.1 §3.13 Owned Objects; RS-07 (internally consistent).

---

### FAA-11RF-003 (Class II — Material Certification Defect)

**Section:** RS-03 — Purpose; RS-04 — Scope

**Deficiency:** RS-03 and RS-04 — the most prominent descriptive sections of the specification — mischaracterized RT-11's constitutional output as "CivilizationalDecision" in five distinct instances:
1. RS-03 Primary Purpose: "issuing CivilizationalDecisions"
2. RS-03 Decision Purpose: "producing CivilizationalDecision objects"
3. RS-03 Constitutional Loop Purpose: "CivilizationalDecision → RT-12"
4. RS-04 Scope Definition Item 2: "producing CivilizationalDecision objects"
5. RS-04 In-Scope List: "CivilizationalDecision production"

Per A0 §3.12 R6, RT-11 produces "CivilizationalDecision proposals" (the CivilizationalDecisionProposal). The formed CivilizationalDecision is RT-12's constitutional output per A0 §3.13.

**Correction implemented:** All five instances replaced with "CivilizationalDecisionProposal" as the RT-11 produced object. Each replacement explicitly identifies RT-12's role in forming the CivilizationalDecision from RT-11's proposal, ensuring the RT-11/RT-12 boundary is clearly stated. The substantive description of RT-11's authority exercise (AIR-3 at civilizational scope) was preserved unchanged.

**Constitutional basis confirmed:** A0-v1.1.1 §3.12 Produced Objects; A0-v1.1.1 §3.12 R6; A0-v1.1.1 §3.13 Owned Objects and R2.

### FAA-11RF-001 (Class III — Minor Defect)

**Section:** RS-06 — Authority

**Deficiency:** RS-06 stated "D-6 §4.1 introduces the five constitutional authority types (AIR-1 through AIR-5)." D6 §4.1 does not use AIR-N labels for authority types. The AIR-N labels for authority types originate from A1-v1.2 §5.1 (Authority Type Distribution table). D6 §4.7 uses AIR-N labels for a wholly different system — the five Authority Integrity Rules — creating a conflation risk.

**Correction implemented:** Authority Source line updated to show "D-6 §4.2–4.4" as the sections defining authority types. Derivation Chain Step 1 rewritten to: (a) credit D6 §4.1 correctly for introducing the five authority type concepts by name (without AIR-N labels); (b) credit D6 §4.2–4.4 for defining those types; (c) credit A1 §5.1 as the source of AIR-N designators; (d) explicitly note that D6 §4.7's AIR-N labels refer to Authority Integrity Rules — a distinct system — and that RS-06 does not conflate these. The authority assignment itself (RT-11 holds AIR-3 at civilizational scope) was not changed.

**Constitutional basis confirmed:** D6-v1.0 §4.1; D6-v1.0 §4.2–4.4; D6-v1.0 §4.7; A1-v1.2 §5.1.

### FAA-11RF-004 (Class III — Minor Defect)

**Section:** RS-13 — External Interactions, PAIR 35

**Deficiency:** PAIR 35 Constitutional Basis cited "D-7 §6.1 (Amendment Process); A0 §3.16 (RT-16 Amendment Runtime)" — omitting "A0 §3.11" which appears in A1-v1.2 PAIR 35's Authorizing list: "D-7 §6.1, A0 §3.11, §3.16."

**Correction implemented:** "A0 §3.11" added to the PAIR 35 Constitutional Basis field. The corrected citation now reads: "D-7 §6.1 (Amendment Process); A0 §3.11; A0 §3.16 (RT-16 Amendment Runtime)." No other PAIR 35 characterization was modified.

**Constitutional basis confirmed:** A1-v1.2 PAIR 35 Authorizing citations.

### FAA-11RF-005 (Class III — Minor Defect)

**Section:** RS-13 — External Interactions, Forbidden Interactions table

**Deficiency:** The Forbidden Interactions table cited "A1 §14.3 FR-3" for the RT-11 self-referential deliberation prohibition. FR-3 is defined in A1-v1.2 §11.2 (Forbidden Recursion), not §14.3 (Forbidden Interactions). A1 §14.3 does not contain an entry for RT-11 self-referential deliberation.

**Correction implemented:** Citation changed from "A1 §14.3 FR-3" to "A1 §11.2 FR-3" in the Forbidden Interactions table row for RT-11 self-referential deliberation. No other text in the row was modified.

**Constitutional basis confirmed:** A1-v1.2 §11.2 (Forbidden Recursion) FR-3.
