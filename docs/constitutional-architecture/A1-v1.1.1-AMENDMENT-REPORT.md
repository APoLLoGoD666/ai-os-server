## 1 — PURPOSE

This amendment corrects the systematic RT-07 identity error and all downstream RT-07 relationship errors in A1-v1.0-canonical.md. A1 v1.0 designated RT-07 as "Temporal Coherence Runtime" with AIR-1 temporal domain authority — both constitutionally incorrect per A0 §3.8 and D6 Part 4. This misidentification propagated into 10 PAIR entries (incorrect directions, fabricated blocking authority, unconstitutional temporal functions), produced one fabricated object type (Temporal Sequence Record), and introduced a critical Gate 6 ownership error (§8.1 VC-6 and PAIR 17 BLOCKING direction).

A1-v1.1.1 applies the minimum corrections required to bring A1 into constitutional compliance with A0 §3.8 and D-series authority. No D-series documents, A0, or R-series specifications are modified by this amendment.

## 3 — PROBLEM IDENTIFIED

### 3.1 The Originating Error

A1 v1.0 established "Temporal Coherence Runtime" as RT-07's canonical name in §3.0 with "temporal ordering, sequence integrity" as its role. This is constitutionally incorrect. A0 §3.8 names RT-07 "Memory Runtime" with a 12-responsibility profile in the memory, persistence, and provenance domain.

The error then propagated into:
- §4.2: Wrong abbreviated label "(Temporal)" in the Tier-2 ASCII diagram
- §5.1: Fabricated AIR-1 "Temporal domain" authority — no A0 §3.8 or D6 basis
- 10 PAIR entries: incorrect directions, fabricated blocking authority, unconstitutional temporal functions
- §6.1: Fabricated "Temporal Sequence Record" object type
- §6.2: False "temporal anchor RT-07" in the Observation Projection provenance chain
- §7.1: RT-07 mutation ownership listing the non-existent "Temporal Sequence Records"
- §8.1: VC-6 assigning RT-07 + RT-03 Gate 6 ownership
- §15.2: RT-07 described as capturing "temporal records" at every phase
- §16.7: CLI-4 attributed to "RT-07 involvement in every interaction requiring temporal anchoring"

### 3.2 Classification

**Type:** Originating identity error with systematic downstream propagation  
**Source:** A1 v1.0 introduced "Temporal Coherence Runtime" with no constitutional basis  
**Severity:** Class I (originating identity error); Class I (PAIR 17 BLOCKING direction — constitutionally impossible authority relationship)  
**Scope of contamination:** 10 PAIRs, 1 missing PAIR, 3 identity sections, 5 related sections  
**Blocking nature:** PAIR 17 BLOCKING direction falsely placed RT-07 above RT-03 in Gate 6 evaluation — constitutionally impossible per D4 §4.6 and A0 §3.4

### 3.3 Most Critical Error: PAIR 17 BLOCKING Direction

PAIR 17 in A1 v1.0 stated:
> *"RT-07 → RT-03 (BLOCK): RT-03 Gate 6 requires RT-07 temporal coherence attestation before admission. RT-07 owns temporal sequencing authority; RT-03 cannot override temporal determination."*

This is constitutionally impossible. D4 §4.6 defines Gate 6 mandatory inputs as proposed operation, asserted constitutional timestamp, and ChangeRecord/HistoricalAnchor history — all sourced from RT-05. R3 RT03-PROC-07 states Gate 6 evaluation is RT-03 internal via RT-05 causal ordering records; RT-07 is not mentioned. The claim "RT-07 owns temporal sequencing authority; RT-03 cannot override temporal determination" has zero D-series or A0 grounding.
