## RS-03 — Functional Model

### RS-03.1 Constitutional Purpose

Verbatim from A0-v1.1.1-canonical.md §3.15: "To form Observed Consequences from external reality post-projection, compare them against Effect Expectations, register divergences in the Causal Model Divergence Register, trigger Understanding updates, and assign terminal status to Open Action Register entries — closing the Constitutional Loop and fulfilling the civilization's mandatory Reality Feedback obligation."

### RS-03.2 Functional Description

RT-14 (Reflection Runtime) is the constitutional mechanism that closes the Constitutional Loop. After RT-13 (Action Runtime) crosses the Projection Boundary into external reality and produces effects, two streams of information become available to RT-14:

1. **Effect Expectation Records** (from RT-13): what RT-13 expected the action to produce, registered before the Projection Boundary crossing.
2. **Consequence Observation Records** (from RT-08): what external reality actually produced in response to the action, formed by RT-08 through the Observation Projection Lifecycle after RT-13 generates consequence signals.

RT-14's core function is the constitutional comparison of these two streams. RT-14 forms ObservedConsequenceRecords that embody this comparison, and where the actual consequences diverge from expected consequences, RT-14 produces CausalModelDivergenceRecords and triggers Understanding revision throughout the epistemic chain (RT-09, RT-11, RT-15).

RT-14 additionally closes the OpenActionRegisterEntry lifecycle by assigning one of the four canonical terminal states (COMPLETE, PARTIAL, FAILED, or LOST) to every entry once the Observed Consequence is formed.

### RS-03.3 Role in the Reality Feedback Loop (D5 Part 8)

D5 Part 8 establishes the Reality Feedback Loop as the constitutional mechanism by which the Reality Fabric remains aligned with External Reality. RT-14 is the operational realization of this loop (A0 §3.15 Constitutional Traceability: "D5 Part 8 (Reality Feedback Loop) → RT-14 is the operational realization of the mandatory feedback loop").

The loop structure per D5 §8.1 and A0 §4.7:
- External Reality produces consequences (following RT-13 Projection Boundary crossing)
- RT-14 captures and compares consequences against expectations
- RT-14 registers divergences and triggers Understanding updates
- Updated Understanding re-enters the epistemic chain (RT-09 → RT-10 → RT-11)
- The cycle continues: consequence observation becomes the seed of the next loop iteration

This is not a one-time procedure — it is a continuous constitutional cycle (D5 §8.1: "The Reality Alignment Loop must be operational at all times").

### RS-03.4 What RT-14 Is NOT

RT-14 does NOT:
- Form CivilizationalDecisions — that is RT-12 (Decision Runtime) (A0 §3.13)
- Form CivilizationalDecisionProposals — that is RT-11 (Civilization Intelligence Runtime) (A0 §3.12)
- Project actions across the Projection Boundary — that is RT-13 (Action Runtime) (A0 §3.14)
- Produce raw ObservationRecords or ConsequenceObservationRecords — that is RT-08 (Observation Runtime) (A0 §3.9)
- Execute TOC-4 or TOC-5 (Model Revision) — RT-14 triggers these; RT-11 executes them (A0 §7.6: "RT-14 executes TOC-3 (Outcome Comparison); RT-11 executes TOC-4 and TOC-5")
- Own the OpenActionRegisterEntry — that is RT-12 (A0 §3.13 Owned Objects); RT-14 assigns terminal status and owns the TerminalStatusRecord
- Directly observe external reality — RT-14 receives ConsequenceObservationRecords formed by RT-08; it does not initiate the Observation Projection Lifecycle independently

## RS-04 — Functional Boundary

### RS-04.1 RT-13 / RT-14 Boundary

**RT-13's scope ends at:** (1) crossing the Projection Boundary into external reality and executing the action; (2) registering Effect Expectations before crossing; (3) sending ConsequenceObservationTriggers to RT-08; (4) assigning Projection Responsibility for resulting effects. RT-13 does not form Observed Consequences (A0 §3.14 Dependents: "RT-14 (receives Effect Expectations for consequence comparison)").

**RT-14's scope begins at:** receipt of the EffectExpectationRecord (from RT-13 via RT-05, after Kernel processing) and receipt of the ConsequenceObservationRecord (from RT-08 via RT-05, after Kernel processing). RT-14 does not execute or project — it observes, compares, assesses, registers, and triggers.

**Explicit boundary statement:** RT-13 executes; RT-14 observes consequences. The constitutional separation is enforced by A1 PAIR 48: "RT-13 → RT-14 (direct): FORBIDDEN. RT-13 executes; RT-14 observes independently." RT-13 does not direct RT-14 in any manner.

**Ground:** A0 §3.14 (RT-13 Dependents), A0 §3.15 (RT-14 Dependencies), A1 PAIR 48, R13-v1.0-canonical.md RS-32.

### RS-04.2 RT-08 / RT-14 Boundary

**RT-08's scope:** RT-08 forms ConsequenceObservationRecords when triggered by RT-13 consequence signals and by RT-14's consequence observation triggers. RT-08 applies the full Observation Projection Lifecycle to consequence signals, producing ConsequenceObservationRecords that are admitted through RT-03 and delivered to RT-14 (R8-v1.1-canonical.md: "RT-08 shall form Consequence Observation Records when RT-14 triggers observation").

**RT-14's scope:** RT-14 receives the ConsequenceObservationRecords produced by RT-08. RT-14 does NOT produce ObservationRecords or ConsequenceObservationRecords — these are RT-08-owned objects. RT-14 owns ObservedConsequenceRecords (distinct from ConsequenceObservationRecords — RT-08 produces the observation; RT-14 forms the comparative consequence assessment from that observation).

**Constitutional object distinction (critical):**
- ConsequenceObservationRecord: produced by RT-08 (owned by RT-08) — raw observational data on what external reality produced
- ObservedConsequenceRecord: produced by RT-14 (owned by RT-14) — the constitutional comparison product of ConsequenceObservationRecord vs. EffectExpectationRecord

**Ground:** A0 §3.9 (RT-08 owned objects), A0 §3.15 (RT-14 owned objects, consumed objects), A1 PAIR 49, R8-v1.1-canonical.md RS-09, RS-27.

### RS-06.3 D6 §4.7 Authority Integrity Rules — Separate System

- D6 §4.2-4.6 AIR-N: the TYPE of authority (Observation, Interpretation, Decision, Projection, Audit)
- D6 §4.7 AIR-N: INTEGRITY RULES about how those authority types must be allocated (AIR-1: no collapse into single actor; AIR-2: no unauthorized projection; AIR-3: no knowledge monopolization; AIR-4: no hidden decisions; AIR-5: audit independence)

RT-14's AIR-1 Observation Authority for the Consequence domain is subject to all five D6 §4.7 Authority Integrity Rules. In particular:
- D6 §4.7 AIR-1: RT-14's Observation Authority over consequences is constitutionally separated from Projection Authority (RT-13), Decision Authority (RT-12), and Audit Authority (RT-04)
- D6 §4.7 AIR-5: RT-04 holds Audit Authority independently of RT-14; RT-04 audits RT-14's operations (PAIR 47)

## RS-10 — State Variables

RT-14 maintains the following logical state during consequence assessment. These are constitutional characterizations — no implementation data types, storage mechanisms, or programming constructs are specified.

**Consequence Assessment State:**
- **EffectExpectationSet:** the set of EffectExpectationRecords currently awaiting consequence comparison; populated when RT-13 delivers EffectExpectationRecords for pending Action Projections
- **ConsequenceObservationSet:** the set of ConsequenceObservationRecords received from RT-08 for active consequence cycles
- **ComparisonInProgress:** logical indicator that a comparison between an EffectExpectationRecord and a ConsequenceObservationRecord is underway
- **DivergenceAssessmentState:** whether a divergence has been detected for the current comparison and what its magnitude classification is

**Open Action Register Monitoring State:**
- **OpenEntryMonitorSet:** the set of OpenActionRegisterEntries (owned by RT-12) that RT-14 is constitutionally obligated to monitor for terminal state achievement (A0 §3.15 Responsibility 13; RT14-INV-6)
- **TerminalStateAchieved:** logical set of entries that have reached a canonical terminal state (COMPLETE, PARTIAL, FAILED, or LOST)
- **TerminalStatePending:** logical set of entries awaiting terminal state assignment; entries in this set represent open constitutional obligations

**Feedback Loop State:**
- **FeedbackLoopOpenSet:** the set of Action Projections for which the Constitutional Loop has not yet been closed; each open entry represents a CLI-3 obligation (D8 CLI-3: Feedback Completeness)
- **FeedbackLoopClosedSet:** the set of Action Projections for which RT-14 has confirmed loop closure (ObservedConsequenceRecord formed, TerminalStatusRecord delivered)

**Effect Observation Window State:**
- **EffectObservationWindowActive:** tracking whether the Effect Observation Window (D8 INV-6) for each pending Action Projection remains active or has expired
- **WindowExpiredSet:** entries whose Effect Observation Window has expired without Observed Consequence — these enter the Broken Feedback Protocol (D5 §8.4 BFP-1 through BFP-4)

## RS-11 — State Transitions

RT-14 undergoes the following constitutional state transitions:

**Transition T1: Idle → ConsequenceAssessmentActive**
- Trigger: Receipt of ConsequenceObservationRecord from RT-08 with a matching EffectExpectationRecord
- Constitutional basis: A0 §3.15 Responsibilities 1 and 2; D5 Part 8 §8.2 "Effect → Observation" stage
- Precondition: Valid EffectExpectationRecord must be in RT-14's EffectExpectationSet for the same Action Projection ID

**Transition T2: ConsequenceAssessmentActive → ObservedConsequenceFormed**
- Trigger: Completion of comparison between ConsequenceObservationRecord and EffectExpectationRecord
- Constitutional basis: A0 §3.15 Responsibility 3 (Form Observed Consequence objects); A1 §12.5 Step 3-4 (Reflection Execution Order)
- Output produced: ObservedConsequenceRecord (owned by RT-14)

**Transition T3: ObservedConsequenceFormed → DivergenceRegistered (conditional)**
- Trigger: Divergence detected between expected and observed consequences
- Constitutional basis: A0 §3.15 Responsibility 4 (Register divergences); RT14-INV-2 (every divergence produces a CausalModelDivergenceRecord); D7 TOC-3
- Output produced: CausalModelDivergenceRecord (owned by RT-14)
- Note: This transition is conditional on divergence detection; if no divergence, proceed to T5

**Transition T4: DivergenceRegistered → UnderstandingUpdateTriggered**
- Trigger: Completion of CausalModelDivergenceRecord formation
- Constitutional basis: A0 §3.15 Responsibilities 5, 6, 7, 9; RT14-INV-5 (mandatory and unconditional feedback to understanding)
- Outputs produced: KnowledgeUpdateTrigger (to RT-09), CUMRevisionTrigger (to RT-11), DomainUpdateTrigger (to RT-15); if divergence significant, Causal Model revision initiated through RT-11 (TOC-4/TOC-5)

**Transition T5: ObservedConsequenceFormed → TerminalStatusAssigned**
- Trigger: ObservedConsequenceRecord formed (with or without divergence)
- Constitutional basis: A0 §3.15 Responsibility 8; RT14-INV-4 (entries closed only after Observed Consequence formation); RT14-INV-5
- Output produced: OpenActionRegisterTerminalStatusRecord (owned by RT-14); Understanding update triggers issued (even in no-divergence case per RT14-INV-5)
- Note: Understanding update triggers are mandatory and unconditional (RT14-INV-5), regardless of whether divergence is detected

**Transition T6: TerminalStatusAssigned → FeedbackLoopClosed**
- Trigger: RT-12 confirms receipt and application of OpenActionRegisterTerminalStatusRecord; RT-14 confirms feedback loop closure per A1 §12.5 Step 9
- Constitutional basis: D8 CLI-3 (Feedback Completeness); A1 §14.4 (RT-14 confirmation is a Loop-Ending interaction)
- Note: This transition satisfies one CLI-3 obligation; the FeedbackLoopOpenSet entry moves to FeedbackLoopClosedSet

**Transition T7: EffectObservationWindowExpired → BrokenFeedbackProtocol**
- Trigger: Effect Observation Window expires (D8 INV-6) without Observed Consequence formation
- Constitutional basis: D5 §8.4 (BFP-1 through BFP-4); RT14-INV-6 (RT-14 must not permit permanent open entries)
- Protocol: BFP-1 (LOST classification) → BFP-2 (Escalation) → BFP-3 (Alternative Observation) → BFP-4 (Conservative Assumptions)

**EP-4 — RT-03 Gate Processing Clearance:** All input objects must have been admitted through RT-03's six-gate process before RT-14 consumes them. RT-14 operates only on constitutionally admitted objects (A0 §3.15 Responsibility 12; D4 KMP).

**EP-6 — Effect Observation Window Active:** The Effect Observation Window (D8 INV-6) for the Action Projection must not have expired. If it has expired, RT-14 enters the Broken Feedback Protocol (D5 §8.4 BFP-1) rather than standard consequence assessment.
