## DEF-001 — Erroneous D6 §3.4 Citation

**Class: II — Minor Constitutional**

**Locations in R7:**
- O7-11 (RS-05): "Constitutional basis: A0 v1.1 §3.8 Responsibility 11; **D6 §3.4**; R0 ADR-3"
- PAIR 21 characterization (RS-13): "Constitutional basis: A1 v1.0 PAIR 21; A0 v1.1 §3.8 Responsibility 9 and 11; **D6 §3.4**; R0 ADR-3"
- RS-13.3 (Specific PAIR 17 context): "**D6 §3.4**"
- RS-35 (Prohibited Responsibilities): "O7-9; **D6 §3.4**; R0 ADR-3"

**What R7 asserts:** That "D6 §3.4" is the constitutional source for RT-04's audit authority independence / AIR-5 non-delegability as applied to RT-07's obligation to permit RT-04 unrestricted audit access.

**What the source documents say:** D6-v1.0-canonical.md has no section §3.4. D6 Part 3 (lines 183–489) defines the twelve civilization domains (DOM-000001 through DOM-000012) using domain-numbered entries, not decimal sub-section numbering. The decimal sub-section scheme does not produce a "3.4" section in D6.

The correct sources for RT-04's audit authority independence are:
- D6 §4.6 — Audit Authority (defines the nature and constitutional basis of audit independence)
- D6 §4.7 — AIR-5 (Audit Independence; non-delegable by D6 §4.7's explicit terms)

Both sections are in D6 Part 4 (Authority Model), which is the correct part for authority type definitions.

**Substantive impact:** LOW. The underlying claim — that RT-04 possesses non-delegable audit authority (AIR-5) over RT-07 persisted records, and that RT-07 may not block RT-04 access — is constitutionally correct and additionally supported by R0 ADR-3 (already cited alongside the erroneous D6 §3.4). The error is a citation pointing to a non-existent section.

**Severity justification:** Class II because the citation is verifiably wrong (D6 §3.4 does not exist), it appears at four distinct locations in R7, and it would cause a citation verification failure during independent review. The constitutional substance is not undermined because R0 ADR-3 independently establishes the same claim.

**Required correction:** Replace all four occurrences of "D6 §3.4" with "D6 §4.6; D6 §4.7 (AIR-5)".

**Affected sections:** O7-11, RS-13 (PAIR 21 characterization), RS-13.3, RS-35.

**Location in R7:** RS-01 identity table.

**What R7 says:** The RS-01 table contains: Runtime identifier, Runtime name, Document, Version, Status, Constitutional seat, Actor, Specification standard, Predecessor document, Next document. Tier is not among the listed fields.

**What the source documents say:**

## DEF-003 — RS-27 Dependents Include RT-08 and RT-14 Not in A0 §3.8 or A0 §4.1

**Class: II — Minor Constitutional**

**Location in R7:** RS-27 (Dependents).

**What R7 says:** RS-27 lists six dependents: RT-09, RT-10, RT-11, RT-04, RT-08, RT-14. R7 notes that "RT-08 and RT-14 are from A0 cross-references (A0 §3.9 and A0 §3.15), not from A0 §3.8 itself."

**What the source documents say:**

A0 §3.8 line 780 (verbatim):
> "Dependents: RT-09 (historical Knowledge States), RT-10 (historical Understanding Models), RT-11 (historical CUMs and Deliberation Records), RT-04 (all historical records)."

A0 §4.1 (Complete Runtime Dependency Graph) — independent verification of A0 §3.8: Lists RT-09, RT-10, RT-11, RT-04 as RT-07 dependents. RT-08 and RT-14 do not appear as RT-07 dependents in A0 §4.1.

A0 §3.9 (RT-08, Observation Runtime) lists RT-07 under RT-08's Dependencies, confirming RT-08 consumes RT-07's output. This makes RT-08 a dependent of RT-07, but this relationship is not reflected in A0 §3.8's Dependents list or A0 §4.1.

A0 §3.15 (RT-14) line 1101 lists RT-07 under RT-14's Dependencies. Same situation.

**Analysis:** The interactions between RT-07 and RT-08/RT-14 are constitutionally real — grounded in A0 §3.9 and A0 §3.15 respectively. However, R0 CERT-04 requires RS-27 to be "the exact inverse of all other RS-26 references to this runtime" and to be in bijective correspondence with A0 §4.1. A0 §4.1 does not list RT-08 or RT-14 as RT-07 dependents.

R7's addition is not invented — it is derived from A0 cross-references. But it exceeds what A0 §3.8 and A0 §4.1 explicitly enumerate, creating a CERT-04 tension.

**Severity justification:** Class II because: (1) the additions are constitutionally grounded in A0 §3.9 and §3.15, not invented; (2) however, A0 §4.1 is the authoritative dependency graph and R7 expands it without an A0 amendment; (3) this creates a CERT-04 verification tension that cannot be resolved by reading R7 alone.

**Resolution options (in order of constitutional preference):**

Option A (Preferred): Request A0 §4.1 and A0 §3.8 amendment to add RT-08 and RT-14 to RT-07's Dependents list. This resolves the tension canonically. After amendment, DEF-003 is fully resolved.

Option B (Acceptable): Move RT-08 and RT-14 to a clearly separated "Extended Dependents (A0 Cross-References)" subsection in RS-27, distinct from the "Core Dependents (A0 §3.8 / A0 §4.1)" subsection. The CERT-04 self-assessment must be revised to acknowledge the core/extended split and note that CERT-04 covers only the core set.

Option C (Unacceptable): Remove RT-08 and RT-14 from RS-27 entirely. This would satisfy CERT-04 strictly but would omit constitutionally real interactions — this option is rejected as incompatible with RS-27's purpose.

**Affected section:** RS-27, RS-36 CERT-04 self-assessment.

**Class: II — Minor Constitutional**

**What R7 says:** "CERT-04: PASS — RS-27 lists RT-08 and RT-14 per A0 cross-references."

A0 §4.1 does not include RT-08 or RT-14 as RT-07 dependents (see DEF-003). Therefore, strict R0 CERT-04 produces a non-trivial evaluation when RS-27 extends beyond A0 §4.1.

**Analysis:** R7's CERT-04 self-assessment as an unconditional PASS does not accurately represent the bijection question under the strict R0 criterion. R7 should self-assess CERT-04 as either "CONDITIONAL PASS (pending A0 §4.1 amendment)" or "PASS with noted exception (RT-08/RT-14 from A0 §3.x cross-references; not in A0 §4.1)."

**Severity justification:** Class II because: (1) the self-assessment misrepresents the strict CERT-04 criterion; (2) an independent certifier reviewing RS-36 would find the CERT-04 PASS unsupported without the A0 cross-reference caveat; (3) this affects the integrity of the CERT self-assessment section.

**Required correction:** Revise RS-36 CERT-04 to read: "CONDITIONAL PASS — RS-26 (Dependencies: RT-03, RT-05) is in exact bijection with A0 §3.8 line 778 and A0 §4.1. RS-27 (Dependents) lists RT-09, RT-10, RT-11, RT-04 per A0 §3.8 and A0 §4.1, and additionally lists RT-08 and RT-14 per A0 §3.9 and §3.15 cross-references respectively. CERT-04 full PASS is conditional on A0 §4.1 amendment to include RT-08 and RT-14 as explicit RT-07 dependents, or on reclassification of RT-08 and RT-14 to a non-CERT-04 subsection of RS-27."

**Affected section:** RS-36 (CERT-04).

## DEF-005 — D7 §9.2 and §9.4 Cited for Claims Those Sections Do Not Make

**Class: III — Major Constitutional**

**Locations in R7:**
- PAIR 38 characterization note: "D7 §9.2 (Understanding Coherence — RT-07 provides historical baseline for CUM resynthesis)"
- PAIR 39 characterization note: "D7 §9.4 (Decision Coherence — decisions relying on expired models traceable through RT-07)"

**What the source documents say:**

D7-v1.0-canonical.md §9.2 actual text (Understanding Coherence):
> "Constitutional response: CUM resynthesis from current domain knowledge states."

This section describes what must happen when understanding models become incoherent. It does not mention RT-07 or name a "historical baseline" provider.

D7-v1.0-canonical.md §9.4 actual text (Decision Coherence):
> "Decisions relying on expired Understanding Models must be reviewed."

This section describes a decision review requirement. It does not mention RT-07 or name RT-07 as a traceability mechanism.

**Analysis:** Both citations represent functional inferences from the RT-07 context projected onto D7 sections that do not make those statements. The underlying functional claims — that RT-07 provides historical records relevant to CUM resynthesis and to decision coherence review — are constitutionally correct and directly derivable from A0 §3.8 Responsibility 3: "Provide historical state access to RT-09, RT-10, RT-11, and all other runtimes requiring historical context." A0 §3.8 is the correct and sufficient citation for both claims.

**Substantive impact:** MODERATE. The cited functional claims are correct; only the cited source sections are incorrect. However, citing D7 sections for claims that D7 does not make is a citation integrity failure — a reader who checks D7 §9.2 will not find the cited claim.

**Severity justification:** Class III because: (1) citations must accurately represent what the cited source says; (2) D7 §9.2 and §9.4 do not make the claims for which they are cited; (3) the functional claims are nonetheless supportable — the error is in citation, not substance. This is correctable without substantive constitutional change.

**Required correction:**
- PAIR 38 characterization note: Replace "D7 §9.2 (Understanding Coherence — RT-07 provides historical baseline for CUM resynthesis)" with "A0 v1.1 §3.8 Responsibility 3 (historical state access provision — historical Understanding Models for resynthesis context)"
- PAIR 39 characterization note: Replace "D7 §9.4 (Decision Coherence — decisions relying on expired models traceable through RT-07)" with "A0 v1.1 §3.8 Responsibility 3 (historical state access provision — historical CUMs and Deliberation Records for decision coherence traceability)"

**Affected section:** RS-13 (PAIR 38 and PAIR 39 characterization notes).

**Substantive impact:** INFORMATIONAL. The expansion is justified by A0 §3.8 Responsibility 3. No correction required.

## DEF-012 — ADR-1 SATISFIED Claim Lacks Clarity on RT-07 Non-AIR-N Status

**Class: III — Major Constitutional**

**Location in R7:** RS-06 §6.4: "ADR-1 (Authority derived from constitutional sources): SATISFIED — derivation chain is D-2 §XIII → D6 DOM-000004 → D8 §5.7 → A0 v1.1 §3.8 → R0 → R7 v1.0"

**What R0 ADR-1 says:** "No runtime may derive constitutional authority directly from any D-series document. The authority derivation path is mandatory: D6 → A0 → A1 → R0 → R1–R16."

**Analysis:** R0 ADR-1 was designed to govern AIR-N authority derivation — the path by which D6 defines authority types, A0 instantiates them, A1 specifies how they flow, and R0/R-series codify them. Since RT-07 holds no AIR-N authority, ADR-1's standard derivation chain does not apply to RT-07 in the typical sense. RT-07's authority is a constitutional mandate (infrastructure obligation) rather than an epistemic authority assignment.

R7's RS-06 §6.4 states ADR-1 is SATISFIED and provides the derivation chain D-2 §XIII → D6 DOM-000004 → D8 §5.7 → A0 §3.8 → R0 → R7. This is RT-07's constitutional mandate derivation chain, not an AIR-N derivation chain. A reader applying the R0 ADR-1 standard to this claim will note that D6 DOM-000004 is a domain definition (not the D6 → A0 AIR assignment path specified in ADR-1). The "SATISFIED" designation without explanation could mislead a reader into thinking R7 claims the AIR-N derivation chain was followed, or alternatively into thinking ADR-1 is being misapplied.

**Substantive impact:** MODERATE. The ADR-1 compliance claim is defensible only if properly explained. As written, the distinction between "RT-07 has no AIR-N authority (ADR-1 does not govern AIR-N for RT-07 because there is no AIR-N to derive)" and "RT-07's constitutional mandate derives from the D-series mandate chain (distinct from the AIR-N assignment chain)" is implicit rather than explicit.

**Severity justification:** Class III because: (1) ADR-1 compliance is a CERT-03 requirement; (2) the current phrasing is ambiguous and could be misread; (3) the correct reasoning requires a sentence of explanation rather than a simple "SATISFIED."

**Required correction:** Revise RS-06 §6.4 ADR-1 entry to read:

> "ADR-1 (Authority derived from constitutional sources): SATISFIED — RT-07 holds no AIR-N authority; ADR-1's AIR-N derivation path (D6 → A0 → A1 → R0 → R-series) governs epistemic authority assignments and is therefore not applicable to RT-07's non-AIR infrastructure mandate. RT-07's constitutional mandate to persist all constitutional objects derives from D-2 §XIII → D6 DOM-000004 → D8 §5.7 → A0 v1.1 §3.8 → R0 → R7 v1.0 — a D-series mandate chain independent of the AIR-N assignment chain."

**Affected section:** RS-06 §6.4.
