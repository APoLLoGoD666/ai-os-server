## FAA-11R-002

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Finding ID** | FAA-11R-002 |
| **Class** | III — Minor Defect |
| **Section Affected** | RS-06 (Authority) |
| **Constitutional Basis** | D6 §4.2–§4.6 (five authority types defined per section); D6 §4.4 (Decision Authority); A1-v1.2 §5.1 (authority type distribution table); R0 §3.5 (authority type names AIR-1 through AIR-5) |

**Description:**

RS-06 contains three distinct errors in authority type citation and naming:

**Error 1 — Wrong D6 Section:** RS-06 states "D-6 §4.3 establishes the five authority types (AIR-1 through AIR-5) and their constitutional definitions" and "AIR-3 Definition (D-6 §4.3): Decision Authority." D6 §4.3 is titled "Interpretation Authority" — it defines one authority type (Interpretation), not all five. Decision Authority is defined at D6 §4.4. The section that introduces the five-type framework is D6 §4.1 ("Constitutional Principle of Domain Authority").

**Error 2 — Wrong Authority Type Names for Non-Held Types:** RS-06 lists authority types RT-11 does not hold as follows:
- "AIR-2 (Knowledge Authority)" — incorrect. Per A1 §5.1 and R0 §3.5: AIR-2 = Interpretation Authority.
- "AIR-4 (Action Authority)" — incorrect. Per A1 §5.1 and R0 §3.5: AIR-4 = Projection Authority.
- "AIR-5 (Constitutional Authority)" — incorrect. Per A1 §5.1 and R0 §3.5: AIR-5 = Audit Authority.

Only AIR-1 "Observation Authority" and AIR-3 "Decision Authority" are correctly named.

**What is correct:** RT-11's held authority (AIR-3, Decision Authority, civilizational scope) is correctly identified and correctly derived through A0 §4.3 and A1 §5.1. The permission matrix is correct. The authority scope restriction is correct. The errors affect only the D6 section citation and the naming of the four non-held authority types.

**This deficiency was identified by FAA-11 as FAA-11-001 (classified Class IV). FAA-11R independently re-identifies it and classifies it Class III (minor defect) because the citation error is not purely editorial — it involves an incorrect positive factual claim about what D6 §4.3 contains.**

**Remediation Required:**
1. Change "D-6 §4.3 establishes the five authority types" to "D6 §4.1 introduces the five constitutional authority types; D6 §4.4 defines Decision Authority."
2. Change "AIR-3 Definition (D-6 §4.3)" to "AIR-3 Definition (D-6 §4.4)."
3. Change "AIR-2 (Knowledge Authority)" to "AIR-2 (Interpretation Authority)."
4. Change "AIR-4 (Action Authority)" to "AIR-4 (Projection Authority)."
5. Change "AIR-5 (Constitutional Authority)" to "AIR-5 (Audit Authority)."

**Certification Impact:** Does not block certification. Must be corrected in v1.2.

## FAA-11R-003

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Finding ID** | FAA-11R-003 |
| **Class** | III — Minor Defect |
| **Section Affected** | RS-09 (Outputs) |
| **Constitutional Basis** | A0-v1.1.1 §3.12 Produced Constitutional Objects: "CivilizationalDecisionProposal (for RT-12)"; A0 §3.13 Owned Objects: "CivilizationalDecision" (owned by RT-12) |

**Description:**

RS-09 output table, first row, labels the primary output delivered to RT-12 via PAIR 40 as "CivilizationalDecision." A0 §3.12's Produced Constitutional Objects list names this object "CivilizationalDecisionProposal (for RT-12)." A0 §3.13 owns "CivilizationalDecision" as an RT-12 object.

RS-07 correctly uses "CivilizationalDecisionProposal" terminology in its "Objects RT-11 Does Not Own" section: "CivilizationalDecision — owned by RT-12 (A0 §3.13 Owned Objects); RT-11 produces CivilizationalDecisionProposal (for RT-12), not the formed Decision." This creates an internal inconsistency: RS-07 correctly states RT-11 produces proposals, but RS-09 labels the output "CivilizationalDecision."

The constitutional distinction matters: RT-11 produces a proposal; RT-12 forms the constitutional decision object. This is the boundary between deliberation and decision formation.

**Remediation Required:** In RS-09 output table, change "CivilizationalDecision" to "CivilizationalDecisionProposal" in the Object column of the first row. Review all occurrences of "CivilizationalDecision" in RS-09 to ensure the proposal terminology is consistently used where RT-11 is the producing party.

**Certification Impact:** Does not block certification. Must be corrected in v1.2.

## FAA-11R-004

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Finding ID** | FAA-11R-004 |
| **Class** | III — Minor Defect |
| **Section Affected** | RS-12 (Internal Processes — Process 3) |
| **Constitutional Basis** | A1-v1.2 §12.3 (Decision Execution Order — 12 steps verbatim) |

**Description:**

RS-12 Process 3 "Decision Authority Execution" claims: "Source: A1-v1.2 §12.3 (Decision Execution Order, Steps 1–12 verbatim)."

A1 §12.3 Steps 1–12 (verbatim from disk):
1. RT-11 receives current CUM from RT-10
2. RT-11 verifies CUM currency (stale CUM → request RT-10 re-synthesis)
3. RT-11 initiates deliberation (Deliberation Principle — D-7 §4.1)
4. [Deliberation may request updated CUM from RT-10 — bounded recursion]
5. RT-11 forms Decision from deliberated CUM
6. RT-11 submits Decision to RT-12 for Compliance Verification
7. RT-12 verifies Decision against constitutional compliance record
8. If compliance fails: RT-11 re-deliberates (Step 3 loop, bounded)
9. RT-12 issues Compliance Verification Record
10. RT-11 submits Decision as Class A through RT-03 (Stages 1-10)
11. Decision admitted to RT-05
12. RT-04 records audit of decision

RS-12 Process 3 Steps 1–12 as written are entirely different: they describe VC-3, VC-4, DA-6, DOM-000001 registration steps that are not the A1 §12.3 steps. The "verbatim" claim is false. The Process 3 content is an internally authored elaboration of the decision process, not A1 §12.3 content. The elaboration has constitutional merit but misrepresents its source.

**Remediation Required:** Either (a) replace the header citation with an accurate source reference (e.g., "internally authored constitutional elaboration, grounded in A0 §3.12, A1 §12.3, and D7 Part 5"), or (b) reproduce the actual A1 §12.3 steps verbatim and separately document the elaborated DA/VC requirements with appropriate citations. Remove the false "verbatim" claim.

**Certification Impact:** Does not block certification. Must be corrected in v1.2.

## FAA-11R-006

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Finding ID** | FAA-11R-006 |
| **Class** | II — Material Certification Defect |
| **Section Affected** | RS-26 (Runtime Dependencies) and RS-36 CERT-09 |
| **Constitutional Basis** | A0-v1.1.1 §3.12 Dependencies: exactly "RT-10 (Domain Understanding Models), RT-15 (domain-level Understanding Models), RT-07 (historical CUMs and Deliberation Records), RT-06 (coherence status)"; R0-v1.0 CERT-09 criterion: "RS-26 dependency registry is in bijective correspondence with A0 §3.12 Dependencies list" |

**Description:**

A0 §3.12 Dependencies lists exactly four entries: RT-10, RT-15, RT-07, RT-06. RS-26 contains seven entries:

A0-listed entries (correct):
1. RT-10 (Domain Understanding Models) ✓
2. RT-15 (domain-level Understanding Models) ✓
3. RT-07 (historical CUMs and Deliberation Records) ✓
4. RT-06 (coherence status) ✓

Non-A0 entries (no constitutional basis cited):
5. RT-03 (Kernel) — not in A0 §3.12 Dependencies
6. DOM-000001 (Decision Registry) — not in A0 §3.12 Dependencies
7. D-7 Architecture — not in A0 §3.12 Dependencies

The RS-26 section header states "Runtimes and systems upon which RT-11 depends (A0-v1.1.1 §3.12 Dependencies list)" — this falsely implies the table content derives entirely from A0 §3.12.

CERT-09 self-certification states: "RS-26 now contains exactly the four dependencies listed in A0-v1.1.1 §3.12: RT-10 (Domain Understanding Models), RT-15 (domain-level Understanding Models), RT-07 (historical CUMs and Deliberation Records), RT-06 (coherence status)." This statement is factually false. RS-26 has seven entries, not four.

**Context:** The prior FAA-11-009 deficiency (RT-12 and RT-16 appearing in RS-26 as dependencies when they are dependents) was correctly remediated by removing RT-12 and RT-16. However, the remediation introduced three new entries (RT-03, DOM-000001, D-7 Architecture) that are not in A0 §3.12. The remediation of FAA-11-009 is partially correct but created a new Class II deficiency.

**Note on substantive merit:** The three additional entries (RT-03, DOM-000001, D-7 Architecture) reflect real operational dependencies of RT-11 and may have basis in other constitutional sources (e.g., D-8 MVCS requirements, A0 §4.4 execution order). However, RS-26's header attribution to A0 §3.12 is false, and no alternative constitutional basis is cited for these entries.

**Remediation Required:** Either:
- (Option A) Remove RT-03, DOM-000001, and D-7 Architecture from RS-26, limiting RS-26 to the exact four A0 §3.12 Dependencies. Correct the CERT-09 statement accordingly.
- (Option B) Retain all seven entries but restructure RS-26 to separate A0-authoritative dependencies from operationally-derived additional dependencies, citing the correct constitutional basis for each additional entry (e.g., D-8 MVCS for RT-03, A0 §4.4 for DOM-000001). Correct the false header claim and the false CERT-09 statement.

**Certification Impact:** BLOCKS unconditional certification. Renders CERT-09 as independent FAIL. This is the only Class II finding in FAA-11R.

## FAA-11R-007

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Finding ID** | FAA-11R-007 |
| **Class** | III — Minor Defect |
| **Section Affected** | RS-30 (Execution Position) |
| **Constitutional Basis** | A1-v1.2 §12.2 (Reasoning Execution Order — 10 steps); A1-v1.2 §12.3 (Decision Execution Order — 12 steps) |

**Description:**

**Error 1 — A1 §12.2 Fabricated Steps:** A1 §12.2 Reasoning Execution Order has exactly 10 steps. RT-11 does not appear by name in any of the 10 steps. RS-30 claims "Steps 1–7: RT-14, RT-09, RT-08, RT-10... Step 9: RT-11 Executes Constitutional Synthesis Process... Step 10: RT-11 CUM Coherence Assessment... Step 11: RT-11 Initiates Deliberation Architecture." Steps 9, 10, and 11 as described do not exist in A1 §12.2. A1 §12.2 Step 9 is "RT-10 submits updated DUM/CUM as Class A through RT-03" and Step 10 is "RT-04 records audit of reasoning operations." RS-30 presents fabricated steps beyond A1 §12.2's 10-step order.

**Error 2 — A1 §12.3 Non-verbatim:** RS-30 presents twelve steps for the Decision Execution Order attributed to A1 §12.3. The 12 steps in RS-30 (VC-3 execution, DA-6 classification, DOM-000001 registration, etc.) do not correspond to A1 §12.3's 12 steps (RT-11 receives CUM, verifies currency, initiates deliberation, forms decision, submits to RT-12, etc.). This is not a verbatim representation. RS-30 presents internally authored elaboration as the "RT-11 position" in A1 §12.3, which it is not.

**What is correct:** RS-30's intent is to show RT-11's constitutional activities in the execution pipeline, which is constitutionally grounded. The deficiency is the misrepresentation of sources (claiming A1 §12.2 steps and A1 §12.3 step content when neither matches). RS-12 Process 3 contains the same content (FAA-11R-004 covers that occurrence).

**Remediation Required:**
1. Remove the three fabricated A1 §12.2 Steps 9-11 and acknowledge that A1 §12.2 does not explicitly enumerate RT-11's role (RT-11's CSP is triggered by the conditions established in A1 §12.2 Step 8, with A0 §3.12 R1 governing).
2. Replace the RS-30 Decision Execution Order with either (a) the actual A1 §12.3 steps verbatim, or (b) an internally authored characterization that does not claim to be A1 §12.3 verbatim content.

**Certification Impact:** Does not block certification. Must be corrected in v1.2.
