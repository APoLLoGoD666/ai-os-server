## RS-03 — Functional Model

RT-16 (Amendment Runtime) is the civilization's sole mechanism for legitimate constitutional evolution. It is the only runtime constitutionally authorized to receive, process, and ratify amendments to the constitutional architecture.

**Core Function:** RT-16 governs the constitutional amendment process. It receives Amendment Proposals from RT-11 (the only authorized initiator), routes proposals through constitutionally mandatory checks and deliberation stages, requests the Preservation Audit from RT-04, routes to founding-level human authorization, and publishes ratified amendments to the constitutional stack.

**Event-Driven Nature:** RT-16 is dormant between amendment events. The standard Constitutional Loop (A0 §4.4, 33 steps; A1 §15.2, 10 phases) operates without RT-16 participation. RT-16 is activated exclusively by receipt of an AmendmentProposal from RT-11. A1 §12.8 governs RT-16's own 15-step amendment execution order, which is a separate execution context from the standard Constitutional Loop.

**Constitutional Significance:** RT-16 is the operational realization of D7 Part 12's Constitutional Amendment Architecture and of the D-2 Forbidden Assumption that civilization cannot govern its own evolution without human oversight. RT-16 ensures that every constitutional change requires deliberation (RT-11), independent audit (RT-04), and founding-level human authorization — no runtime and no constitutional actor can modify the constitutional stack except through RT-16's amendment process.

**What RT-16 Does:**
- Receives AmendmentProposals from RT-11 (PAIR 59)
- Classifies proposals into Amendment Classes I through IV (D7 §12.5)
- Routes Class IV proposals to immediate constitutional rejection — no deliberation opened (RT16-INV-6; D7 §12.1)
- Verifies AP-1 through AP-6 compliance for non-Class IV proposals (RT16-INV-4)
- Routes compliant proposals through deliberation in RT-11 (D7 §12.4 Stage 2)
- Requests Preservation Audit from RT-04 (RT16-INV-2; D7 §12.4 Stage 3)
- Routes to founding-level authorization from human governance actors (RT16-INV-3; D7 §12.4 Stage 4)
- Publishes ratified amendments to the constitutional stack (D7 §12.4 Stage 5)
- Maintains the Amendment Registry across all proposal states
- Produces RatifiedAmendmentRecords and AmendmentRejectionRecords
- Routes all operations through RT-03 per D4 §2.1 KMP

**What RT-16 Does NOT Do:**
- RT-16 does not self-initiate amendments; it receives proposals from RT-11 only (A1 §14.3 forbidden interaction)
- RT-16 does not govern day-to-day operations; it governs constitutional evolution only
- RT-16 does not participate in the standard Constitutional Loop (A1 §15.2 — absent from all 10 phases)
- RT-16 does not conduct deliberation; deliberation is led by RT-11 (PAIR 61 / D7 §12.4 Stage 2)
- RT-16 does not perform its own Preservation Audit; RT-04 performs it (PAIR 60)

## RS-04 — Scope and Boundaries

**RT-16 IS Responsible For:**
- The complete constitutional amendment process from proposal receipt through ratification or rejection
- Classification of Amendment Proposals into the four constitutional amendment classes
- Enforcement of all six AP requirements (AP-1 through AP-6) as gates to deliberation
- Maintaining the Amendment Registry and its six proposal states
- Issuing RatifiedAmendmentRecords and AmendmentRejectionRecords
- Routing all amendment operations through RT-03 per KMP (D4 §2.1)

**RT-16 IS NOT Responsible For:**
- Initiating amendment proposals (RT-11 is the constitutional initiator)
- Conducting deliberation (RT-11 leads deliberation; RT-16 requests and receives the output)
- Performing the Preservation Audit (RT-04 performs it; RT-16 requests and receives the result)
- Governing human constitutional actors (founding-level authorization is provided by humans; RT-16 receives and validates it)
- Standard Constitutional Loop operations (RT-16 is absent from all 10 standard phases)
- Transition monitoring following amendment publication (D7 §12.4 Stage 6; outside RT-16 explicit responsibility per A0 §3.17)

**Boundary with RT-11 (Upstream Primary Interface):**
RT-11 produces an AmendmentProposal through civilizational deliberation and delivers it to RT-16 (PAIR 59). This delivery is the constitutional trigger for RT-16 activation. RT-11 is the ONLY runtime that may initiate an amendment process (A1 §14.3). RT-16 receives the proposal, manages all subsequent stages, and returns the amendment outcome (RATIFIED or REJECTED) to RT-11 for deliberation context integration. RT-11 does not manage the Amendment Registry and does not issue RatifiedAmendmentRecords or AmendmentRejectionRecords.

**Boundary with RT-04 (Preservation Audit):**
RT-16 requests the Preservation Audit from RT-04 (PAIR 60, BLOCKING). RT-04 performs the Preservation Audit independently and returns a PreservationAuditRecord (PASS or HALT). If RT-04 returns HALT, the amendment process terminates — this is the only case where RT-04 output directly gates another runtime's operation (A1 §3.6 PAIR 60). RT-04 additionally audits all RT-16 operations under standard AIR-5 authority (universal observation, Rule R1). RT-16 may not conduct its own Preservation Audit and may not override RT-04's HALT determination.

**Boundary with RT-03 (Kernel Gate):**
RT-16 routes all Class A amendment operations — including amendment commits, RatifiedAmendmentRecord production, AmendmentRejectionRecord production, and KernelOperationManifest updates — through RT-03 per D4 §2.1 KMP (PAIR 61). RT-03 applies the standard six-gate evaluation. RT-16 must satisfy four additional preconditions before submitting an amendment commit: RT-04 Preservation Audit complete, RT-11 deliberation complete, founding-level human authorization obtained, and all six RT-03 gates passed. RT-16 may not bypass RT-03 for any constitutional text modification.

**Boundary with RT-07 (Historical Records):**
RT-16 uses RT-07 for reading historical amendment records (prior ratified amendments, prior rejections) and for persisting new amendment records (through RT-03 mediation). RT-07 does not own or manage the Amendment Registry; RT-16 owns it.

**Boundary with RT-15 (Domain Deliberation Participation):**
RT-15 instances provide domain deliberation participation when RT-16 initiates an amendment process involving their domains (A0 §4.1). This is a conditional participation dependency: RT-15 → RT-16. A0 §3.17 does not list RT-15 as a dependency of RT-16 in the Dependencies field. Per Conflict C-2 resolution (see RS-27), this relationship is a participation dependency from A0 §4.1, not an operational dependency in A0 §3.17's sense. RT-16 does not maintain DomainProfiles and does not govern domain operations.

**Boundary with Human Governance Actors:**
Founding-level authorization (D7 §12.2) is required for Class I, II, and III amendments and is provided by human constitutional actors holding D-2-level authority — not by any runtime. Per A1 §12.8 Step 9, human authorization is submitted through RT-08 → RT-03 as a Class A operation. RT-16 receives and validates the gate processing results confirming authorized amendment. RT-16 does not govern human actors; it receives and validates their authorization.

## RS-05 — Responsibilities

**Verbatim source: [A0-v1.1.1-canonical.md] §3.17. Responsibility count: 11.**

1. Receive Amendment Proposals from authorized actors (via RT-03)
2. Classify Amendment Proposals into one of four constitutional amendment classes (D7 Part 12): Class I (Operational), Class II (Structural), Class III (Foundational), or Class IV (Terminal Modification — proposals that would affect D-2's terminal commitments)
3. Verify Amendment Proposal compliance with AP-1 through AP-6 (all six requirements must be satisfied)
4. Route Amendment Proposal through constitutionally governed deliberation in RT-11
5. Request Preservation Audit from RT-04 (mandatory constitutional precondition for amendment ratification)
6. Route to founding-level authorization for ratification (human governance actors)
7. Publish ratified amendments to the constitutional stack
8. Maintain the Amendment Registry: all Amendment Proposals in all states (proposed, under deliberation, preservation audited, ratified, rejected)
9. Update the KernelOperationManifest through RT-03 if an amendment modifies Class B operations
10. Reject non-compliant Amendment Proposals with full RejectionRecord through RT-03
11. Route Class IV Amendment Proposals (Terminal Modification) to immediate constitutional rejection without opening any deliberation process; issue a RejectionRecord citing D7 §12.1 Constitutional Continuity Principle (D7 §12.1)

## RS-06 — Authority

**Authority Type Held by RT-16:** AIR-3 (Decision Authority), Amendment scope — exclusively.

**Complete Derivation Chain:**

**Step 1 — D6 §4.4 (Decision Authority type definition):**
> "The constitutional right to form CivilizationalDecisions within a domain that will result in Action Projections affecting that domain's ExternalRealitySegments."

D6 §4.4 establishes Decision Authority (the third of the five authority types) as the constitutional right to form decisions within a defined scope. RT-16's authority is a constitutional-level specialization of this type — the right to form constitutional amendments rather than domain-level decisions. The scope is the constitutional architecture itself, not any of the twelve operational domains.

**Step 2 — A0 §4.3 (Authority Relationship Graph instantiation for RT-16):**
> "RT-16 holds: Amendment Initiation Authority — the exclusive authority to initiate constitutional modification"

A0 §4.3 instantiates RT-16's AIR-3 as "Amendment Initiation Authority." This is exclusive: no other runtime holds this authority. The A0 §4.3 authority graph entry for RT-16 is distinct from all other runtime authority entries.

**Step 3 — A1 §5.1 (Authority Distribution Table assignment):**
The A1 §5.1 Authority Distribution Table RT-16 row reads:
| RT-16 | — | — | Amendment | — | — |

RT-16 holds AIR-3 in the Amendment domain. All other authority type columns (AIR-1 Observation, AIR-2 Interpretation, AIR-4 Projection, AIR-5 Audit) are absent (—) for RT-16.

**Authority Types NOT Held by RT-16:**
- AIR-1 (Observation Authority) — not held; RT-08 holds Observation authority
- AIR-2 (Interpretation Authority) — not held; RT-09 holds Interpretation authority
- AIR-4 (Projection Authority) — not held; RT-13 holds Projection authority
- AIR-5 (Audit Authority) — not held; RT-04 exclusively holds AIR-5 (constitutionally independent)

**Domain Scope Note:**
RT-16's AIR-3 is constitutional/civilizational scope — it operates on the constitutional architecture itself (D-series and A-series documents). It is not domain-scoped in the operational sense of D6 §4.4. The AIR-3 conflicts among RT-03 (Gate/Admit authority, operation-level), RT-11 (Civilizational Decision authority, strategy-level), and RT-16 (Amendment authority, constitutional-level) are resolved by scope: these scopes are mutually exclusive (A1-v1.2 §5.2).

**D6 §4.7 Authority Integrity Rules (addressed separately):**
D6 §4.7 defines AIR-1 through AIR-5 as Authority Integrity Rules — a distinct system from the authority type definitions in §4.2 through §4.6. The AIR-1 through AIR-5 in §4.7 are rules about how authority must be structured (separation, no unauthorized projection, no knowledge monopolization, no hidden decisions, audit independence). These are not conflated with the authority type designations in §4.2 through §4.6.

RT-16's compliance with D6 §4.7 Authority Integrity Rules:
- AIR-1 (Authority Separation): RT-16's Amendment authority is entirely separate from RT-04's Audit authority, RT-11's Deliberation authority, and RT-03's Gate/Enforce authority. No authority collapse.
- AIR-2 (No Unauthorized Projection): RT-16 does not hold Projection Authority and does not execute Action Projections. All constitutional text updates are committed through RT-03 (PAIR 61), not projected directly.
- AIR-3 (No Knowledge Monopolization): Not applicable to RT-16 — RT-16 holds Amendment authority, not Observation or Interpretation authority.
- AIR-4 (No Hidden Decisions): All RT-16 amendment decisions produce explicit records (RatifiedAmendmentRecord or AmendmentRejectionRecord) committed through RT-03 to RT-05. No hidden amendment decisions.
- AIR-5 (Audit Independence): RT-04 holds AIR-5 independently and observes RT-16 under universal audit authority (Rule R1). RT-16 does not hold AIR-5 and does not audit itself.

**The "Amendment Initiation Authority" in A0 §4.3 is RT-16's instantiation of AIR-3. No other runtime holds this authority.**

## RS-07 — Owned Objects

**Verbatim source: [A0-v1.1.1-canonical.md] §3.17 Owned Constitutional Objects. Object count: 4.**

**1. AmendmentProposal**
The constitutional object representing a proposed change to the constitutional architecture. An AmendmentProposal must satisfy AP-1 through AP-6 (D7 §12.3) to be eligible for deliberation. RT-16 receives AmendmentProposals from RT-11 (PAIR 59) and takes ownership upon receipt. Defined in D7 §12.3 (Amendment Proposal Requirements). RT-16 owns the AmendmentProposal throughout the amendment process — from receipt through terminal state (RATIFIED or REJECTED or CLASS IV REJECTED).

**2. AmendmentRegistry**
The constitutional record of all Amendment Proposals in all states. The AmendmentRegistry maintains the complete history and current status of every AmendmentProposal ever received by RT-16. States maintained: proposed, under deliberation, preservation audited, ratified, rejected (and by extension Class IV-rejected — see RS-10 for full state enumeration). RT16-INV-5 requires that Amendment Proposals are never silently dropped; the AmendmentRegistry is the constitutional instrument of that invariant. Defined through the operational requirements of D7 §12.4 (six-stage review process) and A0 §3.17 Responsibility 8.

**3. RatifiedAmendmentRecord**
The constitutional record produced when an AmendmentProposal has completed all required stages (RT16-INV-1 through RT16-INV-3 satisfied) and has been formally ratified. The RatifiedAmendmentRecord contains the Deliberation Record from RT-11, the PreservationAuditRecord from RT-04, the founding-level authorization record, and the ratified constitutional text. It is committed to RT-05 through RT-03 (PAIR 61) and persisted in RT-07. Defined through D7 §12.4 Stage 5 (Publication) requirements.

**4. AmendmentRejectionRecord**
The constitutional record produced when an AmendmentProposal is rejected — whether for non-compliance with AP-1 through AP-6, failure to obtain founding-level authorization, RT-04 Preservation Audit HALT, or Class IV immediate rejection. The AmendmentRejectionRecord includes the basis for rejection and the stage at which rejection occurred. It is committed to RT-05 through RT-03 and persisted in RT-07. RT16-INV-5 requires that rejected proposals receive an AmendmentRejectionRecord rather than silent disposal. Defined through D7 §12.1 (Class IV inadmissibility) and the operational requirements of D7 §12.4.

**Objects NOT Owned by RT-16:**
- "Modified constitutional stack" — this is a produced constitutional output (A0 §3.17 Produced Objects), not an object RT-16 owns. It belongs in RS-09.
- DeliberationRecord — owned by RT-11; RT-16 consumes it (RS-08)
- PreservationAuditRecord — owned by RT-04; RT-16 consumes it (RS-08)
- CivilizationalDecisionProposal — owned by RT-11
- CivilizationalDecision — owned by RT-12
- DomainProfile — owned by RT-15

## RS-08 — Received Objects / Consumed Objects

**1. AmendmentProposal (from RT-11 via PAIR 59)**
RT-16 receives an AmendmentProposal from RT-11 through the constitutional trigger interaction (PAIR 59). Receipt of the AmendmentProposal is the activation event for RT-16's amendment process. Upon receipt, RT-16 takes ownership of the AmendmentProposal (RS-07). Constitutional basis: A0 §3.17 Responsibility 1; A1 §3.6 PAIR 59; D7 §6.1.

**2. DeliberationRecord (from RT-11 via PAIR 59 / D7 §12.4 Stage 2)**
RT-16 receives the DeliberationRecord from RT-11 upon completion of the deliberation phase. The DeliberationRecord is a mandatory precondition for ratification (RT16-INV-1). RT-16 consumes but does not own the DeliberationRecord. Constitutional basis: A0 §3.17 Responsibility 4; RT16-INV-1; D7 §12.4 Stage 2.

**3. PreservationAuditRecord (from RT-04 via PAIR 60, BLOCKING)**
RT-16 receives the PreservationAuditRecord from RT-04 after requesting the Preservation Audit. Receipt may be PASS (amendment may proceed) or HALT (amendment process terminates). The PreservationAuditRecord is a mandatory precondition for ratification (RT16-INV-2). Constitutional basis: A0 §3.17 Responsibility 5; RT16-INV-2; A1 §3.6 PAIR 60; D7 §12.4 Stage 3.

**4. Gate Processing Results (from RT-03 via PAIR 61)**
RT-16 receives gate processing results from RT-03 for all Class A amendment operations. This covers the six-gate evaluation of amendment commits. Constitutional basis: A0 §3.17 Responsibility 1 (via RT-03); A1 §3.6 PAIR 61; D4 §2.1 KMP.

**5. Founding-Level Authorization (from Human Governance Actors)**
RT-16 receives founding-level authorization from human governance actors holding D-2-level authority. Per A1 §12.8 Step 9, this authorization is submitted externally through RT-08 → RT-03 as a Class A operation; RT-16 receives the gate processing results confirming the authorized amendment. Human governance actors are constitutional actors, not runtimes; they are not listed in RS-26 (Dependencies). Constitutional basis: A0 §3.17 Responsibility 6; RT16-INV-3; D7 §12.2; D7 §12.4 Stage 4.

**6. Domain Deliberation Participation Input (from RT-15 instances, conditional)**
During the deliberation phase (D7 §12.4 Stage 2), RT-15 domain instances may provide deliberation participation input relevant to their domains. This is conditional and is grounded in A0 §4.1 (dependency graph shows RT-15 → RT-16 for domain deliberation participation). This is a participation input, not an owned object. Constitutional basis: A0 §4.1; A0 §3.16 Responsibility 13; R15-v1.0-canonical.md RS-32.5.

**7. Historical Amendment Records (from RT-07, query)**
RT-16 queries RT-07 for historical amendment records — prior ratified amendments and prior rejections — to inform the amendment classification and process. Constitutional basis: A0 §3.17 Dependencies (RT-07); A1 §13.2 RT-16 row.

## RS-09 — Produced Objects / Outputs

**1. RatifiedAmendmentRecord**
Produced when an AmendmentProposal successfully completes all required stages and receives founding-level authorization. Contains the Deliberation Record (RT-11), PreservationAuditRecord (RT-04), authorization record, and ratified constitutional text. Committed to RT-05 through RT-03 (PAIR 61) and persisted through RT-07. Constitutional basis: A0 §3.17 Produced Objects; D7 §12.4 Stage 5.

**2. AmendmentRejectionRecord**
Produced when an AmendmentProposal is rejected for any reason: AP non-compliance, Preservation Audit HALT, authorization failure, or Class IV immediate rejection. Includes the rejection basis and the stage at which rejection occurred. Committed to RT-05 through RT-03 and persisted through RT-07. RT16-INV-5 requires that no proposal is silently dropped. Constitutional basis: A0 §3.17 Produced Objects; RT16-INV-5; D7 §12.1.

**3. Modified Constitutional Stack**
The ultimate constitutional output of a successful amendment cycle: the D-series or A-series documents as modified by the ratified amendment. This is not an object RT-16 "owns" in the same sense as the AmendmentRegistry; it is the effect of a ratified amendment on the constitutional architecture. Constitutional text updates are committed through RT-03 → RT-05 (PAIR 61). Constitutional basis: A0 §3.17 Produced Objects; D7 §12.4 Stage 5.

**4. KernelOperationManifest Update (conditional)**
When a ratified amendment modifies Class B operations, RT-16 updates the KernelOperationManifest through RT-03. This is conditional on the amendment's scope affecting Class B operations. Constitutional basis: A0 §3.17 Responsibility 9; D4-v2.0-canonical.md Class B operation definition.

**5. Amendment Outcome Notification to RT-11**
RT-16 returns the amendment outcome (RATIFIED or REJECTED) to RT-11 (PAIR 59, non-blocking) for integration into RT-11's future deliberation context. Constitutional basis: A1 §3.6 PAIR 59; A1 §12.8 Step 14.

## RS-10 — State Variables

**1. Amendment Registry State**
The Amendment Registry tracks all AmendmentProposals and their current states. The six proposal states (derived from D7 §12.4 six-stage review process and A1 §12.8 fifteen-step amendment execution order) are:

| State | Description | Constitutional Basis |
|-------|-------------|---------------------|
| DORMANT | No active amendment process; Registry maintained | A0 §3.17 Note on Lifecycle |
| PROPOSAL RECEIVED | AmendmentProposal received from RT-11; classification in progress | A1 §12.8 Steps 2–3 |
| AP VERIFICATION | Proposal classified (non-Class IV); AP-1 through AP-6 verification in progress | RT16-INV-4; D7 §12.3 |
| DELIBERATION | AP verification passed; proposal in RT-11 deliberation process | D7 §12.4 Stage 2; A1 §12.8 Steps 3–4 |
| PRESERVATION AUDIT | Deliberation complete; RT-04 Preservation Audit in progress | D7 §12.4 Stage 3; A1 §12.8 Steps 4–6 |
| AWAITING AUTHORIZATION | Preservation Audit passed (PASS); awaiting founding-level human authorization | D7 §12.4 Stage 4; A1 §12.8 Steps 8–10 |
| RATIFIED | Authorization received and validated; RatifiedAmendmentRecord produced | D7 §12.4 Stage 5; A1 §12.8 Steps 11–12 |
| REJECTED | Proposal rejected at any stage (AP failure, HALT, authorization refused) | D7 §12.4; RT16-INV-5 |
| CLASS IV REJECTED | Class IV proposal immediately rejected; no deliberation opened | RT16-INV-6; D7 §12.1 |

**2. Active Amendment Proposal Tracking**
RT-16 tracks the identity and current state of all active AmendmentProposals. Per RT16-INV-5, no proposal may be silently dropped. The Amendment Registry is the constitutional instrument of this tracking obligation.

**3. Amendment Class Classification State**
For each received proposal, RT-16 maintains the classification state indicating which of the four constitutional amendment classes (Class I through Class IV, per D7 §12.5) has been assigned. Classification determines the subsequent process path (immediate rejection for Class IV; AP verification for all others).

**4. AP-1 through AP-6 Verification State**
For each non-Class IV proposal, RT-16 maintains the verification state of all six AP requirements. All six must be satisfied before deliberation may be entered (RT16-INV-4). The verification state records which requirements have been confirmed and which have not.

**5. Preservation Audit Status**
When a Preservation Audit has been requested from RT-04 (PAIR 60), RT-16 maintains the audit status: REQUESTED, PASS, or HALT. The amendment process is BLOCKED until RT-04 returns the PreservationAuditRecord.

**Note:** All state variables are logical constitutional characterizations. No implementation data types, storage mechanisms, or programming language terms are specified here.

## RS-11 — State Transitions

| From State | To State | Trigger | Constitutional Basis |

This section implements the full amendment process as defined in D7 Part 12 (§12.1 through §12.6) and A1 §12.8. All three Phase 0 conflicts are disclosed herein.

### 12.1 Amendment Classes (D7 §12.5)

**Class I — Operational Amendment:**
Modifications to specific requirements within a document that do not alter its foundational principles. Requires standard amendment process (all six AP requirements, all six D7 §12.4 stages, founding-level authorization).
Source: D7-v1.0-canonical.md §12.5.

**Class II — Structural Amendment:**
Modifications to a document's fundamental architecture — adding or removing Parts, redefining core concepts, restructuring authority. Requires extended deliberation and elevated authorization.
Source: D7-v1.0-canonical.md §12.5.

**Class III — Foundational Amendment:**
Modifications to D1, D0, or D-1. Requires the highest amendment authority and the most comprehensive Preservation Audit.
Source: D7-v1.0-canonical.md §12.5.

**Class IV — Terminal Modification:**
Any change that would affect D-2's terminal commitments. Class IV amendments are constitutionally inadmissible under the Constitutional Continuity Principle (D7 §12.1). RT-16 must classify such proposals as Class IV and route them to immediate constitutional rejection. No deliberation process is opened for Class IV proposals. RT-16 issues an AmendmentRejectionRecord citing D7 §12.1. This path is governed by RT16-INV-6.
Source: D7-v1.0-canonical.md §12.5; D7 §12.1; RT16-INV-6.

**Classification Obligation:** RT-16 must classify every received AmendmentProposal into one of these four classes before any further processing. Classification is the first operation in Step 3 of A1 §12.8. Class IV classification is final and triggers immediate rejection with no further deliberation.

### 12.2 AP-1 through AP-6 Requirements (D7 §12.3)

All six AP requirements must be satisfied before any non-Class IV proposal enters the deliberation phase (RT16-INV-4). These requirements are verified by RT-16 as part of Step 3 (Amendment Process Initiated) in A1 §12.8.

**AP-1 — Identification:**
What document(s) are being amended, what specific provisions, and what version.
Source: D7-v1.0-canonical.md §12.3.

**AP-2 — Justification:**
Why the amendment is constitutionally necessary — what inadequacy, discovered gap, or changed circumstance requires constitutional change; why it cannot be addressed within existing constitutional authority.
Source: D7-v1.0-canonical.md §12.3.

**AP-3 — Preservation Assessment:**
For each D-2 terminal commitment, an explicit assessment of whether the proposed amendment affects it. Any amendment declared to affect a terminal commitment is constitutionally inadmissible unless the effect is a strengthening.
Source: D7-v1.0-canonical.md §12.3.

**AP-4 — Precedence Analysis:**
How the proposed amendment relates to all other documents in the constitutional stack.
Source: D7-v1.0-canonical.md §12.3.

**AP-5 — Implementation Pathway:**
How existing operations relying on amended provisions will transition to amended requirements.
Source: D7-v1.0-canonical.md §12.3.

**AP-6 — Reversibility Assessment:**
Whether the proposed amendment is reversible and, if not, an explicit acknowledgment of its irreversibility and the basis for the heightened confidence required.
Source: D7-v1.0-canonical.md §12.3.

**AP Verification Failure:** If any AP requirement (AP-1 through AP-6) is not satisfied, RT-16 rejects the proposal with a full AmendmentRejectionRecord through RT-03 (A0 §3.17 Responsibility 10). No deliberation is initiated for a proposal that fails AP verification.

**AP Verification Timing:** Per RT16-INV-4 and NB-1 (Specification Baseline), AP verification occurs within RT-16's Step 3 (Amendment Process Initiated), after classification and before deliberation is requested. This ordering is mandated by RT16-INV-4.

### 12.3 Six-Stage Amendment Review Process (D7 §12.4)

RT-16 implements all six D7 §12.4 stages as constitutional obligations. Each stage corresponds to steps in A1 §12.8.

**Stage 1 — Proposal Submission (A1 §12.8 Steps 1–3):**
An AmendmentProposal is submitted by a constitutionally recognized proposer. Any actor with Domain Decision Authority in an affected domain, or any founding operator, may be a proposer. The constitutional trigger for RT-16 activation is RT-11's delivery of the AmendmentProposal (A1 §12.8 Step 2). RT-16 initiates the Amendment Process (Step 3), classifies the proposal, and verifies AP-1 through AP-6.

**Stage 2 — Deliberation (A1 §12.8 Steps 3–4, partially):**
The amendment is deliberated through the D7 Deliberation Architecture. RT-16 routes the proposal to RT-11 for constitutionally governed deliberation (A0 §3.17 Responsibility 4). Deliberation participants must include domain representatives for all affected domains, DOM-000001 authority, Audit Authority, Theory of Change assessment, and the original authority that ratified the document being amended (D7 §12.4 Stage 2). RT-16 receives the DeliberationRecord upon deliberation completion (RT16-INV-1).

**Stage 3 — Preservation Audit (A1 §12.8 Steps 4–7):**
RT-16 requests the Constitutional Preservation Audit from RT-04 (PAIR 60, BLOCKING). RT-04 independently assesses whether the amendment violates any provision of a higher-authority document. RT-16 receives the PreservationAuditRecord (PASS or HALT). If HALT: amendment process terminates; RT-11 is notified; no further action. RT-16 issues an AmendmentRejectionRecord. If PASS: proceed to Stage 4.

**Stage 4 — Approval (A1 §12.8 Steps 8–10):**
Founding-level authorization is required from founding operators with D-2-level authority (D7 §12.4 Stage 4). Authorization must be explicit and registered — implicit approval is not constitutionally valid. Human authorization is submitted through RT-08 → RT-03 as a Class A operation (A1 §12.8 Step 9). RT-03 applies all six gates to the Amendment Authorization (A1 §12.8 Step 10). This is the implementation of RT16-INV-3.

**Stage 5 — Publication (A1 §12.8 Steps 11–12):**
Upon authorization, RT-03 Stages 8+9 commit the amendment to RT-05 — constitutional text is updated (A1 §12.8 Step 11). RT-16 records ratification in the AmendmentRegistry and produces the RatifiedAmendmentRecord (Step 12). The amended document is published as a new canonical version, with explicit notation of what changed, the Deliberation Record, and the authorization record. RT-04 records the complete amendment audit trail (Step 13).

**Stage 6 — Transition (A1 §12.8 Steps 14–15):**
RT-11 receives the amendment outcome for deliberation context integration (Step 14). All affected runtimes receive amendment notification through RT-03 Stage 10 propagation (Step 15). Domains and operations affected by the amendment transition according to the Implementation Pathway (AP-5). RT-16's constitutional responsibility (Responsibility 7) is to publish the amendment; ongoing transition monitoring per AP-5 is an obligation that extends beyond RT-16's explicit responsibilities in A0 §3.17 (see RS-35 and NB-5 from the Specification Baseline).

### 12.4 Class IV Immediate Rejection Process

**Constitutional Basis:** RT16-INV-6; D7 §12.1 Constitutional Continuity Principle; A0 §3.17 Responsibility 11.

Class IV proposals (Terminal Modification — any proposed change that would affect D-2's terminal commitments) are constitutionally inadmissible. This is not a standard rejection path. The Class IV rejection process differs from all other rejection paths in the following ways:

1. **No AP check performed.** The Class IV classification is itself the constitutional ground for immediate rejection. AP-1 through AP-6 are not verified.
2. **No deliberation opened.** RT-16 does not route a Class IV proposal to RT-11 for deliberation. To open deliberation on a Class IV proposal would itself be a constitutional violation of the Constitutional Continuity Principle (D7 §12.1).
3. **Immediate AmendmentRejectionRecord.** RT-16 immediately issues an AmendmentRejectionRecord citing D7 §12.1 Constitutional Continuity Principle as the rejection basis.
4. **Record committed through RT-03.** The AmendmentRejectionRecord is committed to RT-05 through RT-03 per KMP (D4 §2.1). This is a Class A operation.
5. **RT-11 notified.** RT-11 receives notification that the proposal was Class IV-rejected (non-blocking).
6. **RT-16 returns to DORMANT.** After the AmendmentRejectionRecord is produced and committed, RT-16 returns to the DORMANT state.

The Class IV immediate rejection path is constitutionally mandatory. RT-16 may not bypass it (RT16-INV-6).

---

### 12.5 Preservation Audit — Special Gate Authority (D7 §12.4 Stage 3; A1 PAIR 60)

The Preservation Audit is a mandatory constitutional precondition for amendment ratification (RT16-INV-2). It is performed by RT-04 independently of RT-16's own assessment.

**Procedure:**
- RT-16 requests the Preservation Audit from RT-04 (PAIR 60, BLOCKING — amendment cannot proceed without completion)
- RT-04 performs an independent constitutional audit assessing whether the amendment violates any provision of a higher-authority document
- RT-04 returns a PreservationAuditRecord to RT-16: PASS or HALT
- If HALT: RT-16 terminates the amendment process, notifies RT-11, and issues an AmendmentRejectionRecord
- If PASS: RT-16 proceeds to Stage 4 (Approval)

This is the only case where RT-04 produces output that directly gates another runtime's operation (A1 §3.6 PAIR 60). RT-04 normally produces observational output for human governance actors, not gate outputs for other runtimes. The Preservation Audit exception is constitutionally necessary: an amendment that would destroy the constitution would not survive this audit.

RT-16 may not waive the Preservation Audit (RT16-INV-2) and may not override RT-04's HALT determination.

### 12.6 AP-5 Implementation Pathway Obligations

AP-5 (Implementation Pathway) is one of the six mandatory AP requirements. RT-16 verifies during AP verification (Stage 1 / Step 3) that the AmendmentProposal includes an Implementation Pathway describing how existing operations relying on amended provisions will transition to amended requirements.

RT-16's constitutional obligation with respect to AP-5:
- Verify that the AmendmentProposal includes an explicit Implementation Pathway before deliberation is entered
- Publish the Implementation Pathway as part of the RatifiedAmendmentRecord

Ongoing transition monitoring per AP-5 (D7 §12.4 Stage 6) is an obligation that extends beyond RT-16's explicit constitutional responsibilities in A0 §3.17. RT-16 publishes the amendment; transition monitoring may be distributed to affected runtimes and RT-04's audit function. This ambiguity is disclosed rather than resolved: see NB-5 in R16-WRITING-READINESS-REPORT.md.

RT-16 does not participate in any A1 §11.1 authorized recursive structures. The amendment process is a single-pass, event-driven cycle without authorized recursion.
