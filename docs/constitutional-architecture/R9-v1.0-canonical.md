## PART 3 — CORE SPECIFICATION

### RS-03 — Purpose

**Constitutional Purpose Statement (A0-v1.1.1 §3.10 verbatim):** "To form Evidence Objects from Observation Records through registered interpretation protocols, maintain Knowledge States for all twelve domains, manage the Contradiction Register and Reality Gap Register, and track the temporal validity of all epistemic objects — serving as the constitutional nexus of epistemic product formation and knowledge integrity."

**Uniqueness Argument:** RT-09 is the only runtime constitutionally authorized to hold both authority type AIR-1 in the Evidence domain and authority type AIR-2 for the Evidence→Knowledge transformation (A0 §4.3; A1 §5.1). No other runtime owns Evidence Records, Knowledge Records, ContradictionRecords, RealityGapEntries, or KnowledgeStates (D8 §4.1 canonical types). No other runtime maintains the Contradiction Register or Reality Gap Register. The epistemic chain stage sequence (D3 Stages 2–5) is exclusively RT-09's constitutional domain. A0 §2.2 survivorship test: removing RT-09 leaves the epistemic chain broken at Stage 2 — no constitutional actor would exist to convert admitted Observation Projections into epistemic products.

**Constitutional Gap Statement:** If RT-09 were removed: (a) D4 Gate 4 (Epistemic Validation) could not be served — no runtime would supply EpistemicChainState to RT-03; (b) D8 §9.4 (Knowledge Layer, mandatory MVCS layer) would be absent — the constitutional minimum for operational validity would not be satisfied; (c) D3 Epistemic Chain Stages 2–5 would have no constitutional realization; (d) the Contradiction Register and Reality Gap Register (mandated by A0 §3.10 R6 and R7) would have no constitutional owner; (e) RT-10 could not form Domain Understanding Models — it depends on RT-09 Knowledge Records (PAIR 31).

### RS-06 — Authority

**RS-06.1 Authority Types Held**

RT-09 holds exactly two authority types, both instantiated through A0 §4.3 and confirmed by A1 §5.1.

**NOTE ON AIR NOMENCLATURE (lesson applied from R8 FIND-001):** D6 uses "AIR-N" in two distinct senses. D6 §4.2–4.3 uses "AIR-N" to name authority **types** (five categories of authority). D6 §4.7 uses "AIR-N" to name **Authority Integrity Rules** (structural governance rules). These are different content sharing the same notation. In this specification: "authority type AIR-N" always refers to D6 §4.2–4.3 types; "D6 §4.7 Authority Integrity Rule AIR-N (Rule Name)" always refers to D6 §4.7 rules. The two are never conflated.

**Authority type AIR-1 (Observation Authority):**
- Domain: Evidence domain — the constitutional right to receive and assess Observation Projections admitted through RT-03 for epistemic processing
- D6 source: D6 §4.2 (authority type definition: "the constitutional right to initiate Observation Projections within a domain and to register their results as constitutionally valid Observation Records in that domain's knowledge base")
- A0 instantiation: A0 §4.3 authority relationship graph — RT-09 holds AIR-1 in the Evidence domain, granted by RT-02
- A1 confirmation: A1 §5.1 authority type distribution table — RT-09 row: Evidence domain | AIR-1
- Exercise conditions: RT-09 exercises authority type AIR-1 when receiving an admitted Observation Projection from RT-08 through the RT-03 Kernel Mediation Principle (KMP). The Observation Projection must have been admitted through RT-03 before RT-09 exercises authority type AIR-1 upon it (CC-5; A1 PAIR 29 P7)
- Scope limitation: Authority type AIR-1 does not authorize RT-09 to initiate Observation Projections outward — RT-09 holds AIR-1 for inbound epistemic processing only

**Authority type AIR-2 (Interpretation Authority):**
- Domain: Evidence→Knowledge transformation — the constitutional right to apply registered interpretation protocols to form Evidence Records, InterpretationRecords, BeliefObjects, and Knowledge Records
- D6 source: D6 §4.3 (authority type definition: "the constitutional right to apply registered interpretation protocols to Observation Records within a domain, transforming them into Evidence (D5 Part 3, Stage 4)")
- A0 instantiation: A0 §4.3 authority relationship graph — RT-09 holds AIR-2 for Evidence→Knowledge transformation, granted by RT-02
- A1 confirmation: A1 §5.1 authority type distribution table — RT-09 row: Evidence→Knowledge | AIR-2
- Exercise conditions: RT-09 exercises authority type AIR-2 when applying registered, versioned interpretation protocols to admitted Observation Projections and subsequent epistemic stages. All Class A operations exercising AIR-2 are subject to RT-03 KMP (D4 §2.1)
- D6 §4.3 obligations of AIR-2 holders: (a) apply only registered, versioned interpretation protocols; (b) record which protocol version was applied; (c) not distort Evidence in the direction of expected results; (d) escalate interpretive disagreements rather than resolving unilaterally
- D6 §4.3 prohibitions on AIR-2 holders: (a) may not create new interpretation protocols without constitutional registration; (b) may not suppress interpretive uncertainty
- Scope limitation: RT-09 holds AIR-2 exclusively for the Evidence→Knowledge transformation. RT-10 holds AIR-2 for Domain Understanding (a different domain). RT-09's AIR-2 does not extend to Domain Understanding formation.

### RS-10 — Managed Objects

**RS-10.1 Evidence Record (RT09-OBJ-01)**
- D8 §4.1 canonical type: Evidence Record
- D0/D1 category basis: Epistemic Object category (D0); characterized in D1 as an object with source, confidence level, temporal timestamp, and uncertainty attributes
- D2 six-layer structure compliance: Evidence Records satisfy all six layers — (1) Identity: unique Evidence Record ID; (2) Temporal: timestamp of formation + temporal validity metadata per RT09-INV-2; (3) Provenance: chain anchor to Observation Projection ID; (4) Epistemic: confidence and uncertainty attributes preserved from Observation Record per KI-017; (5) Domain: domain classification per RT-15 coordination; (6) Behavioral: governed by D5 §3.2 Stage 4 immutability
- Lifecycle states (D1): FORMING → SUBMITTED (Class A to RT-03) → ADMITTED (committed to RT-05) → HISTORICAL (superseded but not deleted) | REJECTED (RT-03 Gate failure)
- Constitutional validity conditions: (a) provenance chain intact to Observation Projection ID; (b) uncertainty attributes present; (c) registered interpretation protocol version recorded; (d) temporal validity metadata set; (e) not formed from an Observation Projection that was not admitted through RT-03
- Forbidden configurations: Evidence Record without Observation Projection provenance anchor (RT09-INV-1 violation); Evidence Record with suppressed uncertainty (D6 §4.3 AIR-2 prohibition)
- Provenance chain role: First internal epistemic anchor — Evidence Record is the anchor from which all subsequent epistemic objects (InterpretationRecord, BeliefObject, Knowledge Record) derive their provenance
- Classification: Owned (created by RT-09); Constitutional State (committed to RT-05 via RT-03)

**RS-10.2 InterpretationRecord (RT09-OBJ-02)**

- Validity conditions: anchored to Evidence Record; registered inference protocol applied; epistemic confidence inherited from Evidence Record
- Classification: Owned; Constitutional State

**RS-10.3 BeliefObject (RT09-OBJ-03)**
- D8 §4.1 derivation: internal epistemic stage object; derived from D3 Epistemic Chain Stage 4
- D2 compliance: epistemic confidence attributes; temporal validity; provenance to InterpretationRecord
- Lifecycle states: FORMING → SUBMITTED → ADMITTED → HISTORICAL | REJECTED
- Validity conditions: anchored to InterpretationRecord; confidence attributes present per D5 §3.4; not entered into fabric as Knowledge Record without proper justification (RT09-INV-5)

**RS-10.4 Knowledge Record (RT09-OBJ-04)**

- D2 six-layer compliance: all six layers satisfied, including (3) Provenance chain from BeliefObject→InterpretationRecord→Evidence Record→Observation Projection; (4) Epistemic: justification and validation attributes; D3 EP-T4 Validation Gate satisfied

### RS-12 — Internal Processes

**RT09-PROC-01: Evidence Formation Process**
- Process ID: RT09-PROC-01
- Classification: Transformation Process
- Constitutional trigger: Receipt of admitted Observation Projection (RT09-IN-01); CC-5 satisfied (observation admitted before processing)
- Constitutional inputs: RT09-IN-01 (Observation Projection), RT09-IN-03 (current Reality Fabric state for contextualization), RT09-STATE-07 (EpistemicProtocolRegistry — select protocol)
- Constitutional outputs: RT09-OUT-01 (Evidence Record); update to RT09-STATE-01 (queue); initiation of RT09-PROC-02
- Obligation fulfilled: O9-01
- D-series mandate: D5 §3.2 Stage 4 (Evidence Assessment); D3 Epistemic Chain Stage 2; KI-017 (uncertainty attributes preserved); KI-024 (confidence levels respected)
- Sequence: (1) Select registered interpretation protocol from RT09-STATE-07; (2) Apply protocol to Observation Projection; (3) Preserve uncertainty attributes from source Observation Record; (4) Form Evidence Record with provenance anchor to Observation Projection ID + RT-09 operation ID; (5) Set temporal validity metadata (RT09-INV-2); (6) Submit Evidence Record as Class A to RT-03; (7) On admission, update RT09-STATE-08 (EpistemicChainStateCache); (8) Trigger RT09-PROC-04 (Contradiction Check)

**RT09-PROC-02: Epistemic Chain Advancement Process**
- Process ID: RT09-PROC-02
- Classification: Transformation Process
- Constitutional trigger: Successful commitment of Evidence Record (RT09-OUT-01 delivery postcondition met); triggered sequentially per KI-007 (no stage skipping)
- Constitutional inputs: RT09-STATE-02 (EpistemicChainInProgress — Evidence Record), RT09-STATE-07 (registered inference protocols for Interpretation stage)
- Constitutional outputs: InterpretationRecord, BeliefObject, then Knowledge Record (RT09-OUT-02); update RT09-STATE-03 (KnowledgeStateRegistry)
- Obligations fulfilled: O9-02, O9-03, O9-04
- D-series mandate: D3 Epistemic Chain Stages 3–5; D3 EP-T4 (Validation Gate before Knowledge Record); KI-007 (sequential, no skipping, no backward); RT09-INV-5 (no assumption-to-fact conversion — BeliefObject must have justification before KnowledgeClaim formation)
- Sequence: (1) Apply registered inference protocol to Evidence Record → InterpretationRecord; (2) Assign epistemic confidence → BeliefObject; (3) Apply D3 EP-T4 Validation Gate; (4) Add justification and validation attributes → KnowledgeClaim (Knowledge Record); (5) Submit Knowledge Record as Class A to RT-03; (6) On admission, trigger RT09-PROC-03 (KnowledgeState Update)

- DKS transition logic: (a) if no contradiction: new Knowledge Record → DKS-1; (b) if evidence insufficient: → DKS-2; (c) if contradiction detected: → DKS-3 for affected domain, ContradictionRecord created (RT09-PROC-04); (d) if temporal validity expired: → DKS-4

### RS-23 — Audit Requirements

#### RS-23.1 RT-04 Audit Scope

RT-04 (Constitutional Audit Runtime) holds authority type AIR-5 (Audit Authority) over all RT-09 operations under D6 §3.4 and A1 §14.1. RT-04 audits the following RT-09 operations:

3. Every KnowledgeState Update (RT09-PROC-03): DKS transition authority, RT09-INV-4 compliance, RT09-OUT-06 and RT09-OUT-07 delivery
4. Every Contradiction Detection operation (RT09-PROC-04): executed on every Evidence integration; ContradictionRecord creation when contradiction detected
5. Every Reality Gap Registration (RT09-PROC-05): triggered correctly per KI-023 conditions
6. Gate 4 EpistemicChainState provision (RT09-PROC-07): EpistemicChainState accuracy; Gate 4 conditions verified
7. All KnowledgeState transmissions to RT-10 (RT09-OUT-06) and RT-15 (RT09-OUT-07)
8. All ContradictionRecord (RT09-OUT-04) and RealityGapEntry (RT09-OUT-05) submissions
9. All lifecycle state transitions (RT09-STATE-09)
10. All suspension events (RT09-LC-06) and resumption events (RT09-TRANS-09 through RT09-TRANS-11)
11. Rollback compliance (RC-1): RT-09 never initiates rollback; RT-09 correctly reverts on RT-03 directive

#### RS-23.2 Observable Operations

Every RT09-PROC-NN internal process is observable to RT-04. RT-09 is constitutionally obligated to make the following observable per D6 §3.4:

- RT09-PROC-01 through RT09-PROC-08: process initiation, inputs consumed, outputs produced, completion or failure
- Every Class A submission to RT-03: object type, operation ID, provenance chain
- Every Class B Kernel Manifest output: ContradictionRecord, RealityGapEntry
- Every Gate 4 query response: EpistemicChainState content delivered
- Every DKS transition: from-state, to-state, triggering Knowledge Record, domain

#### RS-23.3 Observable State

RT-04 may inspect all RT-09 state variables at any time:

- RT09-STATE-03 (KnowledgeStateRegistry): all twelve domain DKS classifications
- RT09-STATE-04 (ContradictionRegister): all ContradictionRecord entries
- RT09-STATE-05 (RealityGapRegister): all RealityGapEntry entries
- RT09-STATE-06 (TemporalValidityTracker): all temporal validity metadata and flagged-expired sets

Audit Records for RT-09 operations follow the format: RT04-AUD-RT09-NN where NN is a sequential identifier per audit cycle. An audit trail for one RT-09 operation cycle is complete when: (a) RT04-AUD-RT09-NN exists for every RT09-PROC-NN execution in that cycle; (b) every Class A submission has a corresponding audit record showing gate outcomes; (c) RT09-PROC-04 (Contradiction Detection) execution is documented regardless of whether a contradiction was found; (d) provenance chain verification for all objects produced is recorded; (e) DKS transition justification is documented.

#### RS-23.6 Audit Permanence

Audit Records for RT-09 are never rolled back, never deleted, and never modified after creation (A1 RC-3; A1 RC-4). **Application:** The epistemic chain stage sequence (Evidence→Interpretation→Belief→Knowledge Record per D3 KI-007) must be preserved as a temporal ordering in any implementation. Temporal validity metadata (RT09-INV-2) must be preserved on all epistemic objects. Causal ordering of Evidence Records and Knowledge Records must be preserved (KI-026).

**Specific translation obligations:** (a) Stage 2 (Evidence) must precede Stage 3 (Interpretation) which must precede Stage 4 (Belief) which must precede Stage 5 (KnowledgeClaim) — this is a hard constitutional ordering; (b) Temporal validity metadata must be queryable after object creation — it may not be ephemeral; (c) The temporal sequence of admitted Observation Projections must be reflected in the temporal ordering of resulting Evidence Records.

#### RS-33.5 TI-5 (Loop Integrity)

**Application:** RT-09's PRIMARY phase obligations (Evidence, Knowledge, Updated Understanding) must be preserved in any implementation. RT-09's absence from ABSENT phases (Observation, Deliberation, Decision, Action, Consequence, Observation of Consequence) must also be preserved — implementations must not allow RT-09 operations to occur in those phases.

**Specific translation obligations:** (a) The Updated Understanding phase (RT09-LC-05) must close the feedback loop — consequence observations must update Knowledge States; (b) RT-09's Understanding phase SUPPORTING role (RT09-LC-04) must be preserved — implementations may not prevent RT-10 from querying RT-09 for conflict resolution; (c) The Constitutional Loop must not short-circuit through RT-09 — Knowledge Records go to RT-10, not directly to RT-11.
