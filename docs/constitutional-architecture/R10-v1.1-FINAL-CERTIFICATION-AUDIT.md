**Finding:** All occurrences of "D-2 §XI" throughout R10-v1.0 should be "D-2 §VII." The CHANGELOG reports "33 occurrences corrected" and claims "Zero occurrences of 'D-2 §XI' remain in R10-v1.1-canonical.md."

This citation reads "§XI (Philosophy of Intelligence)" — the section number is the pre-correction value ("§XI"), and the section title "Philosophy of Intelligence" is correct (D-2 §VII is Philosophy of Intelligence). However, the section number "§XI" is incorrect — D-2 §XI is Philosophy of Agency.

D-2 §VII IS "Philosophy of Intelligence" (verified from D-2). D-2 §XI is "Philosophy of Agency." The note appears to have inverted the two sections while documenting the correction.

**Summary of RS-02 errors (two distinct errors in the correction machinery):**
1. The RS-02 §2.1 table still contains "§XI" (the uncorrected citation) instead of "§VII"
2. The RS-02 correction note states "D-2 §VII is 'Philosophy of Agency'" — this is factually false (D-2 §VII is Philosophy of Intelligence; D-2 §XI is Philosophy of Agency)

The CHANGELOG claim "Zero occurrences of 'D-2 §XI' remain in R10-v1.1-canonical.md" is therefore factually incorrect. The table entry in RS-02 §2.1 retains "§XI."

All other D-2 citations throughout the document (RS-04, RS-05, RS-07, RS-10, RS-12, RS-15, RS-18, RS-19, RS-20, RS-22, RS-24, RS-25, RS-26, RS-27, RS-34, RS-35, RS-36) correctly use "D-2 §VII." The error is isolated to the RS-02 §2.1 table and correction note.

**Resolution verdict for FAA-10-003:** PARTIALLY RESOLVED. The substantive correction (replacing §XI with §VII throughout the operational sections) has been applied. However, one uncorrected "§XI" instance remains in the RS-02 §2.1 table, and the RS-02 correction note contains a factually inverted statement about D-2 §VII. These are editorial/traceability errors. The four intelligence principles themselves are correct in substance and are now correctly cited as "D-2 §VII" throughout the operational sections.

**New finding derived from FAA-10-003 residual:** See FAA-10R-001 in Part 7.
