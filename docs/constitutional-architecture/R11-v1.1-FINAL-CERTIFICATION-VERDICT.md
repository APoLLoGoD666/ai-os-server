## Basis for Verdict

R11-v1.1-canonical.md achieves conditional certification under FAA-11R on the following basis:

**Grounds for certification:**

1. The five previously identified Class II deficiencies (FAA-11-002, FAA-11-003, FAA-11-004, FAA-11-008) have been fully remediated. RS-05 responsibilities are verbatim from A0 §3.12 (14 entries, all verbatim). RS-20 invariants are verbatim from A0 §3.12 (7 entries, all verbatim). RS-07 ownership is correctly built from A0 §3.12 with CivilizationalDecision properly excluded. RS-29 loop phase classification correctly assigns RT-11 as PRIMARY at Deliberation, Decision, and Updated Understanding per A1 §15.2.

2. No Class I deficiencies (critical constitutional defects) are present. RT-11's constitutional identity, role, authority, and boundary are correctly established. The constitutional stack is correctly identified. No unauthorized authority is claimed. No boundary violation is present.

3. The runtime correctly exercises constitutional identity: A0-v1.1.1 §3.12 is the constitutional seat; "Civilization Intelligence Runtime" is the canonical name; AIR-3 (Decision Authority) at civilizational scope is the sole held authority; all four A0 §3.12 dependencies are present; all 2 A0 §3.12 dependents are present; all 7 A1 PAIRs (32, 35, 39, 40, 41, 42, 59) are characterized; all constitutional conflicts (CON-01 through CON-04) are disclosed.

**Grounds for conditional (not unconditional) certification:**

One Class II deficiency remains (FAA-11R-006):

RS-26 contains 7 entries when A0 §3.12 lists exactly 4 dependencies. The three additional entries (RT-03, DOM-000001, D-7 Architecture) have no A0 §3.12 constitutional basis citation. The RS-26 section header and CERT-09 self-certification both falsely claim bijective correspondence with A0 §3.12. Per R0 CERT-09, bijective correspondence between RS-26 and A0 §3.12 is a mandatory certification criterion. This criterion is independently assessed as FAIL.

This finding supersedes the FAA-11-009 partial remediation. While RT-12 and RT-16 were correctly removed from RS-26, three new entries were added without constitutional authorization, creating a new but structurally equivalent Class II deficiency.

**Four Class III deficiencies and one Class IV deficiency are present and require correction in v1.2 but do not block certification.**
