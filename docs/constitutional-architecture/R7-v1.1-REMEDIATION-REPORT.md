### DEF-003 — RS-27 Dependents Include RT-08 and RT-14 Not in A0 §3.8 or A0 §4.1 (Class II)

**Status: FULLY IMPLEMENTED**

**Finding:** RT-08 and RT-14 were included in RS-27 alongside A0 §3.8/§4.1-enumerated dependents (RT-09, RT-10, RT-11, RT-04) without structural distinction. A0 §3.8 and A0 §4.1 list only four dependents. RT-08 and RT-14 are constitutionally grounded in A0 §3.9 and A0 §3.15 respectively, but not in A0 §3.8 or A0 §4.1.

**Resolution applied (Option B from DEFICIENCY-REGISTER.md):** RS-27 restructured into two subsections:

1. **Core Dependents (A0 v1.1 §3.8 / A0 §4.1):** RT-09, RT-10, RT-11, RT-04 — in exact bijection with A0 §3.8 line 780 and A0 §4.1
2. **Extended Dependents (A0 §3.x Cross-References — Pending A0 §4.1 Amendment):** RT-08 (from A0 §3.9) and RT-14 (from A0 §3.15 line 1101), with explicit note that CERT-04 full bijection for these is conditional on A0 §4.1 amendment

**Dependency relationships unchanged:** RT-08 and RT-14 remain in RS-27 as Extended Dependents. No dependency relationships were removed. The restructuring is a clarity change, not a constitutional change.

**Verification:** RS-27 contains both subsections with clear structural distinction.

### DEF-012 — ADR-1 Compliance Claim Lacks Clarity (Class III)

**Status: FULLY IMPLEMENTED**

**Finding:** RS-06 §6.4 ADR-1 compliance statement said only "SATISFIED — derivation chain is D-2 §XIII → ... → R7 v1.0." This did not explicitly state that RT-07 holds no AIR-N authority and that ADR-1's AIR-N derivation path is therefore not the applicable chain. A reader applying the R0 ADR-1 standard could find the statement ambiguous.

**Correction applied:** ADR-1 entry expanded to explicitly state:
- RT-07 holds no AIR-N authority
- ADR-1's mandatory AIR-N derivation path governs epistemic authority type assignments and is therefore not applicable to RT-07's non-AIR infrastructure mandate
- RT-07's constitutional mandate derives from the D-series mandate chain (an infrastructure mandate chain, not an AIR-N assignment chain)
- No authority is self-derived

**Verification:** RS-06 §6.4 ADR-1 entry contains the phrase "RT-07 holds no AIR-N authority."
