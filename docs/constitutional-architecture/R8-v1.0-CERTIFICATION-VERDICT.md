## BASIS FOR VERDICT

### Constitutional Identity: CONFIRMED

RT-08's constitutional identity is correctly and completely stated. "Observation Runtime" is the exact canonical name per A0-v1.1.1 §3.9. Tier 3 (Epistemic Chain) is the exact tier designation per A0-v1.1.1 §3.1. Constitutional seat A0-v1.1.1 §3.9 is correct. The A1 identity conflict (A1-v1.2 §3.0 designates "Observation Projection Runtime" at T2) is accurately disclosed in RS-01 and RS-13. The SEED-8 D4 disclaimer is accurate.

### Authorization Chain: VERIFIED

R7-v1.1-canonical.md CERT-10 explicitly authorizes RT-08: "RT-08 (Observation Runtime, A0 v1.1 §3.9) is AUTHORIZED to proceed to specification, certification, and canonicalization as R8-v1.0-canonical.md." R7-v1.1 was UNCONDITIONALLY CERTIFIED per R7-v1.1-FINAL-CERTIFICATION-VERDICT.md. The authorization is valid and in force.

### Constitutional Substance: SUBSTANTIALLY CORRECT

The overwhelming majority of R8's constitutional content is correct, grounded, and derivable from source documents:
- 12 obligations in exact bijection with A0 §3.9 R1–R12
- 5 owned objects in exact bijection with A0 §3.9 owned objects
- 6 invariants in exact bijection with A0 §3.9 RT08-INV-1 through RT08-INV-6
- All 9 A1-v1.2 PAIRs correctly characterized
- Dependencies and dependents in exact bijection with A0
- D-series citations accurate throughout
- No architectural invention
- No imported responsibilities from other runtimes
- No Class IV defects

### Basis for Conditional Status

The CONDITIONALLY CERTIFIED verdict rests on FIND-002 (Class II), which causes CERT-07 and CERT-10 to fail:

**FIND-002 — RS-29/RS-31 Loop Phase Characterization Error:**
R8 characterizes RT-08 as "Supporting runtime" for the "Observation of Consequence" constitutional loop phase. A1-v1.2 §15.2 contradicts this: the table places "RT-14 → RT-08" in the Primary Runtime column for this phase, not in the Supporting Runtimes column. The Supporting column contains "RT-07, RT-06." RT-08 is Primary (in sequence with RT-14), not Supporting. This error directly causes CERT-07 to fail per its criterion: "RS-29 exactly matches A1 §15.2 for this runtime."

**Secondary findings (non-blocking):**
- FIND-001 (Class III): RS-06 §6.1 heading nomenclature conflation — non-blocking editorial issue
- FIND-003 (Class I): RS-06 §6.2 authority derivation chain omits A1 §5.1 citation — traceability gap, substantively correct

FIND-002 is the sole blocking deficiency. FIND-001 and FIND-003 are non-blocking. Remediation of FIND-002 alone, with concurrent editorial resolution of FIND-001 and FIND-003, is sufficient for unconditional certification.
