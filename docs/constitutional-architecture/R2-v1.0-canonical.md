This document is the complete R2 specification, covering RS-01 through RS-36 in full conformance with R0 v1.0. R2 v1.0 is the first canonical specification of the Constitutional Authority Runtime (RT-02).

### RS-01.1 — Runtime Position in Constitutional Architecture

RT-02 occupies the second position in Tier 1 of the constitutional runtime architecture. Tier 1 is the Constitutional Infrastructure layer — the four runtimes (RT-01, RT-02, RT-03, RT-04) whose operational integrity is a prerequisite for the constitutional operation of every other runtime.

Within Tier 1, RT-02's constitutional position is:
- **After RT-01**: RT-02 depends on RT-01 for identity resolution. RT-02 cannot bind authority to an actor whose identity has not been constitutionally resolved by RT-01.
- **Before RT-03 (at Gate 3)**: Every Class A operation must obtain an AuthorityResolutionResult from RT-02 before RT-03 may admit the operation to the Reality Fabric.
- **Before RT-04 (as observed)**: RT-04 exercises constitutional audit authority over RT-02's authority records and operations. RT-02 does not gate RT-04's audit access.

### RS-01.2 — Runtime Constitutional Function

RT-02 is the sole constitutional locus of authority record maintenance and authority determination in the APEX system. No other runtime may create, modify, revoke, or resolve constitutional authority records. The RT-02 mandate is singular and non-delegable: authority must be constitutionally managed — not assumed, not implicit, not delegated to the actor claiming it.

### RS-01.3 — Runtime Constitutional Basis Statement

RT-02 exists because D-2 §X establishes that authority is derived, not assumed; because D3 GI-5 requires a single authority root per civilizational era; because D4 §4.3 requires a constitutional actor to provide authority validation at Gate 3; and because D6 Part 4 specifies five distinct constitutional authority types with five integrity rules that must be actively enforced. RT-02 is the constitutional instantiation of the authority enforcement obligation.

### RS-02.1 — Governing Constitutional Principles for RT-02

The following constitutional principles from D-2 govern all RT-02 operations and must be preserved without degradation:

**P-GOV-1: Authority Derivation.** No authority exercised within the APEX system is self-authorizing. Every authority claim must be traceable through a DelegationRecord chain to the FoundingRatification of the current civilizational era. RT-02 is the constitutional actor that maintains and enforces this traceability (D-2 §X; D3 GI-5).

**P-GOV-2: Proportionality.** Authority scope must be proportional to the operation scope. An actor authorized for local decisions does not automatically hold authority for global decisions. An actor with Observation Authority does not automatically hold Decision Authority. RT-02 enforces scope boundaries within every DelegationRecord and AuthorityClaim it maintains (D-2 §X; D4 §4.3(d)).

**P-GOV-3: Non-Delegable Accountability.** Accountability cannot be delegated even when authority is. An actor who delegates authority to another actor remains accountable for the delegation itself. RT-02 preserves this by maintaining DelegationRecords that record both the authority transfer and the delegating actor's identity — accountability cannot be discharged by delegation (D-2 §X).

**P-GOV-4: Explicit Authority.** Authority not explicitly recorded in a DelegationRecord does not exist constitutionally. Silence in authority records means no authority. RT-02 treats absence of a valid DelegationRecord as definitive evidence of no authority — not as an ambiguity requiring resolution in the actor's favor (D-2 §X; D3 RF-A7 — Authority Non-Implication).

**P-GOV-5: Authority Separation.** The five constitutional authority types (Observation, Interpretation, Decision, Projection, Audit) are constitutionally distinct and may not be collapsed within a single actor for a given domain. RT-02 maintains this separation by enforcing AIR-1 through AIR-5 on every authority record it manages (D6 AIR-1 through AIR-5).

## RS-03 — Runtime Purpose

RT-02 exists to make constitutional authority operationally expressible without introducing implementation.

The constitutional architecture requires that every actor-originated operation be performed only by an actor with valid, scoped, and unrevoked constitutional authority for that specific operation. This requirement is stated in D-2 §X (authority is derived, not assumed) and operationalized by D4 §4.3 (Gate 3 — Authority Validation). However, D4 §4.3 requires a constitutional actor to provide the authority determination — it does not perform that determination itself. RT-02 is that constitutional actor.

### RS-03.1 — Functional Purpose

RT-02 serves four interdependent constitutional functions:

**Function 1 — Authority Record Maintenance.** RT-02 maintains the canonical registry of every authority grant, every active authority claim, every revocation, and every authority conflict in the APEX system. This registry is the sole authoritative source of truth for constitutional authority within any given civilizational era.

**Function 2 — Authority Determination.** At Gate 3, for every Class A operation, RT-02 determines whether the initiating actor holds valid, scoped, and unrevoked authority for the proposed operation. This determination is constitutional — it is not advisory, not probabilistic, and not subject to override by the requesting actor.

**Function 3 — Authority Lifecycle Management.** RT-02 manages the complete lifecycle of every constitutional authority grant from creation through revocation or expiration to archival. No authority lifecycle event occurs without RT-02 recording it with full provenance.

**Function 4 — Authority Integrity Enforcement.** RT-02 enforces the five Authority Integrity Rules (AIR-1 through AIR-5 from D6 Part 4) that govern how the five constitutional authority types may be distributed across actors and domains. No AIR violation may persist in RT-02's authority records.

### RS-03.2 — What RT-02 Does Not Do

RT-02 does not enforce constitutional rules (RT-03). RT-02 does not resolve actor identity (RT-01). RT-02 does not audit constitutional compliance independently (RT-04). RT-02 does not admit objects to the Reality Fabric (RT-05). RT-02 does not execute actions in external reality (RT-13). RT-02 does not make civilizational decisions (RT-12). RT-02 provides authority determinations — the acts of enforcement, execution, and admission belong to other runtimes.

## RS-04 — Runtime Scope

### RS-04.1 — In Scope

The following are within the constitutional scope of RT-02:

1. **Authority record ownership.** All DelegationRecords, AuthorityClaims, AuthorityRevocationRecords, AuthorityConflictRecords, and AuthorityScope records are owned by RT-02.

2. **Authority determination at Gate 3.** Every authority determination made in response to a Gate 3 query from RT-03 is within RT-02's scope.

3. **Authority grant processing.** Evaluating and recording authority grant requests from actors holding delegation authority, subject to RT-03 gate processing of the grant operation itself.

4. **Authority revocation.** Processing and propagating revocations of authority grants, including immediate invalidation of all AuthorityClaims derived from a revoked DelegationRecord.

5. **Authority lifecycle transitions.** Recording every transition in the lifecycle of every DelegationRecord and AuthorityClaim.

6. **AIR enforcement.** Detecting and recording violations of any of the five Authority Integrity Rules (AIR-1 through AIR-5) within RT-02's authority records.

7. **Actor invalidation coordination.** Upon receiving a suspension or termination notification from RT-01, immediately invalidating all AuthorityClaims held by the affected actor.

8. **Domain authority map maintenance.** Maintaining current authority maps for all twelve constitutional domains, covering all five authority types.

9. **Authority audit support.** Providing complete authority records to RT-04 without restriction, gatekeeping, or selective disclosure.

### RS-04.2 — Out of Scope

The following are explicitly outside RT-02's constitutional scope:

1. **Identity resolution.** RT-02 does not determine actor identity. RT-02 consumes identity records produced by RT-01 but does not perform identity verification.

2. **Gate enforcement.** RT-02 provides authority determinations to RT-03 at Gate 3, but the gate enforcement itself — the rejection, the RejectionRecord, the sequencing — is RT-03's responsibility.

3. **Fabric admission.** RT-02 does not admit authority records to the Reality Fabric directly. Authority records are admitted through RT-03 gate processing.

4. **Constitutional rule enforcement.** RT-02 does not enforce constitutional rules beyond authority scope. RT-03 is the constitutional enforcement kernel.

5. **Audit authority.** RT-02 does not hold audit authority over any runtime, including itself. RT-04 is the sole holder of constitutional audit authority.

6. **Operational execution.** RT-02 does not execute any action on behalf of any actor. It determines authority; it does not exercise it.

7. **New authority type creation.** RT-02 may not create authority types beyond the five established in D6 Part 4. The authority type taxonomy is constitutionally fixed.

## RS-05 — Runtime Responsibility

RT-02 holds the following twelve constitutional obligations, designated RT02-OBL-01 through RT02-OBL-12.

### RT02-OBL-01: DelegationRecord Registry Maintenance

**Obligation:** RT-02 must maintain a complete, append-only registry of every DelegationRecord created within the current civilizational era. No DelegationRecord may be modified after creation. No DelegationRecord may be deleted. DelegationRecords transition to Revoked or Superseded state but remain in the registry permanently.

**Constitutional source:** D-2 §X (explicit authority); D3 GCR-2 (authority chain completeness); D8 IOR-3 (authority history preservation).

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Gate 3 validation becomes impossible. The constitutional basis for any authority determination is lost. D3 GCR-2 is violated.

### RT02-OBL-02: AuthorityClaim Maintenance

**Obligation:** RT-02 must maintain a current registry of all AuthorityClaims for all actors across all five authority types and all twelve domains. An AuthorityClaim is current if its backing DelegationRecord is Active and unrevoked. RT-02 must reflect AuthorityClaim state changes immediately upon their constitutional trigger.

**Constitutional source:** D4 §4.3 (Gate 3 conditions); D6 Part 4 (five authority types); A0 §3.3.

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Gate 3 cannot determine whether an actor holds valid authority. Constitutional operation stalls.

### RT02-OBL-03: Gate 3 Authority Validation

**Obligation:** RT-02 must respond to every Gate 3 authority validation request from RT-03 with a definite AuthorityResolutionResult. The result must be either AUTHORITY CONFIRMED or AUTHORITY DENIED. RT-02 may not return an ambiguous, deferred, or probabilistic result for a Gate 3 query. The response must evaluate all six conditions specified in D4 §4.3(a) through (f).

**Constitutional source:** D4 §4.3 (Gate 3 — Authority Validation); A0 §3.3 (Responsibility 3); A1 PAIR 04.

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Gate 3 cannot complete. The Class A operation cannot be admitted or rejected constitutionally. Constitutional deadlock.

### RT02-OBL-04: Authority Scope Enforcement

**Obligation:** RT-02 must enforce scope constraints within every DelegationRecord. An actor's AuthorityClaim may not exceed the scope specified in the DelegationRecord chain at any link. Scope constraints include: domain specificity, operation type, temporal window, and autonomy band as specified in D4 §4.3(d) and (f).

**Constitutional source:** D-2 §X (proportional authorization); D4 §4.3(d) and (f); D6 Part 4 (domain-specific authority types).

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Actors exercise authority beyond their constitutional scope. D-2 §X proportionality principle violated. Unconstitutional operations admitted.

### RT02-OBL-05: Authority Revocation Processing and Propagation

**Obligation:** RT-02 must process every authority revocation immediately and propagate the revocation to all AuthorityClaims derived from the revoked DelegationRecord. Revocation propagation has no grace period — per RT02-INV-5, revoked authority is immediately invalid. RT-02 must record every revocation in an AuthorityRevocationRecord.

**Constitutional source:** A0 §3.3 (Responsibility 5); D4 §4.3 (Revocation propagation); D8 PROH-3 (No Authority Bypass).

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Revoked actors continue to hold valid-appearing AuthorityClaims. Unconstitutional authority persists.

### RT02-OBL-06: Five Authority Type Distinctness Enforcement

**Obligation:** RT-02 must maintain the five constitutional authority types (Observation, Interpretation, Decision, Projection, Audit) as constitutionally distinct and non-interchangeable in all records it maintains. An AuthorityClaim for Observation Authority is not an AuthorityClaim for Interpretation Authority, even if held by the same actor. Conflation of authority types in RT-02 records is an AIR-1 violation.

**Constitutional source:** D6 §4.1 through §4.6 (five authority types); D6 AIR-1 (Authority Separation); A0 §3.3 (Responsibility 6).

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** AIR-1 violated. Authority collapse conditions created. Self-authorization possible.

### RT02-OBL-07: AIR-5 Audit Independence Enforcement

**Obligation:** RT-02 must enforce AIR-5 within its authority records: no actor may hold Audit Authority for a domain simultaneously with any other authority type for that same domain. If a proposed authority grant would create this condition, RT-02 must reject the grant and record an AuthorityConflictRecord. Existing records that violate AIR-5 constitute a constitutional emergency requiring immediate resolution.

**Constitutional source:** D6 §4.6 (Audit Authority definition); D6 AIR-5 (Audit Independence); A0 §3.3 (Responsibility 7); RT02-INV-3.

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Audit Independence destroyed. A single actor could hold and audit their own authority, eliminating constitutional oversight.

### RT02-OBL-08: Non-Delegable Accountability Preservation

**Obligation:** RT-02 must preserve non-delegable accountability in every DelegationRecord. A DelegationRecord must record the delegating actor and the recipient actor independently. The delegating actor's identity is never removed from the record chain by the act of delegation. Authority may be passed; accountability for the delegation may not.

**Constitutional source:** D-2 §X (non-delegable accountability); A0 §3.3 (Responsibility 8).

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Accountability gaps created. Actors can delegate authority without remaining accountable for having done so. D-2 §X violated.

### RT02-OBL-09: Authority Conflict Detection and Recording

**Obligation:** RT-02 must detect and record AuthorityConflictRecords when two or more actors hold conflicting authority claims — claims that cannot both be constitutionally valid simultaneously. Detected conflicts must be recorded and notified to RT-04. Conflicts do not prevent RT-02 from providing Gate 3 determinations; RT-02 must report which AuthorityClaim is under authority review.

**Constitutional source:** A0 §3.3 (Responsibility 9); D4 Part 7 (Under Authority Review); D4 §4.3 (Revocation propagation and authority challenge detection).

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Authority conflicts persist undetected. Actors operate under conflicting authority claims. Constitutional coherence in the authority domain is lost.

### RT02-OBL-10: Actor Suspension and Termination Coordination with RT-01

**Obligation:** Upon receiving an actor suspension notification from RT-01, RT-02 must immediately place all AuthorityClaims held by that actor in Suspended state. Upon receiving an actor termination notification from RT-01, RT-02 must immediately transition all AuthorityClaims held by that actor to Revoked state. The transition must be recorded with the RT-01 notification as its constitutional trigger.

**Constitutional source:** A0 §3.3 (Responsibility 10); A1 PAIR 01 (RT-01 → RT-02 interaction); RT02-INV-5 (revoked authority immediately invalid).

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Suspended or terminated actors retain valid-appearing authority. Operations by constitutionally ineligible actors pass Gate 3.

### RT02-OBL-11: Authority Chain Completeness Maintenance

**Obligation:** RT-02 must ensure that every active AuthorityClaim is backed by a DelegationRecord chain that is complete and traceable from the actor's ActorProfile to the current era's FoundingRatification (D3 GCR-2 and GI-5). A chain with any broken link is not a valid authority chain. RT-02 must detect chain breaks and place affected AuthorityClaims Under Authority Review immediately upon detection.

**Constitutional source:** D3 GCR-2 (Authority Chain Completeness); D3 GI-5 (Single Authority Root); D4 §4.3(c) (DelegationRecord chain completeness); A0 §3.3 (Responsibility 11).

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Authority chains with broken links persist as apparently valid. Gate 3 validation based on incomplete chains admits constitutionally unauthorized operations.

### RT02-OBL-12: Domain Authority Map Maintenance

**Obligation:** RT-02 must maintain current domain authority maps for all twelve constitutional domains, recording which actors hold which authority types in which domains at any given constitutional moment. Domain authority maps are derived from the active AuthorityClaim registry and must remain synchronized with it. RT-15 domain instances consume these maps.

**Constitutional source:** D6 Part 4 (domain-specific authority architecture); A0 §3.3 (State Responsibilities — domain authority maps); A0 §3.15 (RT-15 specification — authority map consumption).

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** RT-15 domain instances cannot determine which actors hold valid authority within their domains. Domain operations proceed without authority verification.

## RS-06 — Runtime Authority

### RS-06.1 — Held Authority

RT-02 holds one constitutional authority:

**AIR-2: Authority Validation Authority.** RT-02 is the sole constitutional actor authorized to produce AuthorityResolutionResults. No other runtime may create, modify, or revoke authority records. No other runtime may produce a binding authority determination. This authority is not delegable — RT-02 cannot authorize another runtime to perform authority validation on its behalf.

RT-02 also holds:
- **Exclusive custody of DelegationRecords.** RT-02 is the sole constitutional custodian of all DelegationRecords in the APEX system.
- **Authority grant authority (conditional).** RT-02 may create DelegationRecords when the grant request comes from an actor holding constitutionally valid delegation authority and passes RT-03 gate processing.
- **Authority revocation authority (conditional).** RT-02 may revoke DelegationRecords when the revocation request comes from an actor holding constitutionally valid revocation authority and passes RT-03 gate processing.

### RS-06.2 — Authority Not Held

RT-02 explicitly does not hold the following authorities:

| Authority | Held By | Why RT-02 Does Not Hold It |
|---|---|---|
| Constitutional enforcement (Gate 1–6) | RT-03 | Authority determination and constitutional enforcement are constitutionally distinct (D8 INV-3) |
| Identity resolution | RT-01 | Identity and authority are constitutionally distinct categories (D-2 §IX, §X) |
| Fabric admission authority | RT-05 | RT-02 authority records are admitted through RT-03 gate processing |

| Decision authority | RT-12 actors | RT-02 validates Decision Authority; it does not hold or exercise it |

### RS-06.3 — Gate Authority

RT-02 provides the constitutional input for Gate 3 of every Class A operation. Gate 3 is RT-02's sole gate contribution. RT-02 does not participate in Gate 1 (identity — RT-01), Gate 2 (object state — RT-05), Gate 4 (epistemic — RT-09), Gate 5 (constitutive coherence — RT-03), or Gate 6 (temporal integrity — RT-03).

Gate 3 is RT-02's constitutional activation point for the Class A operation lifecycle. Every Class A operation requires RT-02's authority determination before it can be admitted to the Reality Fabric.

## RS-07 — Owned Constitutional Objects

RT-02 exclusively owns the following constitutional objects. These objects may not be created, modified, or terminated by any other runtime.

### RT02-OWN-01: DelegationRecord

**Definition:** A constitutional record documenting the explicit grant of a specific authority type, within a specific scope, from a delegating actor to a recipient actor, for a defined temporal window or until revoked.

**Constitutional basis:** D-2 §X (explicit authority); D3 GCR-2 (authority chain completeness); D4 §4.3 (Gate 3 conditions); D8 IOR-3 (authority history preservation).

**Required fields (constitutional, not implementation):** DelegationRecord identifier; delegating actor identity (ActorProfile reference from RT-01); recipient actor identity (ActorProfile reference from RT-01); authority type (one of: Observation, Interpretation, Decision, Projection, Audit); domain scope (specific domain or domains authorized); operation scope (specific operation types authorized); temporal scope (valid-from, valid-until, or indefinite-until-revoked); autonomy band; authorization chain reference (parent DelegationRecord or FoundingRatification); DelegationRecord state (Draft, Active, Superseded, Revoked); creation provenance.

**State transitions:** Draft → Active (upon RT-03 gate processing of grant operation); Active → Superseded (upon creation of a superseding DelegationRecord with higher authority); Active → Revoked (upon valid revocation); terminal states: Superseded, Revoked. No deletion.

### RT02-OWN-02: AuthorityClaim

**Definition:** A constitutional record representing a specific actor's current claim to hold a specific authority type in a specific domain. An AuthorityClaim is valid if and only if it is backed by at least one Active, unrevoked DelegationRecord.

**Constitutional basis:** D4 §4.3(a) (AuthorityClaim existence condition); D6 Part 4 (five authority types, domain-specific scope).

**Required fields:** AuthorityClaim identifier; actor identity (ActorProfile reference); authority type; domain scope; backing DelegationRecord reference(s); AuthorityClaim state (Active, Suspended, UnderAuthorityReview, Revoked, Expired); effective-from; effective-until (if time-bounded); revocation reference (if revoked).

**State transitions:** Active → Suspended (on actor suspension notification from RT-01); Active → Revoked (on actor termination or DelegationRecord revocation); Active → Expired (on temporal bound crossing); Active → UnderAuthorityReview (on authority chain break detection or active challenge); UnderAuthorityReview → Active (on challenge resolution confirming validity) or → Revoked (on challenge resolution invalidating claim); terminal states: Revoked, Expired.

### RT02-OWN-03: AuthorityRevocationRecord

**Definition:** A constitutional record documenting the revocation of a DelegationRecord or AuthorityClaim, including the revoking actor, the constitutional basis for revocation, the timestamp, and all AuthorityClaims invalidated by the revocation.

**Constitutional basis:** A0 §3.3 (Owned Objects); D4 §4.3 (Revocation propagation); D8 IOR-3 (authority history preservation).

**Required fields:** AuthorityRevocationRecord identifier; revoked object reference (DelegationRecord or AuthorityClaim); revoking actor identity; constitutional basis for revocation; revocation timestamp; downstream invalidations list (all AuthorityClaims placed in Revoked state by this action); RT-04 notification status.

**State:** AuthorityRevocationRecords are immutable upon creation. No state transitions.

### RT02-OWN-04: AuthorityConflictRecord

**Definition:** A constitutional record documenting detected conflicts between two or more AuthorityClaims — conditions in which two claims cannot both be constitutionally valid simultaneously.

**Constitutional basis:** A0 §3.3 (Owned Objects); D6 AIR-1 through AIR-5 (conditions for conflict detection).

**Required fields:** AuthorityConflictRecord identifier; conflicting AuthorityClaim references; conflict type (AIR violation type, or chain conflict, or scope overlap); detection timestamp; resolution status (Unresolved, Resolved, EscalatedToRT04); resolution reference (if resolved); RT-04 notification record.

**State transitions:** Unresolved → Resolved (on constitutional resolution); Unresolved → EscalatedToRT04 (on escalation); terminal state: Resolved.

### RT02-OWN-05: AuthorityScope

**Definition:** A constitutional record specifying the precise boundaries of a specific authority grant — the domain, operation types, and temporal window within which the authority is valid.

**Constitutional basis:** D-2 §X (proportional authorization); D4 §4.3(d) and (f) (scope and autonomy band conditions); D6 Part 4 (domain-specific scope).

**Required fields:** AuthorityScope identifier; domain reference; permitted operation types; temporal validity window; autonomy band; scope constraint notes; associated DelegationRecord.

**State:** AuthorityScope records are immutable once created. A new AuthorityScope must be created for scope modifications; the prior scope is retained.

## RS-10 — Managed Constitutional Objects

RT-02 manages the following constitutional objects throughout their complete lifecycle. Management includes creation, state transition, and permanent archival — never deletion.

### RT02-MAN-01: DelegationRecord (Full Lifecycle)

RT-02 manages DelegationRecords from their creation (Draft state) through their terminal state (Revoked or Superseded). Every state transition of every DelegationRecord is recorded with its constitutional trigger and the identity of the actor or RT-01 notification that caused the transition. Management includes:
- Evaluating grant requests against D4 §4.3 and AIR rules before creating a DelegationRecord in Draft state
- Transitioning DelegationRecords from Draft to Active upon RT-03 gate processing confirmation
- Transitioning DelegationRecords to Superseded upon grant of a higher-scope replacement
- Transitioning DelegationRecords to Revoked upon valid revocation

### RT02-MAN-02: AuthorityClaim (Full Lifecycle)

RT-02 manages AuthorityClaims as derived records synthesized from its DelegationRecord registry. AuthorityClaims are not independently created — they are maintained as a structured view of which actors hold valid authority at any constitutional moment. Management includes:
- Deriving AuthorityClaims from Active DelegationRecords
- Transitioning AuthorityClaims to Suspended on ActorSuspensionNotification from RT-01
- Transitioning AuthorityClaims to Revoked on ActorTerminationNotification or DelegationRecord revocation
- Transitioning AuthorityClaims to Expired on temporal bound crossing
- Placing AuthorityClaims UnderAuthorityReview on chain integrity failure

### RT02-MAN-03: AuthorityRevocationRecord (Immutable after creation)

RT-02 creates AuthorityRevocationRecords at the moment of revocation processing. These records are immutable — they are never modified after creation. RT-02 retains all AuthorityRevocationRecords permanently as part of the authority history archive.

### RT02-MAN-04: AuthorityConflictRecord (Lifecycle through resolution)

RT-02 creates AuthorityConflictRecords on detection of AIR violations or incompatible concurrent authority claims. RT-02 manages conflict records through their resolution lifecycle: Unresolved → Resolved or EscalatedToRT04. RT-02 does not resolve conflicts by unilaterally revoking authority; conflict resolution is a constitutional process that may involve RT-04 and founding authority.

### RT02-MAN-05: AuthorityScope (Immutable after creation)

RT-02 creates AuthorityScope records as part of every DelegationRecord. AuthorityScope records are immutable after creation. If scope must change, a new AuthorityScope is created and the old DelegationRecord is superseded.

### RT02-MAN-06: FoundingAuthorityRecord

RT-02 manages the single FoundingAuthorityRecord that establishes the constitutional root of all authority chains within the current civilizational era. The FoundingAuthorityRecord corresponds to the FoundingRatification constitutional object (D4 §4.3 FoundingRatification exception) and is the terminal point of every valid authority chain traversal. RT-02 holds this record in read-only state — it was established at founding and is immutable within the era.

## RS-11 — Managed Constitutional State

RT-02 maintains the following constitutional state structures. These are not implementation data structures — they are constitutional state domains that any conforming implementation must preserve.

### RT02-STATE-01: Active DelegationRecord Registry

The complete set of DelegationRecords currently in Active state. This registry is the primary source for Gate 3 authority validation. Every DelegationRecord in this registry must have a complete chain traceable to the FoundingRatification.

**State domain properties:** Append-only (records transition out but are never removed); consistent (all Active records have traceable chains); complete (no Active DelegationRecord exists outside this registry).

### RT02-STATE-02: Revoked Authority Archive

The complete set of DelegationRecords in Revoked state and AuthorityRevocationRecords. This archive grows monotonically — entries are never purged. It supports RT-04's historical authority audit capability.

**State domain properties:** Permanent (no deletion); append-only; accessible to RT-04 without restriction.

### RT02-STATE-03: Expired Authority Archive

The complete set of DelegationRecords that have reached their temporal bound without revocation, transitioned to Expired state. Expired records are retained permanently.

**State domain properties:** Permanent; append-only; accessible to RT-04.

### RT02-STATE-04: AuthorityClaim Registry

The current registry of all AuthorityClaims indexed by actor identity and authority type. This registry provides O(1) conceptual lookup for Gate 3 validation — given an actor and an authority type, RT-02 can immediately determine the claim's current state.

**State domain properties:** Synchronized with DelegationRecord registry; immediately updated on every state transition trigger; reflects the authoritative current state of every actor's authority holdings.

### RT02-STATE-05: AuthorityConflict Register

The current set of unresolved AuthorityConflictRecords. This register drives RT-02's conflict notification obligations to RT-04 and tracks outstanding authority integrity issues.

**State domain properties:** Consistent (every entry corresponds to an actual detected conflict); active (entries are removed on resolution and replaced by resolution records).

### RT02-STATE-06: Domain Authority Maps

Twelve current authority maps — one per constitutional domain. Each map records which actors hold which of the five authority types in that domain at the current constitutional moment.

**State domain properties:** Synchronized with the AuthorityClaim Registry; consistent with AIR-1 through AIR-5 (if any AIR violation is present, it is reflected in the AuthorityConflict Register); consumed by RT-15 domain instances.

### RT02-STATE-07: FoundingAuthorityRecord

The single immutable record establishing the constitutional root of all authority chains for the current era. Created at FoundingRatification; never modified within the era.

**State domain properties:** Immutable; persistent; the terminal point of all authority chain traversals.

### RT02-STATE-08: Authority Revocation Propagation Queue

The queue of pending revocation propagations — cases where a revocation has been recorded but downstream AuthorityClaims have not yet been transitioned to Revoked state. This queue must be drained immediately; no revocation propagation may remain pending beyond the constitutional completion of RT02-PROC-03.

**State domain properties:** Must be empty at every Gate 3 evaluation point; any non-empty state indicates a revocation propagation in progress.

### RT02-STATE-09: AuthorityScope Registry

The complete set of AuthorityScope records, indexed by DelegationRecord. Provides scope constraints for every Gate 3 evaluation.

**State domain properties:** Consistent with DelegationRecord registry; every Active DelegationRecord has exactly one AuthorityScope entry.

### RT02-STATE-10: Gate 3 Response Record

A log of every Gate 3 AuthorityResolutionResult produced by RT-02, retained for RT-04 audit access. Every Gate 3 response is logged with: the requesting RT-03 operation identifier, the actor evaluated, the authority type evaluated, the result (CONFIRMED or DENIED), the specific D4 §4.3 condition evaluated (if DENIED), and the DelegationRecord chain evaluated (if CONFIRMED).

**State domain properties:** Append-only; permanent; accessible to RT-04 without restriction.

## RS-12 — Internal Processes

RT-02 performs the following ten internal processes.

### RT02-PROC-01: Authority Grant Processing

**Trigger:** Receipt of RT02-IN-01 (AuthorityGrantRequest) from an authorized actor via RT-03 gate processing.

**Constitutional preconditions:**
- The granting actor's identity must be resolved (RT02-IN-10 must have been received from RT-01 for this actor)
- The granting actor must hold an Active AuthorityClaim for the authority type being granted (they must have delegation authority)
- The recipient actor's identity must be resolved (ActorProfile must exist in RT-01 registry)
- The proposed grant must not violate AIR-1 (the recipient must not hold conflicting authority in the same domain)
- The proposed grant must not violate AIR-5 (Audit Authority and any other authority type cannot be granted to the same actor for the same domain)
- The proposed scope must not exceed the granting actor's own authority scope

**Process steps:**
1. Verify granting actor's AuthorityClaim for delegation authority in specified domain
2. Verify recipient actor's ActorProfile with RT-01 (RT02-IN-10)
3. Evaluate proposed grant against AIR-1 through AIR-5
4. If any AIR condition violated: produce AuthorityConflictRecord (RT02-OWN-04); deny grant; notify RT-04
5. If all conditions satisfied: create DelegationRecord in Draft state (RT02-OWN-01)
6. Create AuthorityScope record (RT02-OWN-05)
7. Submit DelegationRecord to RT-03 gate processing for fabric admission
8. Upon RT-03 confirmation: transition DelegationRecord from Draft to Active
9. Update AuthorityClaim Registry (RT02-STATE-04)
10. Update Domain Authority Map for affected domain (RT02-STATE-06)
11. Produce AuthorityAuditRecord (RT02-OUT-10) logging the grant

**Constitutional postconditions:**
- DelegationRecord in Active state with complete authority chain to FoundingRatification
- AuthorityClaim in Active state for recipient actor
- Domain Authority Map updated
- AuthorityAuditRecord logged

**Failure exit:** RT02-FAIL-03 (Unauthorized Authority Grant) if grant conditions not satisfied.

### RT02-PROC-02: Authority Validation (Gate 3)

**Trigger:** Receipt of RT02-IN-02 (Gate3AuthorityValidationRequest) from RT-03 at Gate 3; or RT02-IN-04/IN-05 from RT-12/RT-13.

**Constitutional preconditions:**
- Requesting runtime has provided a resolved actor identity (Gate 1 has passed — RT-01 has confirmed identity)
- Proposed operation type has been specified
- Target object scope has been specified

**Process steps:**
1. Retrieve AuthorityClaim for the specified actor and authority type (RT02-STATE-04)
2. Evaluate D4 §4.3(a): Does an AuthorityClaim exist for the actor covering the proposed operation type and target?
3. Evaluate D4 §4.3(b): Is every backing DelegationRecord unrevoked?
4. Evaluate D4 §4.3(c): Is the DelegationRecord chain complete and traceable to FoundingRatification without cycle?
5. Evaluate D4 §4.3(d): Does the claimed scope not exceed the scope in the DelegationRecord chain at any link?
6. Evaluate D4 §4.3(e): Is there any active unresolved authority challenge on the specific AuthorityClaim being invoked?
7. Evaluate D4 §4.3(f): Does the actor's autonomy band permit the proposed operation without escalation?
8. If all six conditions satisfied: produce AuthorityResolutionResult CONFIRMED (RT02-OUT-01)
9. If any condition not satisfied: produce AuthorityResolutionResult DENIED specifying the specific condition (RT02-OUT-02)
10. Log Gate 3 Response Record (RT02-STATE-10)
11. Produce AuthorityAuditRecord (RT02-OUT-10)

**Constitutional postconditions:**
- A definite AuthorityResolutionResult has been delivered to the requesting runtime
- Gate 3 Response Record logged
- AuthorityAuditRecord produced

**Failure exit:** RT02-FAIL-01 (Authority Resolution Failure) if RT-02 cannot produce a definite result due to internal state inconsistency.

### RT02-PROC-03: Authority Revocation Processing

**Trigger:** Receipt of RT02-IN-09 (AuthorityRevocationRequest) from an authorized actor via RT-03.

**Constitutional preconditions:**
- Revoking actor must hold revocation authority for the specified DelegationRecord or AuthorityClaim
- The object being revoked must be in Active or Suspended state

**Process steps:**
1. Verify revoking actor's authority to revoke the specified record
2. Identify the DelegationRecord to be revoked
3. Transition DelegationRecord to Revoked state
4. Create AuthorityRevocationRecord (RT02-OWN-03)
5. Identify all downstream AuthorityClaims backed by this DelegationRecord
6. Immediately transition all downstream AuthorityClaims to Revoked state (RT02-INV-5: no grace period)
7. Identify all further downstream DelegationRecords that depend on the revoked record
8. Recursively apply steps 3-7 to all downstream DelegationRecords
9. Drain RT02-STATE-08 (Authority Revocation Propagation Queue) completely
10. Notify RT-04 of revocation and propagation scope (RT02-OUT-07)
11. Update Domain Authority Maps for all affected domains (RT02-STATE-06)
12. Produce AuthorityAuditRecord (RT02-OUT-10)

**Constitutional postconditions:**
- Revoked DelegationRecord and all downstream records in Revoked state
- RT02-STATE-08 empty
- RT-04 notified
- AuthorityAuditRecord produced

**Failure exit:** RT02-FAIL-04 (Authority Revocation Propagation Failure) if propagation cannot complete.

### RT02-PROC-04: Actor Suspension Authority Invalidation

**Trigger:** Receipt of RT02-IN-07 (ActorSuspensionNotification) from RT-01.

**Process steps:**
1. Identify all AuthorityClaims held by suspended actor in RT02-STATE-04
2. Immediately transition all identified AuthorityClaims to Suspended state
3. Record suspension trigger (RT-01 notification reference) in each AuthorityClaim transition record
4. Update Domain Authority Maps (RT02-STATE-06) to remove suspended actor's active authority indicators
5. Notify RT-04 of actor suspension authority invalidation (RT02-OUT-10)
6. Maintain DelegationRecords in Active state — suspension of the actor does not revoke the delegation (it may be restored upon actor reinstatement)

**Failure exit:** RT02-FAIL-08 (Actor Identity Mismatch) if RT-01 notification references an actor with no AuthorityClaims in RT-02's registry.

### RT02-PROC-05: Actor Termination Authority Archival

**Trigger:** Receipt of RT02-IN-08 (ActorTerminationNotification) from RT-01.

**Process steps:**
1. Identify all AuthorityClaims held by terminated actor
2. Immediately transition all identified AuthorityClaims to Revoked state
3. Create AuthorityRevocationRecord for each claim (constitutional trigger: actor termination)
4. For each active DelegationRecord where the terminated actor was the delegating actor: evaluate whether the delegation must be reviewed (non-delegable accountability — the delegation remains valid but is flagged for RT-04 review)
5. Update Domain Authority Maps
6. Notify RT-04 of actor termination authority archival
7. Produce AuthorityAuditRecord

**Failure exit:** RT02-FAIL-09 (Invariant Breach) if termination archival cannot be completed, creating an authority record gap.

### RT02-PROC-06: Authority Chain Integrity Verification

**Trigger:** RT02-IN-11 (AuthorityChainIntegritySignal) from RT-02 internal continuous scan; or upon receipt of an ActorSuspensionNotification or ActorTerminationNotification from RT-01.

**Process steps:**
1. Traverse the DelegationRecord chain for the specified AuthorityClaim from the actor's record to the FoundingRatification
2. If chain is complete and unbroken: update integrity status; no action required
3. If any link is broken (a DelegationRecord in the chain is Revoked, missing, or in an invalid state): immediately place the affected AuthorityClaim UnderAuthorityReview
4. Create AuthorityConflictRecord recording the chain break (RT02-OWN-04)
5. Notify RT-04 of authority chain integrity failure
6. Block Gate 3 validation for the affected actor until chain is restored or claim is Revoked

**Failure exit:** RT02-FAIL-02 (DelegationRecord Chain Break) on detection of an irresolvable chain break.

### RT02-PROC-07: AIR Violation Detection

**Trigger:** Continuous process; triggered on any change to RT02-STATE-04 (AuthorityClaim Registry) or RT02-STATE-06 (Domain Authority Maps).

**Process steps:**
1. For each domain, evaluate AIR-1: Do any actors hold two or more of the five authority types in this domain? If yes, create AuthorityConflictRecord specifying AIR-1 violation
2. For each domain, evaluate AIR-3: Does the domain have at least two registered actors with Observation Authority? Does it have at least two with Interpretation Authority? If any domain falls below the minimum: create AuthorityConflictRecord specifying AIR-3 violation
3. For each domain, evaluate AIR-5: Does any actor hold Audit Authority and any other authority type in this domain? If yes: create AuthorityConflictRecord specifying AIR-5 violation (constitutional emergency — this is RT02-INV-3 breach)
4. AIR-2 and AIR-4 enforcement: AIR-2 violations are detected at Gate 3 evaluation (actor claiming Projection Authority without valid DelegationRecord); AIR-4 violations are detected via RT-04 monitoring of the Open Action Register (not within RT-02's direct scope)
5. For any detected violation: notify RT-04; add to RT02-STATE-05 (AuthorityConflict Register)

**Failure exit:** RT02-FAIL-05 (AIR Violation) on detection of any AIR integrity rule breach.

### RT02-PROC-08: Authority Expiration Processing

**Trigger:** Continuous temporal boundary evaluation process within RT-02.

**Process steps:**
1. Identify DelegationRecords whose temporal scope (valid-until) has been crossed
2. Transition identified DelegationRecords from Active to Expired state
3. Transition all dependent AuthorityClaims to Expired state
4. Move records to RT02-STATE-03 (Expired Authority Archive)
5. Update Domain Authority Maps
6. Notify RT-04 of expirations
7. Produce AuthorityAuditRecord

**Failure exit:** RT02-FAIL-09 (Invariant Breach) if expired authority remains in Active state.

### RT02-PROC-09: Domain Authority Map Maintenance

**Trigger:** Any change to RT02-STATE-04 (AuthorityClaim Registry); any DelegationRecord state transition; any ActorSuspensionNotification or ActorTerminationNotification.

**Process steps:**
1. For the affected domain(s), reconstruct the domain authority map from the current AuthorityClaim Registry
2. The map records: for each of the five authority types, the set of actors currently holding Active AuthorityClaims for that type in that domain
3. Deliver updated domain authority map to RT-15 via RT02-OUT-09 (if RT-15 has an active domain instance for this domain)
4. Verify AIR-3 minimum actor count requirement is satisfied in the updated map
5. If AIR-3 violated: trigger RT02-PROC-07 (AIR Violation Detection)

**Failure exit:** RT02-FAIL-01 (Authority Resolution Failure) if domain authority map cannot be constructed from current records.

### RT02-PROC-10: Audit Record Provision

**Trigger:** Receipt of RT02-IN-03 (AuditAuthorityQueryRequest) from RT-04.

**Process steps:**
1. Identify the authority records, authority history, or authority operations requested by RT-04
2. Provide complete records without gatekeeping, filtering, or selective disclosure
3. RT-02 cannot reject an RT-04 audit query on any grounds — RT-04 holds Audit Authority and RT-02 is subject to it
4. RT-02 may not notify any other runtime of an RT-04 audit query before fulfilling it
5. Produce audit provision record in RT02-STATE-10

**Constitutional constraint:** RT-04's access to RT-02 records is unconditional. RT-02 cannot gate, defer, or condition RT-04 audit access. Any RT-02 failure to provide records to RT-04 is an RT02-FAIL-09 (Invariant Breach).

## RS-13 — External Interactions

RT-02 has constitutional interaction contracts with the following runtimes. All interactions conform to A1 PAIR specifications.

### RS-13.1 — RT-02 ↔ RT-01 (A1 PAIR 01)

**Nature:** Bidirectional. RT-02 depends on RT-01 for identity resolution before authority binding. RT-01 depends on RT-02 to invalidate authority when actors are suspended or terminated.

**RT-01 → RT-02 (Identity presents to Authority):**
- RT-01 provides ActorProfile records when authority grants are requested (RT02-IN-10)
- RT-01 provides ActorSuspensionNotifications (RT02-IN-07)
- RT-01 provides ActorTerminationNotifications (RT02-IN-08)
- RT-02 cannot bind authority to an actor whose identity RT-01 has not resolved

**RT-02 → RT-01 (Authority queries Identity):**
- RT-02 queries RT-01 to confirm actor identity before processing any authority grant
- RT-02 does not write to RT-01's records
- RT-01 records are immutable during this interaction

**Constitutional constraint:** RT-02 may not grant authority to an actor whose ActorProfile does not exist in RT-01's registry. Identity is a prerequisite for authority. This constraint derives from D-2 §IX (identity) and §X (authority) as distinct constitutional categories.

### RS-13.2 — RT-02 ↔ RT-03 (A1 PAIR 04)

**Nature:** Bidirectional within Gate 3. RT-03 queries RT-02 at Gate 3 for every Class A operation. RT-02 provides authority determinations; RT-03 enforces them.

**RT-03 → RT-02 (Kernel queries Authority for Gate 3):**
- RT-03 sends Gate3AuthorityValidationRequest (RT02-IN-02) for every Class A operation at Gate 3
- RT-03 does not proceed past Gate 3 without RT-02's AuthorityResolutionResult
- Gate 3 is never skipped (A1 CLI-1 — Loop Completeness)

**RT-02 → RT-03 (Authority presents to Kernel — Gate 3):**
- RT-02 returns a definite AuthorityResolutionResult (CONFIRMED or DENIED)
- RT-02's authority records are immutable during this Gate 3 interaction
- RT-03 owns Gate 3 timing; RT-02 must respond within that window
- On DENIED: RT-03 generates a RejectionRecord; the operation is rejected
- On CONFIRMED: RT-03 proceeds to Gate 4

**Constitutional constraint:** RT-02 cannot instruct RT-03 to admit or reject an operation — RT-02 provides the authority determination; RT-03 applies it. The distinction between authority determination (RT-02) and constitutional enforcement (RT-03) is maintained per D8 INV-3.

### RS-13.3 — RT-02 ↔ RT-04 (A1 PAIR 05)

**Nature:** Unidirectional (RT-04 observes RT-02). RT-04 exercises constitutional audit authority over RT-02's authority records and operations.

**RT-04 → RT-02 (Audit observes Authority):**
- RT-04 queries RT-02's authority records, DelegationRecord chains, revocation history, and Gate 3 response records
- RT-04's access is unconditional — RT-02 cannot gate, defer, or deny RT-04 audit access
- RT-04 independently validates authority chains back to FoundingRatification
- RT-04 independently verifies AIR-1 through AIR-5 compliance in RT-02's records

**RT-02 → RT-04 (Authority presents to Audit):**
- This direction is **FORBIDDEN**. RT-02 may not initiate any action toward RT-04.
- RT-02 may not alert, pre-notify, or condition RT-04 on any matter.
- RT-02 may not provide selective or filtered records to RT-04.

**Constitutional constraint:** Audit Independence (D6 AIR-5) requires that RT-04's access to RT-02's records be completely unconditional. Any RT-02 filtering or gating of RT-04 access destroys audit independence.

### RS-13.4 — RT-02 ↔ RT-05 (A1 PAIR 11)

**Nature:** Conditional. Authority records (DelegationRecords) are Class A constitutional objects. When a DelegationRecord is created, it must be admitted to the Reality Fabric through RT-03 gate processing. RT-05 is the fabric; RT-02 produces DelegationRecords that pass through RT-03 to reach RT-05.

**RT-02 → RT-05 (via RT-03):** DelegationRecord production triggers RT-03 gate processing; upon successful gate passage, the DelegationRecord enters RT-05 as a Class A fabric object.

**RT-05 → RT-02:** RT-05 provides canonical authority object state (read-only, non-blocking direct read path). RT-02 may read fabric-admitted DelegationRecords from RT-05 for chain verification purposes.

**Constitutional constraint:** RT-02 does not directly admit DelegationRecords to the fabric — all fabric admission passes through RT-03. This enforces the Kernel Mediation Principle (D4 §2.1).

### RS-13.5 — RT-02 ↔ RT-06 (A1 PAIR 12)

**Nature:** Observational. RT-06 captures coherence state from RT-02's authority domain. RT-02 does not address RT-06.

**RT-06 → RT-02:** RT-06 reads RT-02 authority records as part of its coherence state assessment. This is non-blocking and non-gating.

**RT-02 → RT-06:** RT-02 does not initiate any action toward RT-06.

### RS-13.6 — RT-02 ↔ RT-07 (A1 PAIR 13)

**Nature:** Archival. RT-07 provides long-term historical preservation of RT-02's authority records for cross-era authority provenance.

**RT-07 → RT-02:** RT-07 preserves historical DelegationRecord archives for era-end and cross-era audit purposes. RT-02 does not query RT-07 for current-era authority operations — all current-era authority state is maintained in RT-02's own state registry.

**RT-02 → RT-07:** At era-end or upon constitutional archival triggers, RT-02 transfers its complete authority record archive to RT-07 for permanent preservation.

### RS-13.7 — RT-02 ↔ RT-08 (A1 PAIR 14)

**Nature:** Authority validation. RT-08 (Observation Runtime) must hold valid Observation Authority before any observation channel activation.

**RT-08 → RT-02:** RT-08 validates Observation Authority for observer actors through RT-03 Gate 3 (RT-02 provides the authority determination as part of the standard Gate 3 flow, not a separate interaction).

### RS-13.8 — RT-02 ↔ RT-12

**Nature:** Decision authority validation. RT-12 validates Decision Authority with RT-02 before forming any CivilizationalDecision (RT02-IN-04).

**RT-12 → RT-02:** DecisionAuthorityValidationRequest specifying actor identity and CivilizationalDecision scope.

**RT-02 → RT-12:** AuthorityResolutionResult (CONFIRMED or DENIED) for Decision Authority (RT02-OUT-04).

**Constitutional constraint:** RT-12 may not form a CivilizationalDecision without first obtaining a CONFIRMED Decision Authority result from RT-02 (through RT-03 Gate 3).

### RS-13.9 — RT-02 ↔ RT-13

**Nature:** Projection authority validation. RT-13 validates Projection Authority with RT-02 before crossing the Projection Boundary.

**RT-13 → RT-02:** ProjectionAuthorityValidationRequest specifying actor identity and Action Projection scope.

**RT-02 → RT-13:** AuthorityResolutionResult (CONFIRMED or DENIED) for Projection Authority (RT02-OUT-05).

**Constitutional constraint:** No Action Projection crosses the Projection Boundary without Projection Authority validation from RT-02 (A0 RT13-INV-3; D6 AIR-2).

### RS-13.10 — RT-02 ↔ RT-15

**Nature:** Domain authority map provision. RT-15 domain instances consume RT-02's domain authority maps.

**RT-15 → RT-02:** DomainAuthorityQuery requesting the current authority map for a specific domain (RT02-IN-06).

**RT-02 → RT-15:** DomainAuthorityMap (RT02-OUT-09) providing current actor-to-authority-type assignments for the requested domain.

**Constitutional constraint:** RT-15 must coordinate with RT-02 to enforce AIR-5 within domains — no actor may hold Audit Authority and any other authority type for the same domain (A0 RT15-INV-3).

## RS-14 — Runtime Lifecycle

RT-02 progresses through the following six lifecycle phases.

### RT02-LC-01: Pre-Activation (Founding Ceremony)

**Description:** RT-02 exists in its constitutional specification before FoundingRatification. During the Founding Ceremony, RT-02 participates in the establishment of the FoundingAuthorityRecord — the constitutional root of all authority chains. No AuthorityClaims exist in this phase (there are no actors yet). RT-02 begins accepting the founding authority configuration.

**Constitutional trigger:** Constitutional Activation begins with FoundingRatification initiation.

**State:** RT02-STATE-07 (FoundingAuthorityRecord) is being established. All other state registries are empty.

**Authority during this phase:** The Founding Ceremony Protocol (D4 Part 13) governs this phase. Normal Gate 3 conditions are superseded by the Founding Ceremony Protocol for this phase only. The exception expires at FoundingRatification.

### RT02-LC-02: Activation (FoundingRatification)

**Description:** Upon FoundingRatification, RT-02 is constitutionally activated. The FoundingAuthorityRecord becomes immutable. The first DelegationRecords for founding members are created. The founding authority chain is established.

**Constitutional trigger:** FoundingRatification constitutional object is created and admitted to the Reality Fabric.

**State:** RT02-STATE-07 (FoundingAuthorityRecord) becomes immutable. Initial DelegationRecords and AuthorityClaims are created for founding members.

**Exit to:** RT02-LC-03 (Operational).

### RT02-LC-03: Operational

**Constitutional constraint:** RT-02 suspension halts all Class A operations in the APEX system. This is the most severe constitutional operational state. The FoundingAuthorityRecord and authority archives remain accessible to RT-04 during suspension.

**Constitutional trigger:** Constitutional era end declared by founding authority.

**Activities:** Complete authority archive transferred to RT-07; era-final audit record produced for RT-04; FoundingAuthorityRecord sealed as historical; RT-02 transitions to historical state for the closed era.

### RS-20.2 — A0 RT-02 Invariants

The following invariants are specified for RT-02 in A0 §3.3 and are binding on all RT-02 operations.

**RT02-INV-1: DelegationRecord Completeness**
Every active authority grant has a DelegationRecord in Active state with a traceable authority chain to the founding authority root, without cycle, and without broken link.
*Constitutional source:* D3 GCR-2; D3 GI-5.
*Enforcement:* RT02-PROC-06 (Authority Chain Integrity Verification); verified at every Gate 3 evaluation.

**RT02-INV-2: No Unrecorded Authority**
No actor holds constitutional authority within the APEX system without a corresponding DelegationRecord in RT-02's registry. Authority without a DelegationRecord does not exist constitutionally.
*Constitutional source:* D-2 §X (explicit authority); D3 RF-A7 (Authority Non-Implication).
*Enforcement:* RT02-PROC-07 (AIR Violation Detection); RT-04 independent verification.

**RT02-INV-3: Audit Authority Independence**
No actor holds Audit Authority for a domain simultaneously with any other authority type (Observation, Interpretation, Decision, or Projection) for that same domain.
*Constitutional source:* D6 §4.6 (Audit Authority); D6 AIR-5.
*Enforcement:* RT02-PROC-07 at every AuthorityClaim Registry update; grant rejection if violation would result.

**RT02-INV-4: Scope Containment**
Every AuthorityClaim is bounded by the scope specified in its backing DelegationRecord chain. No AuthorityClaim may exceed the scope of its narrowest-scope backing DelegationRecord.
*Constitutional source:* D-2 §X (proportional authorization); D4 §4.3(d).
*Enforcement:* RT02-PROC-02 at Gate 3 evaluation; RT02-PROC-01 at grant processing.

**RT02-INV-5: Immediate Revocation Effect**
Revoked authority is immediately invalid. There is no grace period between revocation recording and invalidation of downstream AuthorityClaims. RT02-STATE-08 (Revocation Propagation Queue) must be drained before any Gate 3 response is issued following a revocation event.
*Constitutional source:* D4 §4.3 (Revocation propagation); D8 PROH-3 (No Authority Bypass).
*Enforcement:* RT02-PROC-03 drains propagation queue to completion; RT02-PROC-02 checks queue is empty before responding.

**RT02-INV-6: Authority Chain Completeness**
Every active DelegationRecord chain is complete — no link is missing, no link is in Revoked state, and the chain terminates at FoundingRatification. A chain with any broken link is not a valid authority chain.
*Constitutional source:* D3 GCR-2; D3 GI-5.
*Enforcement:* RT02-PROC-06 continuous chain verification.

### RS-20.3 — A1 Constitutional Conditions Applicable to RT-02

The following A1 constitutional conditions bind RT-02's interactions:

**CC-1 (Identity Resolution Prerequisite):** RT-02 cannot bind authority to an actor whose identity has not been resolved by RT-01. Authority binding is always subsequent to identity resolution.

**CC-6 (Founding Authority Supremacy):** Founding authority may revoke any DelegationRecord, including DelegationRecords delegating authority to RT-02 itself. RT-02 must process founding-authority revocations without exception.

## RS-21 — Failure Modes

RT-02 defines the following nine constitutional failure modes. Each failure mode carries the eight required fields per R0 §4.22.

### RT02-FAIL-01: Authority Resolution Failure

**Identifier:** RT02-FAIL-01
**Description:** RT-02 cannot produce a definite AuthorityResolutionResult for a Gate 3 query or authority validation request, due to internal state inconsistency, registry corruption, or RT-02 operational failure.
**Constitutional violation:** RT02-OBL-03 (Gate 3 response obligation); RT02-INV-7 (Gate 3 Response Completeness). D4 §4.3 requires a definite result.
**Detection conditions:** RT-02 cannot locate the AuthorityClaim registry entry for the queried actor and authority type; or the registry state is inconsistent; or RT-02 is in an operational state that prevents response.
**Severity:** CRITICAL — Gate 3 cannot complete; the Class A operation cannot be admitted or rejected constitutionally.
**Affected objects:** RT02-STATE-04 (AuthorityClaim Registry); RT02-STATE-10 (Gate 3 Response Record).
**Containment requirement:** RT-02 must notify RT-03 immediately so RT-03 can reject the pending operation with a constitutional failure record. RT-03 must not leave Gate 3 open indefinitely.
**Recovery requirement:** RT02-REC-01 — Restore AuthorityClaim Registry consistency from DelegationRecord registry; verify chain integrity; re-enter Operational if consistent.

### RT02-FAIL-02: DelegationRecord Chain Break

**Identifier:** RT02-FAIL-02
**Description:** An authority chain traversal reveals a broken link — a DelegationRecord in the chain is missing, is in Revoked state, or creates a cycle (violating D3 GI-4).
**Constitutional violation:** D3 GCR-2 (Authority Chain Completeness); D3 GI-5 (Single Authority Root); RT02-INV-6.
**Detection conditions:** RT02-PROC-06 (chain verification) detects a missing or Revoked intermediate DelegationRecord; or D4 §4.3(c) condition evaluates false.
**Severity:** HIGH — Affected AuthorityClaims cannot be used at Gate 3; actors may be unable to exercise authority until chain is restored.
**Affected objects:** Specific DelegationRecord chain; downstream AuthorityClaims in UnderAuthorityReview state.
**Containment requirement:** Immediately place affected AuthorityClaims UnderAuthorityReview; create AuthorityConflictRecord; notify RT-04.
**Recovery requirement:** RT02-REC-02 — Investigate chain break origin; if chain can be repaired with documented retroactive authorization, restore with full provenance; if irresolvable, place affected claims in Revoked state.

### RT02-FAIL-03: Unauthorized Authority Grant

**Identifier:** RT02-FAIL-03
**Description:** An authority grant request was submitted by an actor who does not hold sufficient authority to delegate the requested authority type, scope, or domain.
**Constitutional violation:** D-2 §X (authority is derived, not assumed); D4 §4.3(b) and (c) (DelegationRecord chain and revocation conditions); RT02-OBL-04 (scope enforcement).
**Detection conditions:** RT02-PROC-01 finds: granting actor's AuthorityClaim does not cover the requested authority type; or granting actor's scope is narrower than the proposed grant; or granting actor's AuthorityClaim is not Active.
**Severity:** HIGH — Unauthorized authority grant rejected; no DelegationRecord created.
**Affected objects:** Proposed (rejected) DelegationRecord; AuthorityConflictRecord created.
**Containment requirement:** Reject the grant; create a rejection record; notify RT-04 that an unauthorized grant was attempted.
**Recovery requirement:** RT02-REC-03 — Record the unauthorized grant attempt with full provenance; notify RT-04; no further action if grant correctly rejected.

### RT02-FAIL-04: Authority Revocation Propagation Failure

**Identifier:** RT02-FAIL-04
**Description:** A revocation event has been recorded but downstream AuthorityClaims have not been transitioned to Revoked state within the constitutional requirement of immediate effect (RT02-INV-5).
**Constitutional violation:** RT02-INV-5 (Immediate Revocation Effect); D4 §4.3 (Revocation propagation); D8 PROH-3 (No Authority Bypass).
**Detection conditions:** RT02-STATE-08 (Revocation Propagation Queue) is non-empty when a Gate 3 query is received; or downstream AuthorityClaims remain Active after a revocation has been recorded.
**Severity:** CRITICAL — Revoked authority may be used to pass Gate 3. Unconstitutional operations may be admitted.
**Affected objects:** All AuthorityClaims downstream of the revoked DelegationRecord; RT02-STATE-08.
**Containment requirement:** Immediately halt all Gate 3 responses until RT02-STATE-08 is drained; RT-03 must be notified that Gate 3 is temporarily suspended.
**Recovery requirement:** RT02-REC-04 — Complete all pending revocation propagations; verify no downstream AuthorityClaim remains Active; drain RT02-STATE-08; resume Gate 3 operations.

### RT02-FAIL-05: AIR Violation

**Identifier:** RT02-FAIL-05
**Description:** An Authority Integrity Rule (AIR-1 through AIR-5) violation has been detected in RT-02's authority records — a condition that violates the constitutional distribution requirements for the five authority types.
**Constitutional violation:** D6 AIR-1 through AIR-5 (whichever rule is violated); RT02-INV-3 (for AIR-5 violations).
**Detection conditions:** RT02-PROC-07 detects: AIR-1 — single actor holding multiple authority types in same domain; AIR-2 — actor crossing Projection Boundary without Projection Authority (detected at Gate 3); AIR-3 — domain falling below two-actor minimum for Observation or Interpretation Authority; AIR-4 — decision without Decision Record (detected via RT-04 monitoring); AIR-5 — Audit Authority held concurrently with any other type in same domain.
**Severity:** HIGH (AIR-1, AIR-3, AIR-4) / CRITICAL (AIR-5 — destroys audit independence).
**Affected objects:** AuthorityConflictRecord created; affected domain's Authority Map; RT02-STATE-05 (AuthorityConflict Register).
**Containment requirement:** Create AuthorityConflictRecord immediately; notify RT-04; for AIR-5 violations: place all affected Audit Authority claims UnderAuthorityReview pending resolution.
**Recovery requirement:** RT02-REC-05 — Identify which authority grants created the AIR violation; revoke the violating grants or restructure authority distribution to restore AIR compliance; RT-04 confirms compliance restored.

### RT02-FAIL-06: Authority Audit Access Denial

**Identifier:** RT02-FAIL-06
**Description:** RT-02 has failed to provide complete authority records to RT-04 in response to an audit query, or RT-02's authority operation log has a gap that prevents RT-04 from reconstructing authority history.
**Constitutional violation:** D6 AIR-5 (Audit Independence — RT-04 must have unconditional access); RT02-OBL audit obligations; D8 IOR-3 (authority history preservation).
**Detection conditions:** RT-04 reports an incomplete or inaccessible authority record; or RT-02 internal log detects a gap in the append-only authority operation log (RT02-STATE-10).
**Severity:** HIGH — Audit independence is compromised; constitutional accountability cannot be verified.
**Affected objects:** RT02-STATE-10 (Gate 3 Response Record); authority operation log; RT-04 audit capability.
**Containment requirement:** Immediately notify RT-04 of the log gap; enter Degraded state; cease all Gate 3 operations until log integrity is restored.
**Recovery requirement:** RT02-REC-06 — Reconstruct the authority operation log from available state (DelegationRecords, RevocationRecords, ConflictRecords); document any irrecoverable gap in a constitutional gap record; RT-04 confirms audit access restored.

### RT02-FAIL-07: Gate Authority Overflow

**Identifier:** RT02-FAIL-07
**Description:** A Gate 3 query presents an authority claim whose scope exceeds the maximum constitutional scope permitted by the DelegationRecord chain — an actor attempting to exercise authority beyond what any link in their chain permits.
**Constitutional violation:** RT02-INV-4 (Scope Containment); D4 §4.3(d) (scope condition); D-2 §X (proportional authorization).
**Detection conditions:** RT02-PROC-02 evaluates D4 §4.3(d) and finds that the claimed scope of the operation exceeds the scope of at least one DelegationRecord in the authority chain.
**Severity:** MEDIUM — The specific Gate 3 query is rejected; the actor's other authority claims are not affected.
**Affected objects:** Specific Gate 3 evaluation; specific DelegationRecord scope; Gate 3 Response Record.
**Containment requirement:** Produce Gate 3 DENIED result specifying §4.3(d) as the failing condition; log in RT02-STATE-10; notify RT-04 if repeated overflow detected for the same actor.
**Recovery requirement:** RT02-REC-07 — No structural recovery needed for individual overflow events; if systematic overflow is detected, RT-04 review of the actor's DelegationRecord chain is warranted.

### RT02-FAIL-08: Actor Identity Mismatch

**Identifier:** RT02-FAIL-08
**Description:** RT-02 receives an authority-related notification (suspension, termination, or grant request) referencing an actor identity that does not correspond to any known ActorProfile in RT-01's registry.
**Constitutional violation:** CC-1 (Identity Resolution Prerequisite); RT02-OBL-10 (actor suspension and termination coordination).
**Detection conditions:** RT-02 queries RT-01 for an ActorProfile and receives no result; or the ActorProfile referenced in an authority grant or revocation does not exist in RT-01's registry.
**Severity:** MEDIUM — The specific operation cannot be processed; no authority is bound or released.
**Affected objects:** Proposed DelegationRecord (rejected); ActorSuspensionNotification or ActorTerminationNotification that cannot be processed.
**Containment requirement:** Reject the operation; create a record of the identity mismatch; notify RT-04.
**Recovery requirement:** RT02-REC-08 — Verify with RT-01 whether the actor identity exists; if it exists, determine why the query failed (RT-01 operational issue); if it does not exist, the notification was constitutionally invalid — record and close.

### RT02-FAIL-09: Invariant Breach

**Identifier:** RT02-FAIL-09
**Description:** An RT-02-specific constitutional invariant (RT02-INV-1 through RT02-INV-10) or D8 system invariant has been breached — a condition that compromises RT-02's constitutional integrity at a fundamental level.
**Constitutional violation:** Whichever RT02-INV or INV is breached.
**Detection conditions:** Internal invariant monitoring detects a condition that violates any RT02-INV-1 through RT02-INV-10; or RT-04 audit detects an invariant breach; or a Gate 3 evaluation reveals an internally inconsistent state.
**Severity:** CRITICAL — Invariant breaches represent fundamental constitutional integrity failures.
**Affected objects:** Depends on which invariant is breached; potentially the entire AuthorityClaim Registry or DelegationRecord registry.
**Containment requirement:** Immediately enter Degraded state; notify RT-04 and RT-03; halt Gate 3 operations if the breach affects authority determination capability.
**Recovery requirement:** RT02-REC-09 — Identify the specific invariant breached; reconstruct the affected state from authoritative sources (DelegationRecord registry for AuthorityClaim inconsistencies; FoundingRatification for chain root issues); RT-04 verifies invariant restoration before return to Operational.

## RS-22 — Recovery Behaviour

RT-02 defines the following nine recovery procedures, each corresponding to one failure mode.

### RT02-REC-01: Authority Resolution Failure Recovery

**Triggered by:** RT02-FAIL-01 (Authority Resolution Failure)
**Constitutional objective:** Restore the AuthorityClaim Registry to a consistent state that permits definite Gate 3 responses.

**Recovery steps:**
1. Notify RT-03 that Gate 3 is suspended for the affected operation stream
2. Reconstruct the AuthorityClaim Registry from the Active DelegationRecord Registry (RT02-STATE-01)
3. Verify that every reconstructed AuthorityClaim has a complete authority chain to FoundingRatification (RT02-PROC-06)
4. Verify AIR-1 through AIR-5 compliance in reconstructed registry (RT02-PROC-07)
5. If reconstruction is complete and consistent: notify RT-03 that Gate 3 is restored; resume Operational state
6. If reconstruction cannot produce a consistent state: escalate to RT02-FAIL-09 (Invariant Breach); notify RT-04

**Recovery obligations:** Full provenance record of the failure and recovery must be logged in the authority operation log; RT-04 notified of the failure and recovery.

**Outcome:** RT-02 returns to Operational with a verified AuthorityClaim Registry; Gate 3 capability restored.

### RT02-REC-02: DelegationRecord Chain Break Recovery

**Triggered by:** RT02-FAIL-02 (DelegationRecord Chain Break)
**Constitutional objective:** Restore authority chain completeness for affected AuthorityClaims, or formally revoke claims that cannot be restored.

**Recovery steps:**
1. Place all affected AuthorityClaims in UnderAuthorityReview state
2. Notify RT-04 of the chain break and the affected claims
3. Traverse the broken chain to identify the specific broken link
4. If the break is due to a revocation that was not propagated: complete the revocation propagation per RT02-PROC-03; move claims to Revoked
5. If the break is due to a missing DelegationRecord that should exist (documentation gap): establish the missing record with retroactive documentation of the original authorization, with RT-04 oversight
6. If the break is irresolvable: move affected AuthorityClaims to Revoked state; create AuthorityRevocationRecords citing chain break as constitutional basis
7. Update Domain Authority Maps
8. RT-04 verifies chain restoration or revocation

**Outcome:** Authority chains are complete for all Active AuthorityClaims; irresolvable chains are revoked with full provenance.

### RT02-REC-03: Unauthorized Authority Grant Recovery

**Triggered by:** RT02-FAIL-03 (Unauthorized Authority Grant)
**Constitutional objective:** Ensure no DelegationRecord is created from an unauthorized grant; ensure the attempt is fully documented.

**Recovery steps:**
1. Reject the proposed DelegationRecord (it remains in rejected state, never created in Draft)
2. Create a rejection record documenting: attempting actor, proposed grant details, specific authority condition not met
3. Notify RT-04 of the unauthorized grant attempt
4. Assess whether repeated unauthorized grant attempts by the same actor warrant authority review of that actor's existing AuthorityClaims

**Outcome:** No unauthorized DelegationRecord created; RT-04 notified; attempt documented.

### RT02-REC-04: Authority Revocation Propagation Failure Recovery

**Triggered by:** RT02-FAIL-04 (Authority Revocation Propagation Failure)
**Constitutional objective:** Drain the revocation propagation queue completely; ensure no revoked authority remains Active.

**Recovery steps:**
1. Immediately halt Gate 3 responses; notify RT-03 that Gate 3 is suspended
2. Process all entries in RT02-STATE-08 (Revocation Propagation Queue) to completion
3. Verify that no AuthorityClaim backed by a Revoked DelegationRecord remains in Active or Suspended state
4. Update Domain Authority Maps to reflect all revocations
5. Notify RT-04 of the propagation failure and its resolution
6. Resume Gate 3 operations

**Outcome:** RT02-STATE-08 empty; all downstream AuthorityClaims in Revoked state; Gate 3 resumed.

### RT02-REC-05: AIR Violation Recovery

**Triggered by:** RT02-FAIL-05 (AIR Violation)
**Constitutional objective:** Restore full compliance with AIR-1 through AIR-5 in all twelve domain authority maps.

**Recovery steps:**
1. Identify the specific AIR rule violated and the specific actors and domain involved
2. For AIR-5 violations (highest severity): immediately place all Audit Authority claims for actors who also hold other authority types in UnderAuthorityReview; notify RT-04 as constitutional emergency
3. For AIR-1 violations: identify which authority type the actor obtained first (or which was most recently granted); the more recent grant may be revoked as the violating grant
4. For AIR-3 violations: identify which actors can be granted Observation or Interpretation Authority to restore the minimum two-actor requirement
5. Process any required revocations through RT02-PROC-03
6. RT-04 verifies AIR compliance restoration across all twelve domain authority maps

**Outcome:** All five AIR rules satisfied in all twelve domain authority maps; RT-04 verification confirmed.

### RT02-REC-06: Authority Audit Access Denial Recovery

**Triggered by:** RT02-FAIL-06 (Authority Audit Access Denial)
**Constitutional objective:** Restore RT-04's unconditional access to complete authority records.

**Recovery steps:**
1. Identify the gap in the authority operation log (RT02-STATE-10)
2. Reconstruct the missing log entries from available state: DelegationRecord creation timestamps, RevocationRecord timestamps, ConflictRecord timestamps
3. If reconstruction is complete: restore the log; notify RT-04 of the gap origin and reconstruction
4. If reconstruction is incomplete: create a constitutional gap record documenting the irrecoverable provenance gap; notify RT-04
5. Resume RT-04 audit access

**Outcome:** RT-04 audit access restored; log integrity confirmed or gap formally documented.

### RT02-REC-07: Gate Authority Overflow Recovery

**Triggered by:** RT02-FAIL-07 (Gate Authority Overflow)
**Constitutional objective:** Document the overflow event; assess whether systematic intervention is needed.

**Recovery steps:**
1. The Gate 3 DENIED result has already been issued — the specific operation is rejected
2. Log the overflow event in the authority operation log
3. Assess whether the same actor has triggered multiple overflow events: if yes, notify RT-04 for authority chain review
4. No further structural recovery required for individual overflow events

**Outcome:** Overflow documented; Gate 3 DENIED confirmed; systematic review triggered if repeated.

### RT02-REC-08: Actor Identity Mismatch Recovery

**Triggered by:** RT02-FAIL-08 (Actor Identity Mismatch)
**Constitutional objective:** Resolve the identity mismatch and ensure authority records remain consistent with RT-01.

**Recovery steps:**
1. Query RT-01 for the actor identity referenced in the failing operation
2. If RT-01 confirms the identity exists: determine why RT-02's query failed (transient RT-01 query failure); retry the operation
3. If RT-01 confirms the identity does not exist: the notification was constitutionally invalid; create a mismatch record; notify RT-04
4. If RT-01 is unreachable: RT-02 must defer the authority operation until RT-01 is reachable; no authority is bound or released on the basis of an unresolved identity

**Outcome:** Identity mismatch resolved; authority operations correctly processed or rejected with documentation.

### RT02-REC-09: Invariant Breach Recovery

**Triggered by:** RT02-FAIL-09 (Invariant Breach)
**Constitutional objective:** Restore all violated invariants; verify complete constitutional integrity before returning to Operational.

**Recovery steps:**
1. Enter Degraded state if not already; notify RT-03 and RT-04
2. Identify which specific RT02-INV is breached
3. For RT02-INV-1 (DelegationRecord Completeness): reconstruct any missing DelegationRecords from audit records; if irresolvable, revoke affected claims
4. For RT02-INV-2 (No Unrecorded Authority): identify and revoke any orphaned AuthorityClaims
5. For RT02-INV-3 (Audit Authority Independence): emergency AIR-5 restoration per RT02-REC-05
6. For RT02-INV-4 (Scope Containment): review and revoke any out-of-scope AuthorityClaims
7. For RT02-INV-5 (Immediate Revocation Effect): drain revocation queue per RT02-REC-04
8. For RT02-INV-6 (Authority Chain Completeness): chain break recovery per RT02-REC-02
9. For RT02-INV-7 through RT02-INV-10: specific remediation per the violated invariant
10. RT-04 performs independent invariant verification across all RT-02 state registries
11. Upon RT-04 confirmation: return to Operational

**Outcome:** All RT02-INV invariants satisfied; RT-04 verification confirmed; RT-02 returns to Operational.

## RS-24 — Validation Requirements

### VC-01 — Authority Chain Completeness Validation

**Criterion:** For every AuthorityClaim in Active state in RT-02's registry, the DelegationRecord chain from that claim to FoundingRatification must be complete, acyclic, and unrevoked.
**Constitutional basis:** D3 GCR-2; D3 GI-5; RT02-INV-6.
**Measurement:** Every Active AuthorityClaim is auditable to FoundingRatification through an unbroken chain of Active, unrevoked DelegationRecords.
**Validated by:** RT02-PROC-06 continuous monitoring; RT-04 independent verification.

### VC-02 — Gate 3 Response Completeness Validation

**Criterion:** Every Gate 3 query received by RT-02 must produce a definite CONFIRMED or DENIED AuthorityResolutionResult within the Gate 3 timing window specified by RT-03.
**Constitutional basis:** D4 §4.3; RT02-INV-7; RT02-OBL-03.
**Measurement:** RT02-STATE-10 must contain a Gate 3 Response Record for every Gate 3 query received. No unanswered Gate 3 query may exist.
**Validated by:** RT-04 cross-checks RT02-STATE-10 entries against RT-03's Gate 3 operation records.

### VC-03 — AIR-5 Audit Independence Validation

**Criterion:** No actor in any of the twelve constitutional domains holds both Audit Authority and any other authority type (Observation, Interpretation, Decision, or Projection) for that same domain.
**Constitutional basis:** D6 AIR-5; RT02-INV-3; RT02-OBL-07.
**Measurement:** For each of the twelve domains, the set of actors holding Audit Authority must be disjoint from the set of actors holding any other authority type in that domain.
**Validated by:** RT02-PROC-07 at every AuthorityClaim Registry update; RT-04 independent verification.

### VC-04 — Immediate Revocation Effect Validation

**Criterion:** At any Gate 3 evaluation point, no AuthorityClaim backed solely by a Revoked DelegationRecord must be in Active state.
**Constitutional basis:** RT02-INV-5; D4 §4.3(b); D8 PROH-3.
**Measurement:** RT02-STATE-08 (Revocation Propagation Queue) is empty at every Gate 3 evaluation. Cross-check: no AuthorityClaim is Active whose sole backing DelegationRecord is Revoked.
**Validated by:** RT02-PROC-02 pre-check; RT-04 spot audit of revocation records vs. AuthorityClaim states.

### VC-05 — Scope Containment Validation

**Criterion:** Every AuthorityClaim in Active state must be bounded by the scope of its narrowest backing DelegationRecord. No AuthorityClaim may assert scope beyond what its DelegationRecord chain permits at any link.
**Constitutional basis:** RT02-INV-4; D4 §4.3(d); D-2 §X.
**Measurement:** For any Gate 3 evaluation, the scope of the proposed operation must not exceed the scope of the narrowest DelegationRecord in the chain.
**Validated by:** RT02-PROC-02 evaluation of D4 §4.3(d); RT-04 scope audit.

### VC-06 — Domain Authority Map Currency Validation

**Criterion:** Each of the twelve domain authority maps in RT02-STATE-06 must reflect the current AuthorityClaim Registry state at all times during Operational phase.
**Constitutional basis:** RT02-INV-8; RT02-OBL-12.
**Measurement:** The domain authority map for any domain must be updated within the same constitutional processing cycle as any AuthorityClaim state change affecting that domain.
**Validated by:** RT-04 cross-check of domain authority maps against AuthorityClaim Registry state.

### VC-07 — No Unrecorded Authority Validation

**Criterion:** Every actor who exercises constitutional authority in the APEX system must have a corresponding Active AuthorityClaim in RT-02's registry, backed by an Active DelegationRecord.
**Constitutional basis:** RT02-INV-2; D-2 §X; D3 RF-A7.
**Measurement:** RT-04 can trace every authority-bearing operation in the Reality Fabric to a specific DelegationRecord in RT-02's registry.
**Validated by:** RT-04 authority trace audit against fabric operations.

### VC-08 — Founding Authority Record Immutability Validation

**Criterion:** The FoundingAuthorityRecord (RT02-STATE-07) must be identical to its state at FoundingRatification throughout the current civilizational era.
**Constitutional basis:** RT02-INV-9; D3 GI-5; D4 §4.3 (FoundingRatification exception applies to exactly one object per era).
**Measurement:** Hash-equivalent (constitutional comparison, not cryptographic) of FoundingAuthorityRecord against the founding ceremony record.
**Validated by:** RT-04 founding record verification audit.

**Related invariant:** CC-1; RT02-OBL-10.

### RT02-MET-09: Unauthorized Grant Attempt Rate

**Metric:** The count of AuthorityGrantRequests (RT02-IN-01) that were rejected due to insufficient delegation authority (RT02-FAIL-03) per constitutional period.
**Constitutional requirement:** No specific constitutional threshold — this metric is primarily for RT-04 awareness. Elevated rates warrant RT-04 investigation of the actors generating unauthorized grant attempts.
**Measured by:** RT02-PROC-01 rejection records.
**Related invariant:** RT02-INV-2; D-2 §X.

### RT02-MET-10: FoundingAuthorityRecord Integrity

**Metric:** A binary metric: the FoundingAuthorityRecord (RT02-STATE-07) is either identical to its FoundingRatification state or it is not.
**Constitutional requirement:** Always IDENTICAL — any modification is an RT02-INV-9 violation and a constitutional crisis.
**Measured by:** RT-04 independent verification of FoundingAuthorityRecord against founding ceremony record.

### RS-26.3 — RT-01 Mediation Relationship

- Existing AuthorityClaims derived from previously resolved actor identities remain valid
- Gate 3 authority validation for existing claims proceeds normally
- New authority grants cannot be processed (no new identity resolution available)
- Actor lifecycle notifications (suspension, termination) cannot be received — RT-02 must flag this as a Degraded state condition
- RT-03 is notified that new authority grants are unavailable pending RT-01 restoration

This behavior derives from A0 §3.3 and D4 §2.1 (Kernel Mediation Principle) — Class A operations continue to process through Gate 3 for actors with existing valid AuthorityClaims; new authority binding requires RT-01 restoration.

## RS-35 — Prohibited Responsibilities

RT-02 is constitutionally prohibited from performing the following responsibilities. These prohibitions are absolute — no operational context, performance requirement, or constitutional emergency creates an exception.

### RT02-PROH-01: Creating Actor Identities

RT-02 must never create, modify, or terminate ActorProfile records. Identity is the exclusive domain of RT-01. RT-02 consumes identity records produced by RT-01 but may not produce them.

*Constitutional basis:* D8 INV-3 (Authority Separation); D-2 §IX (identity) and §X (authority) as distinct constitutional categories.

### RT02-PROH-02: Making Civilizational Decisions

RT-02 must never make civilizational decisions, form CivilizationalDecision records, or exercise Decision Authority on behalf of any actor. RT-02 validates Decision Authority — it does not exercise it.

*Constitutional basis:* D-2 §X (authority is exercised by the holder, not the validator); A0 §3.12 (RT-12 exclusive responsibility for CivilizationalDecision formation).

### RT02-PROH-03: Executing Actions in External Reality

RT-02 must never execute Action Projections or any operation in external reality. Execution is the domain of RT-13 under Projection Authority validation from RT-02. RT-02 validates authority to project — it does not project.

*Constitutional basis:* D-2 §XI (agency requires constitutional authority grounding); A0 §3.13 (RT-13 exclusive responsibility for Action Projection).

### RT02-PROH-04: Modifying Constitutional Authority Definitions

RT-02 must never modify the definitions of Observation, Interpretation, Decision, Projection, or Audit Authority. These definitions are established in D6 Part 4 and may only be changed through founding-level constitutional amendment via RT-16.

*Constitutional basis:* D8 PROH-2 (No Category Redefinition); D7 (Amendment Authority Architecture — only founding-level authority may amend constitutional definitions).

### RT02-PROH-05: Bypassing RT-03 Gate Processing

RT-02 must never admit DelegationRecords or any other constitutional object to the Reality Fabric directly. All Class A constitutional objects — including authority records — are admitted through RT-03 gate processing. RT-02 produces DelegationRecords; RT-03 processes them.

*Constitutional basis:* D4 §2.1 (Kernel Mediation Principle); D8 PROH-3 (No Authority Bypass applied to RT-02's own operations).

### RT02-PROH-06: Granting Authority to Itself

RT-02 must never create a DelegationRecord that grants authority to RT-02 itself. RT-02 cannot self-authorize. RT-02's own constitutional function is defined by FoundingRatification — not by any DelegationRecord that RT-02 could produce.

*Constitutional basis:* D-2 §X (authority is derived, not assumed — self-authorization is the paradigm case of assumed authority); D8 PROH-3; D8 PROH-8.

### RT02-PROH-07: Initiating Any Action Toward RT-04

RT-02 must never initiate any action, notification, alert, or information transfer toward RT-04. RT-04 reads RT-02's records. The direction of the relationship is unconditionally inbound from RT-04's perspective. RT-02 may not pre-notify, warn, or condition RT-04 on any RT-02 internal event.

*Constitutional basis:* D6 AIR-5 (Audit Independence); A1 PAIR 05 (RT-02 → RT-04: FORBIDDEN).

### RT02-PROH-08: Performing Constitutional Gate Enforcement

RT-02 must never enforce constitutional gates, generate RejectionRecords, or execute any Stage 8-9 atomic commit. Gate enforcement is exclusively RT-03's constitutional function. RT-02 provides the authority determination that informs Gate 3 — it does not apply the gate.

*Constitutional basis:* D8 INV-3 (Authority Separation — authority determination and constitutional enforcement are distinct); A0 §3.4 (RT-03 exclusive gate enforcement responsibility).

### RT02-PROH-09: Suppressing or Filtering RT-04 Audit Access

RT-02 must never provide RT-04 with filtered, redacted, summarized, or otherwise incomplete authority records in response to an audit query. RT-04's access to RT-02's complete authority records is unconditional.

*Constitutional basis:* D6 AIR-5 (Audit Independence); D8 PROH-8 (No Hidden Authority Pathways); A1 PAIR 05.

### RT02-PROH-10: Performing Identity Resolution

RT-02 must never perform identity resolution, maintain identity records, or substitute its own identity determination for RT-01's. When RT-01 is unavailable, RT-02 defers new authority binding — it does not perform identity verification independently.

*Constitutional basis:* D8 INV-3 (Authority Separation); A1 PAIR 01 (identity resolution is RT-01's constitutional function).
