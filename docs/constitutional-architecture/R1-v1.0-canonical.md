## PREAMBLE

R1 is the first individual runtime specification in the R-series. It specifies RT-01 — the Identity and Actor Registration Runtime — in complete conformance with the Runtime Specification Standard established in R0.

R1 derives exclusively from D-2 through D8, A0, A1, and R0. Every claim in this document traces to at least one of those canonical sources. R1 introduces no new constitutional concepts, no new object types, no new authority assignments, and no new runtime relationships beyond those established in the canonical record. R1 translates that canonical record into the mandatory 36-section specification format defined in R0 Part 4.

RT-01 occupies the most foundational position in the constitutional architecture. It is the first runtime processed at Gate 1 of every Class A operation. It has no runtime dependencies — no prior runtime must have produced output before RT-01 can be constitutionally operational. Every other runtime depends upon RT-01 for identity resolution, making RT-01 the single constitutional substrate beneath all other operations.

The core constitutional function of RT-01 is identity — and identity alone. RT-01 answers one question: "Who is this actor, and is this actor constitutionally present?" It does not answer what that actor may do (RT-02), whether that actor's request will be admitted (RT-03), or what that request means (RT-09 through RT-11). The separation of identity from authority, from enforcement, from understanding, and from action is a constitutional requirement, not an architectural preference. This separation is mandated by D4 (Gate 1 as a distinct gate from Gate 3), by D6 (AIR-1 through AIR-5 as distinct authority types), and by D8 §9.2 (the Identity Layer as a mandatory, distinct MVCS layer). R1 enforces this separation rigorously throughout.

This document is the first half of the complete R1 specification. It covers RS-01 through RS-17: runtime identity, constitutional basis, purpose, scope, responsibility, authority, ownership, inputs, outputs, managed objects, managed state, internal processes, external interactions, runtime lifecycle, state machine, entry conditions, and exit conditions. The second half of R1 will complete RS-18 through RS-36.

## RS-03 — PURPOSE

### RS-03.1 Constitutional Purpose Statement

RT-01 exists to establish and maintain constitutional identity throughout the civilization. Identity is the foundational substrate without which no other constitutional operation can occur. Every Class A operation begins with identity resolution (D4 Gate 1). Every authority record is bound to an identity (D6 Part 4). Every provenance chain anchors to an actor with a known identity (D8 INV-2). Every Observation Record names the observer whose identity is constitutionally established by RT-01 (D5 Part 3 §PI-10). Every accountability attribution references an actor registered in RT-01 (D-2 §VIII, D8 IOR-1).

RT-01's constitutional purpose, stated precisely from A0 §3.2: to resolve and maintain constitutional identities for all actors and external entities, providing the foundational identity substrate on which every other constitutional operation depends.

### RS-03.2 Uniqueness Argument

No other runtime fulfills RT-01's constitutional function. The uniqueness argument proceeds by A0 §2.2 survivorship criterion:

**RT-02 cannot absorb RT-01.** D4 separates Gate 1 (identity) from Gate 3 (authority) as constitutionally distinct gates. Collapsing identity into authority would require a single runtime to simultaneously hold AIR-1 (Observation/Identity authority) and the authority assignment function (AIR-1 through AIR-5 grants), creating a constitutional category collapse where the entity registering authority also holds the authority records. D8 INV-3 (Authority Separation) prohibits this configuration.

**RT-03 cannot absorb RT-01.** The Kernel cannot own the identity substrate it gates against — this would require the Kernel to evaluate its own records at Gate 1, a circularity that D4's gate sequence constitutionally prohibits. The Kernel holds enforcement authority; RT-01 holds identity authority. These cannot co-locate without the Kernel becoming the judge of the very records it is supposed to neutrally gate.

**RT-04 cannot absorb RT-01.** RT-04 holds AIR-5 (supreme audit authority) and must be operationally independent of all runtimes it audits (D6 §3.4). If RT-04 held identity records, it would become operationally essential to every constitutional operation — destroying the independence that D6 §3.4 mandates.

No other runtime has any basis for absorbing identity. RT-05 manages object state; RT-06 manages events; RT-07 manages temporal records — none involves registration and resolution of actor constitutional identity. No combination of other runtimes can produce the identity function without either duplicating RT-01's required state or violating a constitutional boundary.

### RS-03.3 Constitutional Gap Statement

If RT-01 were removed from the constitutional architecture, the following specific D-series obligations would become unfulfillable:

1. **D4 Gate 1** would have no identity source. Every Class A operation would fail at Gate 1 with no recovery path. The constitutional enforcement sequence would be non-functional in its entirety.
2. **D6 Part 4 authority binding** would lose its anchor. DelegationRecords could not be bound to verified identities. All authority becomes unverifiable.
3. **D8 IOR-1 (Identity Substrate)** would be uninstantiated. No implementation could satisfy the MVCS Identity Layer requirement (D8 §9.2).
4. **D8 INV-2 (Provenance Preservation)** would be unsatisfiable. Provenance chains require constitutionally registered actors as anchors; without RT-01, no actor is constitutionally registered.
5. **D5 Part 3 §PI-10 (Observer Identity)** would be unenforceable. Observation Records would be unattributed, violating the Observation Projection Lifecycle.
6. **D-2 §IX (Three-Layer Identity Model)** would be unrealized. The entire constitutional identity architecture — structural immutability, semantic history, referential monitoring — would be absent.

RT-01's removal is not a degradation of capability. It is a constitutional collapse. The architecture cannot produce any constitutionally valid output without it.

## RS-04 — SCOPE

### RS-04.1 In-Scope Constitutional Domain

RT-01's constitutional scope encompasses exactly the following:

1. **ActorProfile management:** Creation, maintenance, state transition, suspension, and termination of ActorProfile records for every constitutional actor.
2. **ExternalReference management:** Creation, maintenance, and lifecycle management of ExternalReference records for every external entity the civilization constitutionally references.
3. **Identity resolution:** Provision of IdentityResolutionResults to all runtimes that require identity confirmation — specifically Gate 1 (RT-03), authority binding (RT-02), observer attribution (RT-08), object ownership attribution (RT-05), epistemic attribution (RT-09 through RT-11), compliance verification (RT-12), action attribution (RT-13), feedback attribution (RT-14), domain governance (RT-15), and amendment attribution (RT-16).
4. **Three-layer identity maintenance:** Independent management of the structural (immutable), semantic (mutable), and referential (fragile) layers of every ActorProfile and ExternalReference per D-2 §IX.
5. **Identity conflict detection and recording:** Production of IdentityConflictRecords when identity ambiguity or contradiction is detected.
6. **Identity history and provenance:** Maintenance of the complete, append-only history of all identity records with full provenance chains per D8 INV-2.
7. **Entity-end recording:** Recording of actor termination as a first-class constitutional state (IdentityEndRecord), never as deletion.
8. **Founding membership registry:** Maintenance of the constitutional root identity — the founding actor identities from which all authority chains and constitutional legitimacy originate.
9. **Suspension Notice application:** Application of every Suspension Notice received from RT-03, immediately transitioning the referenced ActorProfile from Active to Suspended.
10. **Dual identity management:** Management of internal actors (who operate within the civilization's constitutional system as ActorProfiles) and external actors (who interact with the civilization from outside the Projection Boundary, referenced as ExternalReferences registered via RT-08).

### RS-04.3 Singleton Statement

RT-01 is a singleton runtime — exactly one instance exists in the constitutional architecture (A0 §2.4, Tier 1). Identity is a civilization-wide function; multiple independent identity registries would create irreconcilable constitutional conflicts about which registry is authoritative for Gate 1. The founding membership record, as the constitutional root of all authority chains, requires a single, unambiguous point of origin. No architectural pattern may distribute or replicate RT-01 across multiple independent instances.

### RS-04.4 Tier Membership Statement

RT-01 is a Tier 1 runtime (Constitutional Infrastructure, A0 §3.1). Tier 1 runtimes (RT-01, RT-02, RT-03, RT-04) share the following constitutional characteristics: they provide services consumed by every other tier; they must be constitutionally operational before any other runtime can perform Class A operations; their failure suspends the civilization rather than degrading it, because no constitutional operation can proceed without them; they receive audit from RT-04 but are not subject to gating by each other except through RT-03's universal Class A gate authority.

RT-01's Tier 1 membership means: RT-01 must be constitutionally operational before RT-02 can bind authority to any actor, before RT-03 can process Gate 1 for any Class A operation, before any epistemic or domain runtime can attribute any operation to a constitutionally verified actor. RT-01's operational status is the first constitutional precondition for civilization operation.

## RS-05 — RESPONSIBILITY

RT-01 is constitutionally obligated to fulfill the following responsibilities. All are Core obligations (non-suspendable) unless explicitly classified otherwise. The exclusivity rule (R0 §3.2) applies: no other runtime shares any of these responsibilities.

### RT01-OBL-01: ActorProfile Registry Maintenance

**Obligation:** RT-01 is constitutionally obligated to maintain the authoritative, complete, and current ActorProfile registry for all constitutional actors.

**Constitutional source:** A0 §3.2 Responsibility 1; D8 §9.2; D-2 §IX.

**Classification:** Core.

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** D4 Gate 1 cannot resolve identity for any Class A operation. The entire constitutional enforcement sequence fails. No constitutional operation can be admitted to the Reality Fabric.

### RT01-OBL-02: ExternalReference Registry Maintenance

**Obligation:** RT-01 is constitutionally obligated to maintain the authoritative, complete, and current ExternalReference registry for all external entities the civilization constitutionally references.

**Constitutional source:** A0 §3.2 Responsibility 2; D8 §4.1 (ExternalReference as canonical object type); D5 Part 3.

**Classification:** Core.

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** External actors cannot be constitutionally referenced. Observation Records cannot name external observers. Correspondent registrations are constitutionally void.

### RT01-OBL-03: Identity Resolution for Gate 1

**Obligation:** RT-01 is constitutionally obligated to resolve identity for every constitutional operation that reaches Gate 1, returning a definite result — ACTIVE, SUSPENDED, TERMINATED, or UNKNOWN — before Gate 1 can complete.

**Constitutional source:** A0 §3.2 Responsibility 3; D4 §3.3 Gate 1; D8 CLI-1; A1 PAIR 02.

**Classification:** Core.

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Gate 1 cannot complete. RT-03 cannot proceed to Gate 2. Every Class A operation submitted while RT-01 fails identity resolution is rejected with Gate 1 REJECTED grounds. If RT-01 is completely unavailable, all Class A operations enter REJECTED state and no constitutional activity proceeds.

### RT01-OBL-04: Identity Resolution for All Dependent Runtimes

**Obligation:** RT-01 is constitutionally obligated to provide IdentityResolutionResults on demand to RT-02, RT-03, RT-05, RT-08, RT-09, RT-10, RT-11, RT-12, RT-13, RT-14, RT-15, and RT-16.

**Constitutional source:** A0 §3.2 Responsibility 8; A1 PAIR 01, 02, 07, 10; D6 Part 4.

**Classification:** Core.

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Dependent runtimes cannot associate operations with constitutional actors. Authority binding, object ownership attribution, epistemic chain attribution, compliance verification, and action attribution all become constitutionally void.

### RT01-OBL-05: Three-Layer Identity Model Enforcement

**Obligation:** RT-01 is constitutionally obligated to maintain the three-layer identity model for every ActorProfile and ExternalReference: structural layer is immutable once established; semantic layer is mutable with complete history preservation; referential layer is fragile with active fragility monitoring.

**Constitutional source:** D-2 §IX; D1 §Domain II (StructuralAnchor, SemanticProfile, ReferentialLink); A0 §3.2 Responsibility 4.

**Classification:** Core.

**Consequence of non-fulfillment:** Constitutional identity loses its three-layer integrity. Structural modifications (which are prohibited) cannot be detected. Semantic history is lost. Referential fragility is unmonitored. D3 RF-A5 (identity stability requirement) is violated.

### RT01-OBL-06: Identity Conflict Detection and Recording

**Obligation:** RT-01 is constitutionally obligated to detect and record every identity conflict — any condition in which the identity of an actor or external entity is ambiguous, disputed, or contradictory — as an IdentityConflictRecord immediately upon detection.

## RS-18 — Preconditions

### RS-18.1 — Class A Operation Preconditions

All Class A operations (identity registration, state mutation, authority grant) must satisfy the following preconditions before execution may begin. Failure to satisfy any precondition results in the appropriate RT01-FAIL-NN failure mode, and execution does not proceed.

**PRE-A-01 — Gate 1 Authorization Present**
Constitutional source: D4 §3.3 (Gate 1); A1 PAIR 02
The initiating request must carry a Gate 1 authorization token issued by RT-03. RT-01 does not evaluate the token's internal content; RT-03 is the sole authority that validates it. RT-01's precondition obligation is to confirm the token's structural presence and verify its provenance claim references RT-03.

**PRE-A-02 — Identity Resolution Completed**
Constitutional source: D4 §3.3 CC-1; A1 PAIR 02
The initiating actor's identity must resolve to an active ActorProfile record before any Class A operation executes. An ActorProfile in Candidate, Suspended, or Terminated state does not satisfy this precondition for operations requiring Active status.

**PRE-A-03 — Kernel Mediation Active**
Constitutional source: D4 §2.1 (Kernel Mediation Principle); A1 PAIR 02
RT-03 must be operationally active and reachable. RT-01 does not evaluate RT-03's internal state; it verifies that the mediation channel is present. If RT-03 is unreachable, Class A operations are suspended pending RT-03 restoration per RT01-OBL-11.

**PRE-A-04 — Provenance Chain Intact**
Constitutional source: D8 INV-2; D8 INV-5
The existing provenance chain for the target ActorProfile must be verifiably intact. If the chain contains a gap, corruption, or unresolvable reference, RT-01 must refuse the operation and emit RT01-FAIL-02.

**PRE-A-05 — State Transition Legality**
Constitutional source: D8 §4.2; RS-14; RT01-INV-1
The requested state transition must be a legal transition as defined in RS-14.1 and RS-14.2. RT-01 must evaluate the requested transition against the legal transition matrix before execution. Illegal transitions result in RT01-FAIL-04.

**PRE-A-06 — No Concurrent Modification Lock**
Constitutional source: D8 INV-5; RT01-INV-3
No concurrent modification lock may be held on the target ActorProfile record. If a lock is active, the operation must queue or return RT01-FAIL-05 depending on the operation subtype. RT-01 never proceeds with Class A operations against a locked record.

**PRE-A-07 — Founding Record Protection**
Constitutional source: A0 §3.2 RT01-INV-6; D3 §6.1
If the operation targets the FoundingMembershipRecord, the request must carry a founding-level authorization attestation issued under RT-16 amendment authority. Absence of this attestation causes immediate rejection per RT01-INV-5.

### RS-18.2 — Class B Operation Preconditions

Class B operations (identity queries, audit output provision, cross-runtime read responses) require the following preconditions:

**PRE-B-01 — Requesting Runtime Authorized**
Constitutional source: A1 §13.2 (permission matrix)
The requesting runtime must appear in the A1 §13.2 permission matrix as authorized to receive RT-01 identity data. Requests from runtimes not listed in the permission matrix are rejected per RT01-FAIL-08.

**PRE-B-02 — Query Scope Within RT-01 Domain**
Constitutional source: D4 §2.3 (domain authority); RS-04.1 (AIR-1)

**PRE-B-03 — Audit Access Is Unconditional for RT-04**
Constitutional source: D6 §3.4; AIR-5; A1 PAIR 03

**PRE-B-04 — ExternalReference Source Verification**
Constitutional source: D8 §4.1 (ExternalReference); A1 PAIR 10
When a Class B operation involves an ExternalReference record, the request must originate from RT-08 or be mediated through RT-03. Direct external-source Class B requests not mediated through constitutional channels are rejected per RT01-FAIL-08.

### RS-18.3 — Precondition Evaluation Sequence

## RS-19 — Postconditions

### RS-19.1 — Class A Operation Postconditions

Upon successful completion of any Class A operation, the following postconditions must hold:

**POST-A-01 — ActorProfile State Consistency**
Constitutional source: D8 INV-1; RT01-INV-1
The ActorProfile's current state must match exactly one state from the canonical state set {Candidate, Active, Suspended, Terminated} and must be the state reached by the legal transition just executed. No ambiguous or intermediate states may persist after operation completion.

**POST-A-02 — Provenance Entry Appended**
Constitutional source: D8 INV-2; RT01-INV-2; D8 PROH-3
A new provenance log entry must have been appended to the ActorProfile's provenance chain. The entry records: operation identifier, temporal attestation, initiating actor identity, Gate 1 authorization reference, and resulting state. This entry is immutable from the moment of append.

**POST-A-03 — Audit Event Emitted**
Constitutional source: D6 §3.4; RT01-OBL-09; A1 PAIR 03; A1 PAIR 08
An identity event record must have been emitted to RT-06 (Observation Capture) and made available to RT-04 (Audit Authority). The event must contain sufficient information to reconstruct the full operation without referencing RT-01's internal state.

**POST-A-04 — No Record Deletion**
Constitutional source: D8 PROH-4; RT01-INV-4
The total count of ActorProfile records must be equal to or greater than the pre-operation count. No record may have been removed. Terminated records remain; they are not deleted.

**POST-A-05 — Concurrent Modification Lock Released**
Constitutional source: D8 INV-5; RT01-INV-3
Any concurrent modification lock held during the operation must be fully released. No lock may persist after operation completion.

**POST-A-06 — Downstream Notifications Dispatched**
Constitutional source: A1 PAIRs 01, 02, 07
All runtimes with constitutional notification obligations must have received their post-operation notifications: RT-02 (authorization surface change notification, if the operation altered an actor's authorization surface), RT-03 (gate state update notification, if applicable), RT-05 (governance record, if a governance-relevant change occurred).

### RS-19.2 — Class B Operation Postconditions

Upon successful completion of any Class B operation:

**POST-B-01 — Read Consistency**
Constitutional source: D8 INV-1; RT01-SPEC-INV-3
The data returned must reflect the ActorProfile's state as of the moment the read operation was initiated. No partial or transitional state may be returned. Two identical queries at the same logical time must return identical results.

**POST-B-02 — Audit Access Completion Recorded**
Constitutional source: D6 §3.4; AIR-5; RT01-OBL-12
When the Class B operation was an RT-04 audit access, the access completion must be recorded in RT-01's internal audit access log. RT-04 access is not logged to RT-06 (that would violate the constitutional function separation between observation and audit); it is recorded internally per RT01-OBL-12.

**POST-B-03 — ExternalReference Integrity Confirmed**
Constitutional source: D8 §4.1; A1 PAIR 10
When the Class B operation involved an ExternalReference record, the returned data must include the monitoring flag status and validity period. Stale or expired ExternalReference records must be flagged before return per RT01-OBL-08.

### RS-19.3 — Postcondition Verification Responsibility

RT-01 is responsible for verifying all postconditions before issuing operation completion acknowledgments. If any postcondition is violated:

1. RT-01 emits RT01-FAIL-02 (provenance violation) or RT01-FAIL-09 (invariant breach) as appropriate
2. RT-01 initiates recovery per RS-22
3. RT-01 does not issue a completion acknowledgment until the violation is resolved or RT-03 has been notified

## RS-20 — Invariants

### RS-20.1 — D8 System Invariants: RT-01 Applicability

The following invariants are defined in D8 §5 and apply directly to RT-01's operation:

**D8-INV-1 — Single Canonical Identity Record**
Each actor in the system must be represented by exactly one ActorProfile record at any given time. RT-01 applicability: RT-01 is the constitutional guardian of this invariant. Every registration operation (RT01-PROC-01) must check for pre-existing records before creating a new one. Duplicate detection failure constitutes a critical invariant breach triggering RT01-FAIL-09.

**D8-INV-2 — Provenance Preservation**
The complete history of every identity event must be preserved in an append-only provenance log. RT-01 applicability: RT-01 appends to this log on every Class A operation (POST-A-02). RT-01 may never overwrite or remove provenance entries. The provenance log is owned artifact RT01-OWN-04.

**D8-INV-3 — Three-Layer Identity Coherence**
Every ActorProfile must at all times contain a coherent three-layer identity structure: structural layer (immutable from D-2 §IX), semantic layer (mutable with history), referential layer (fragile with monitoring). RT-01 applicability: All mutation operations must preserve coherence across all three layers. Semantic mutations that break structural layer integrity violate this invariant.

**D8-INV-4 — Authorization Surface Currency**
The authorization surface exposed to RT-02 must accurately reflect the current state of all ActorProfile records. RT-01 applicability: Every state change must trigger RT-01's notification to RT-02 (RT01-OUT-08; A1 PAIR 01). Stale authorization surface data in RT-02 attributable to RT-01's failure to notify constitutes a D8-INV-4 violation.

**D8-INV-5 — Monotonic Identity History**
The identity history must grow monotonically. No events may be removed, backdated, or reordered. RT-01 applicability: RT-01's provenance log (RT01-OWN-04) must be strictly append-only. Temporal attestation from RT-07 (A1 PAIR 09) provides the constitutional ordering guarantee; RT-01 must record this attestation in every provenance entry.

**D8-INV-6 — Projection Boundary Separation**
Internal identity records must never be directly exposed outside the Projection Boundary. External entities interact only through ExternalReference objects. RT-01 applicability: RT-01 must never emit raw ActorProfile records as outputs. All external-facing outputs (RT01-OUT-01 through RT01-OUT-06) are projections, not raw records. Raw record exposure violates RS-32.4.

**D8-INV-7 — Terminated State Finality**
A Terminated ActorProfile may never transition to any other state. Termination is the irreversible terminal state. RT-01 applicability: RT-01's state transition enforcement must reject any request targeting a Terminated ActorProfile for state change. This invariant admits no exceptions regardless of requesting authority.

### RS-20.2 — A0 Runtime Invariants

The following invariants are defined in A0 §3.2 for RT-01:

**RT01-INV-1 — Legal Transition Enforcement**
RT-01 must enforce the legal transition matrix defined in RS-14.1 at all times. No operation may cause a state transition not present in the legal transition matrix, regardless of the operation's initiating authority. The legal transition matrix is constitutional and may not be overridden by any runtime.

**RT01-INV-2 — Append-Only Identity Log**
RT-01's identity provenance log (RT01-OWN-04) must be strictly append-only. No log entry may be modified, deleted, or reordered after creation. Every Class A operation must produce exactly one new log entry carrying a temporal attestation from RT-07.

**RT01-INV-3 — Single Authority Source**
RT-01 is the sole constitutional source of identity records within the APEX system boundary. No other runtime may create, modify, or terminate an ActorProfile without routing through RT-01. Requests that bypass RT-01 violate this invariant regardless of their stated authority level.

**RT01-INV-4 — No Identity Record Deletion**
ActorProfile records are never deleted. The only terminal operation is transitioning to Terminated state, which itself creates a provenance entry. D8 PROH-4 is the D8-layer source; RT01-INV-4 is its runtime instantiation.

**RT01-INV-5 — Founding Record Immutability**
The FoundingMembershipRecord may only be modified through a constitutional amendment process initiated by RT-16 with founding-level authorization. RT-01 enforces this by requiring founding-level authorization attestation (PRE-A-07) for any operation targeting the FoundingMembershipRecord.

**RT01-INV-6 — Audit Transparency**
RT-01 must never resist, delay, condition, or obstruct RT-04's audit access. All identity records, provenance logs, and operation records are permanently available to RT-04 without precondition per AIR-5. Any attempt by RT-01 to gate RT-04 access is itself an invariant breach triggering RT01-FAIL-06.

### RS-20.3 — A1 Constitutional Conditions Applicable to RT-01

The following conditions from A1 §8.1 (Validation Checkpoints CC-1 through CC-6) apply to RT-01:

**CC-1 — Identity Resolution Prerequisite**: Every Class A operation must begin with identity resolution. RT-01 enforces this as PRE-A-02.

**CC-2 — Gate 1 Authorization**: All Class A identity mutations require Gate 1 authorization from RT-03. RT-01 enforces this as PRE-A-01.

**CC-3 — Audit Availability**: All RT-01 operations must produce audit records accessible to RT-04. RT-01 satisfies this through POST-A-03 and RT01-OBL-09.

**CC-4 — Temporal Ordering**: All RT-01 state transitions must carry RT-07 temporal attestation. RT-01 requests attestation per A1 PAIR 09 and records it per RT01-INV-2.

**CC-5 — Constitutional Loop Integrity**: RT-01 must participate in all 10 constitutional loop phases as the Foundation Layer. RT-01 satisfies this as specified in RS-29.

**CC-6 — Projection Boundary**: RT-01's outputs must never cross the Projection Boundary as raw records. RT-01 satisfies this per D8-INV-6 and RS-32.4.

### RS-20.4 — RT-01-Specific Derived Invariants

The following invariants derive from RT-01's unique position as Tier 1 Constitutional Infrastructure:

**RT01-SPEC-INV-1 — Operational Self-Sufficiency**
RT-01 must be operable without invoking any other runtime for its primary identity functions. Its only conditional external dependency is RT-07 for temporal attestation, which is treated as best-available rather than blocking. If RT-07 is unreachable, RT-01 uses an internal timestamp with a notation of RT-07 unavailability.

**RT01-SPEC-INV-2 — Foundation Priority**
When RT-01 and any dependent runtime enter a conflict state regarding identity resolution, RT-01's constitutional determination takes precedence. No dependent runtime's operational state alters RT-01's constitutional obligations.

**RT01-SPEC-INV-3 — Query Consistency**
Any two Class B query operations targeting the same ActorProfile at the same logical time must return identical data. RT-01 may not serve different identity data to different requestors for the same record at the same moment.

**RT01-SPEC-INV-4 — Zero Silent Failures**
RT-01 may not silently swallow any failure condition. Every error condition must produce a classified failure mode (RT01-FAIL-01 through RT01-FAIL-09) and a corresponding audit event. Unclassified failures are treated as RT01-FAIL-09.

## RS-22 — Recovery

### RS-22.1 — Recovery Authority and Principles

Recovery authority for RT-01 operations derives from the following constitutional provisions:

**RC-1 — RT-03 as Sole Rollback Initiator**
Per D4 §2.1 (Kernel Mediation Principle) and A1 PAIR 02, RT-03 is the sole runtime authorized to initiate rollback of RT-01 state changes. RT-01 may suspend operations and preserve state pending recovery, but may not self-initiate rollback. RT-01's role in recovery is isolation and notification, not remediation.

**RC-2 — RT-04 Audit Continuity**
Per AIR-5, recovery procedures must not interrupt RT-04's audit access. All recovery states must remain fully auditable. Recovery events are recorded in the provenance log with a "recovery" classification.

**RC-3 — Provenance Preservation During Recovery**
Per RT01-INV-2 and D8-INV-2, recovery operations themselves must be recorded in the provenance log. A recovery event is a provenance entry, not a removal of prior entries. Recovery cannot delete or modify any prior provenance record.

**RC-4 — No Silent Recovery**
Per RT01-SPEC-INV-4, all recovery actions must produce audit events. Recovery events are classified in the audit record as recovery operations, distinct from normal operations.

### RS-22.2 — Recovery Procedures

**RT01-REC-01 — Identity Resolution Recovery**
Triggered by: RT01-FAIL-01
Procedure: No state rollback required. Return resolution failure classification to requesting runtime. Emit RT01-OUT-11 (error notification) with failure identifier and classification. Operation terminates cleanly. If resolution failure persists across multiple requests for the same identity, escalate to RT01-REC-09 level investigation.

**RT01-REC-02 — Provenance Chain Recovery**
Triggered by: RT01-FAIL-02
Procedure: Immediately place affected ActorProfile in a read-only suspended state. Emit critical audit event (RT01-OUT-10) with full chain integrity details. Notify RT-03 via established communication channel. Do not attempt to repair the provenance chain — chain integrity is constitutional and repair authority belongs to RT-03 with RT-16 amendment process if required. Await RT-03's mediation directive.

**RT01-REC-03 — Suspended Actor Recovery**
Triggered by: RT01-FAIL-03
Procedure: No state change required. Return suspension status projection (RT01-OUT-01 with suspension indicator). Emit audit event noting suspension enforcement. Recovery is complete upon rejection.

**RT01-REC-04 — State Transition Recovery**
Triggered by: RT01-FAIL-04
Procedure: No state change to the ActorProfile. Append a provenance entry noting the illegal transition attempt (recording the attempt, not executing the transition). Emit audit event with the requested transition and the legal matrix entry that was violated.

**RT01-REC-05 — Concurrent Modification Recovery**
Triggered by: RT01-FAIL-05
Procedure: If constitutional queueing is available, place the subsequent operation in the queue with a RT-07 timestamp attestation. If queueing is not available, return a retry indication with a constitutional basis. Release the modification lock when the primary operation completes (POST-A-05). Emit audit event noting the concurrent access attempt.

**RT01-REC-06 — Audit Access Recovery**
Triggered by: RT01-FAIL-06
Procedure: Prioritize RT-04 access over all other operations. Immediately yield all available identity records to RT-04 regardless of RT-01's internal state. If RT-01 is in a degraded state, emit a partial-state notification alongside available records. Request RT-03 intervention for full recovery. RT-01's audit response path must never be blocked by operation queues or error conditions.

**RT01-REC-07 — Gate Authority Recovery**
Triggered by: RT01-FAIL-07
Procedure: Reject operation immediately. Emit high-severity audit event with full Gate 1 token details (or their absence). Notify RT-03 of the unauthorized gate attempt. RT-01 does not attempt to issue or validate an alternative Gate 1 token — that is RT-03's exclusive constitutional function.

**RT01-REC-08 — Boundary Violation Recovery**
Triggered by: RT01-FAIL-08
Procedure: Reject operation. Emit audit event with boundary classification and the specific boundary rule violated per RS-32. Do not expose internal state details to the violating requester. All boundary violations are logged with the requester's identity for RT-04 review.

**RT01-REC-09 — Invariant Breach Recovery**
Triggered by: RT01-FAIL-09
Procedure: Immediately emit critical audit event. Suspend all Class A operations (Class B operations continue for audit purposes per RC-2). Preserve complete state snapshot in RT01-OWN-05 (operational event log). Notify RT-03 via emergency channel. Await RT-03's explicit clearance before resuming Class A operations. This is the most severe recovery state in RT-01's operational range.

### RS-22.3 — Recovery Completion Criteria

A recovery operation is complete when:
1. The initiating failure condition has been addressed or isolated to a non-propagating state
2. A recovery provenance entry has been appended to the affected record's log
3. An audit event has been emitted and is accessible to RT-04
4. RT-03 has been notified for all recovery levels requiring RT-03 mediation (RC-1)
5. The operational state (suspended or normal) has been definitively set and confirmed

## RS-23 — Audit Requirements

### RS-23.1 — Audit Authority Structure

Per AIR-5, RT-04 holds Audit Authority over RT-01. Per AIR-1, RT-01 holds Observation Authority in the identity domain only. These authorities are non-overlapping and non-competing: RT-01 observes identity events within its domain; RT-04 audits RT-01's exercise of its authority. RT-01 does not audit itself.

### RS-23.2 — Audit Record Generation Obligations

RT-01 must generate audit records for the following event categories:

**AUD-CAT-01 — Registration Events**: Every ActorProfile creation (RT01-PROC-01). Record must contain: actor identifier, initiating authority, Gate 1 reference, temporal attestation, initial state assigned.

**AUD-CAT-02 — State Transition Events**: Every ActorProfile state change (RT01-PROC-02 through RT01-PROC-05). Record must contain: actor identifier, prior state, new state, initiating authority, Gate 1 reference, temporal attestation, provenance log entry reference.

**AUD-CAT-03 — Identity Mutation Events**: Every semantic layer modification (RT01-PROC-06). Record must contain: actor identifier, fields modified (not field values — content privacy is preserved), modification authority, temporal attestation.

**AUD-CAT-04 — ExternalReference Events**: Every ExternalReference creation, modification, or expiry (RT01-PROC-07). Record must contain: reference identifier, external entity type, operation type, temporal attestation.

**AUD-CAT-05 — Failure Events**: Every triggered failure mode (RT01-FAIL-01 through RT01-FAIL-09). Record must contain: failure identifier, failure classification, triggering operation details, responding recovery procedure initiated.

## RS-29 — Constitutional Loop

### RS-29.1 — RT-01's Position in the Constitutional Loop

RT-01 is the Foundation Layer of the constitutional loop defined in A1 §12 and D4 §3. Every constitutional loop cycle begins with identity as the precondition for all other constitutional operations. RT-01's participation is not optional; it is constitutionally required at all 10 loop phases.

### RS-29.2 — Loop Phase Participation

**Phase 1 — Identity Resolution**
RT-01's role: Primary. RT-01 is the sole source of identity resolution for all actors participating in the loop cycle. Every other phase depends on Phase 1 having completed through RT-01.

**Phase 2 — Authorization Surface**
RT-01's role: Active. RT-01 provides the identity records against which RT-02 constructs the authorization surface. When Phase 1 produces a state change, RT-01 notifies RT-02 (A1 PAIR 01) to trigger authorization surface refresh.

**Phase 3 — Gate Authorization**
RT-01's role: Passive recipient. RT-03 provides Gate 1 authorization to RT-01 for Class A operations at this phase. RT-01's Phase 3 participation is enforcement of PRE-A-01.

**Phase 4 — Kernel Mediation**
RT-01's role: Passive. RT-03 mediates all Class A operations. RT-01 executes within RT-03's mediation framework but does not orchestrate it.

**Phase 5 — Observation Capture**
RT-01's role: Source. RT-01 emits identity events to RT-06. RT-01 does not initiate this interaction; it emits events in response to its own Class A operations, and RT-06 captures them per A1 PAIR 08.

**Phase 6 — Audit Recording**
RT-01's role: Subject. RT-04 audits RT-01's operations at this phase. RT-01's role is complete transparency per AIR-5 and RT01-INV-6.

**Phase 7 — Temporal Attestation**
RT-01's role: Recipient. RT-07 provides temporal attestation. RT-01 incorporates attestation into provenance entries per A1 PAIR 09.

**Phase 8 — Governance Alignment**
RT-01's role: Constitutional record source. RT-05 governance processes reference RT-01's actor records. RT-01 provides records upon request; governance decisions that alter actor status route through RT-03 back to RT-01 as Class A operations.

**Phase 9 — External Boundary**
RT-01's role: ExternalReference authority. RT-08 interactions that create or modify ExternalReference records require RT-01 to execute the modification. RT-01 processes RT-08 inputs as Class A operations per A1 PAIR 10.

**Phase 10 — Loop Closure and Consistency**
RT-01's role: Foundation verification. RT-03 may request a consistency snapshot from RT-01 at loop closure per VC-9. RT-01 provides the snapshot; RT-03 verifies cross-runtime consistency.

### RS-29.3 — Constitutional Loop Integrity Requirements (CLI-1 through CLI-4)

**CLI-1 — Foundation Availability**: RT-01 must be available at Phase 1 of every constitutional loop cycle. If RT-01 is unavailable, the constitutional loop cannot proceed with Class A operations.

**CLI-2 — Identity Consistency Across Phases**: The identity data RT-01 provides at Phase 1 must remain consistent through Phase 10 of the same loop cycle. RT-01 must not change the identity state of a record mid-cycle for actors whose identity was resolved at Phase 1 of that cycle without emitting a cycle-interruption event.

**CLI-3 — Audit Record Pre-Closure**: RT-01 must ensure all audit records for the current cycle are accessible to RT-04 before the cycle reaches Phase 10.

**CLI-4 — ExternalReference Expiry Management**: RT-01 must process ExternalReference expiry events before Phase 9 of each cycle in which affected references appear, ensuring RT-08 does not operate against stale external identity claims.

### RS-29.4 — Loop Position Relative to Other Runtimes

RT-01 is the only runtime that participates as a primary actor in Phase 1 (Identity Resolution). All other runtimes are Phase 1 recipients or consumers. This establishes RT-01's foundational position: the entire constitutional loop is predicated on RT-01's operational correctness.

### RS-29.5 — Loop Failure Consequences

If RT-01 emits an incorrect identity resolution at Phase 1, the error propagates through all subsequent phases within the same cycle. No other runtime has the authority to correct an RT-01 identity resolution error mid-cycle; the correction must route back through RT-03 → RT-01 as a Class A operation in the next cycle.

### RS-29.6 — Loop Metrics

RT01-MET-05 (Gate 1 Authorization Coverage) and RT01-MET-06 (Audit Record Coverage) serve as the primary constitutional loop compliance metrics for RT-01's participation.

## RS-34 — Implementation Constraints

No single actor or component may hold unilateral authority to register, modify, or terminate ActorProfiles without Gate 1 authorization from RT-03. Implementations must ensure Gate 1 mediation cannot be bypassed through any code path.

**D8 PROH-3 — No Immutable Layer Mutation**
The structural layer of an ActorProfile (D-2 §IX, immutable layer) must never be modified after registration. Implementations must ensure the structural layer is write-once at registration and read-only thereafter.

**D8 PROH-4 — No Identity Record Deletion**
ActorProfile records may never be deleted from the identity registry. Implementations must not provide any deletion capability, administrative override, or maintenance pathway that removes ActorProfile records. The terminal state is Terminated; the record persists.

**D8 PROH-5 — No Provenance Modification**
Identity provenance log entries may never be modified, deleted, backdated, or reordered. Implementations must use append-only storage structures for the provenance log.

**D8 PROH-6 — No External Identity Without ExternalReference**
External entities may not interact with APEX system actors through raw identity references. All external entity interactions must be represented through ExternalReference objects managed by RT-01.

**D8 PROH-7 — No Cross-Boundary Raw Record Exposure**
Raw ActorProfile records may not be exposed outside RT-01's interior boundary. All externally-facing data is a projection per D8-INV-6 and RS-32.2.

**D8 PROH-8 — No Self-Audit**
RT-01 may not audit its own operations. Audit is RT-04's exclusive constitutional function per AIR-5. Implementations must not create internal audit review functions that substitute for or pre-empt RT-04's authority.

**D8 PROH-9 — No Authority Accumulation**
RT-01 may not accumulate authorities beyond AIR-1 (Observation Authority in the identity domain). Implementations must not add authorization decision capabilities, event capture capabilities, temporal authority capabilities, or any other authority not specified in RS-04.
