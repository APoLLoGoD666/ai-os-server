## §12 Boundary Analysis

### §12.1 Explicit Prohibitions on RT-11

From A1 §14.3 (Forbidden Interactions involving RT-11):

**FR-3 (A1 §11.2):** "RT-11 deliberation may not reference a Decision it itself produced as evidence for the same deliberation cycle. Decision D cannot be used as evidence for the deliberation that produced D." (Self-referential prohibition)

**LCI-4 (A1 §15.3):** RT-11 may initiate loop termination via: "(c) civilizational decision to suspend (RT-11 → RT-03 → suspension declaration, which itself is a Constitutional Loop cycle)." Suspension is a constitutional act, not an ad hoc operational decision.

**A1 PAIR 41:** RT-13 → RT-11 interaction as initiation: FORBIDDEN.

**A1 PAIR 42:** RT-11 → RT-14: FORBIDDEN.

**CC-5 (A1 §2.3):** RT-11 may not produce epistemic products from operations that have not passed through RT-08 (inbound) or been generated by constitutionally authorized internal runtimes.

**RT11-INV-7 (A0 §3.12):** RT-11 governs DOM-000001 through deliberation, not fiat. Fiat governance of the root domain is constitutionally prohibited.

### §12.2 Ownership Limits

**RT-11 owns:** CUM (per A0 §3.12) — but the CUM is synthesized through a process that RT-10 initiates (per A1 §12.2 Step 8). RT-11's ownership is of the completed, synthesized object; RT-10's role is production of DUMs and initiation of the Constitutional Synthesis Process. The formal synthesis authority belongs to RT-11 (R10 v1.1 RS-12 disclosure).

**RT-11 does NOT own:**
- Domain Understanding Models (owned by RT-10 and RT-15)
- CivilizationalDecision objects (owned by RT-12)
- Action Projections (owned by RT-13)
- Compliance Verification Records (owned by RT-12)

### §12.3 RT-10 / RT-11 Boundary

The boundary between RT-10 and RT-11 is the most complex and hazard-prone boundary in the system.

**What RT-10 does (does not cross into RT-11 territory):**
- Produces twelve Domain Understanding Models (one per domain)
- Initiates the nine-step Constitutional Synthesis Process (per A1 §12.2 Step 8: "RT-10 initiates CUM synthesis")
- Submits updated DUM/CUM as Class A through RT-03 (per A1 §12.2 Step 9)

**What RT-11 does (does not cross into RT-10 territory):**
- Owns the completed CUM object (per A0 §3.12)
- Conducts deliberation (RT-10 has no AIR-3 Decision Authority)
- Produces CivilizationalDecisionProposals (RT-10 does not)
- Manages CUM Degradation Protocol (RT-10 flags degradation; RT-11 manages the protocol)

**Boundary hazard:** A1 §12.2 Steps 8-9 state RT-10 "initiates CUM synthesis" and submits "updated DUM/CUM" through RT-03. This creates ambiguity about whether RT-10 or RT-11 owns the synthesis step. R10 v1.1 resolves this as: "formal synthesis authority belongs to RT-11" (RS-12 disclosure). The specification author must address this boundary explicitly.

### §12.4 CUM Ownership

### §13.2 D-2 Section Citation Conflict

**Nature:** A0 §3.12 Constitutional Traceability cites "D-2 §XI (Philosophy of Intelligence)" — but D-2 v1.2 §XI is "Philosophy of Agency" and D-2 §VII is "Philosophy of Intelligence."

**Resolution authority required:** This is an A0 §3.12 authoring error. Correcting it would require an A0 amendment. The specification author must use D-2 §VII in the specification (the correct section) and disclose the A0 citation discrepancy in RS-12.

### §13.3 CUM Ownership Conflict

**Nature:** Multiple documents assign CUM creation/ownership differently.
- A0 §3.12 Owned Objects: lists CUM as owned by RT-11
- A1 §6.1 Object Flow Graph: lists "Civilization Understanding Model | Creating Runtime: RT-10"

- R10 v1.1 RS-12 disclosure: "CUM is owned by RT-11 per A0 §3.12; RT-10 produces and delivers CUM but does not own the completed object"

**Assessment:** A0 §3.12 is the governing authority. The A1 §6.1 table tracks production (creation) not ownership. PAIR 32's "provisionally owned by RT-10" is not elaborated in A1. R10 v1.1's resolution is constitutionally grounded in A0 §3.12 authority. However, A1 PAIR 32's text has not been corrected and remains a source of potential confusion.

**Resolution authority required:** An A1 amendment correcting PAIR 32 P4 language would fully resolve this. Pending such amendment, A0 §3.12 governs.

### §13.4 A1 §12.2 Step 8-9 RT-10/RT-11 Boundary Hazard

**Nature:** A1 §12.2 (Reasoning Execution Order) steps 8-9 state:

- Step 9: "RT-10 submits updated DUM/CUM as Class A through RT-03"

This attributes both CSP initiation and CUM submission to RT-10. Yet A0 §3.12 states RT-11 synthesizes the CUM and owns it.

**Assessment:** A1 §12.2 describes the operational sequence (RT-10 triggers the CSP as a precondition for RT-11's deliberation). A0 §3.12 describes constitutional ownership. These are complementary, not strictly contradictory — but the A1 language is ambiguous about which runtime executes the CSP vs. which runtime owns the result. R10 v1.1 resolves: "formal synthesis authority belongs to RT-11." The specification author must not treat A1 §12.2 Step 8 as conferring synthesis authority on RT-10.

### §13.5 PAIR 32 P4 "Provisionally Owned" Language

**Nature:** PAIR 32 P4 states CUM is "provisionally owned by RT-10." The word "provisionally" is not defined anywhere in A1. This is an undefined qualifier on an ownership claim, creating uncertainty.

**Assessment:** No authority resolves what "provisionally" means in this context. Per A0 §3.12 (higher authority), RT-11 owns the CUM. The "provisionally" qualifier in A1 may intend to capture that RT-10 creates and holds the CUM during synthesis before delivery to RT-11, but this interpretation is not stated.

**Resolution authority required:** A1 amendment to PAIR 32.

### §13.6 A1 §6.1 Object Flow Table Inconsistency

**Nature:** A1 §6.1 lists "Civilization Understanding Model | RT-10 | RT-11 | RT-05" meaning RT-10 creates it. This conflicts with A0 §3.12 listing CUM in RT-11's owned objects.

**Assessment:** Same root cause as §13.3. A0 §3.12 governs. A1 §6.1 describes production chain, not ownership.
