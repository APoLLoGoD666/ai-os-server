## RS-03 — Purpose

**Constitutional Purpose Statement (verbatim from A0 §3.13):**

> To form constitutionally valid CivilizationalDecision objects grounded in Deliberation Records, manage the Open Action Register, and call the Kernel for gate evaluation before authorizing any civilizational decision — serving as the constitutional nexus between deliberated understanding and authorized action.

**Uniqueness Argument:** RT-12 is the sole constitutional runtime that performs the specific transformation from CivilizationalDecisionProposal (produced by RT-11 following deliberation) into CivilizationalDecision (the formal constitutional decision object). No other runtime may perform this transformation:

- RT-11 (Civilization Intelligence Runtime) produces the CivilizationalDecisionProposal through deliberation but does not form the CivilizationalDecision. RT-11's product is a proposal; RT-12's product is the constitutional act of decision formation.
- RT-03 (Constitutional Enforcement Kernel) evaluates the formed CivilizationalDecision at six gates but does not form it. RT-03 admits or rejects; RT-12 constitutes.
- RT-13 (Action Projection Runtime) receives the authorized CivilizationalDecision for projection but does not form or authorize it.
- No other runtime in Tiers 1 through 6 owns the CivilizationalDecision object or the Open Action Register (A0 §3.13 Owned Objects).

RT-12 also owns the permanent decision archive — a constitutional record, not an operational log, covering all decisions in all states. This ownership cannot be delegated.

**Functional Character:** RT-12 performs three constitutionally grounded functions: (1) decision formation — receiving proposals from RT-11, validating authority with RT-02, and forming the formal CivilizationalDecision object satisfying DA-1 through DA-6 and ER-1 through ER-5; (2) constitutional gate submission — submitting every formed CivilizationalDecision to RT-03 for full six-gate Kernel processing; and (3) Open Action Register management — creating, maintaining, tracking, and archiving all decision records through their complete lifecycle.

## RS-04 — Scope

**Scope:** RT-12 operates within Tier 4 (Decision and Action) of the constitutional runtime hierarchy (A0 §3.1). Its scope is the formation of constitutionally valid CivilizationalDecision objects and the management of the decision record lifecycle — from proposal receipt through Open Action Register terminal state closure.

**Scope Boundaries:**

**Upstream boundary (RT-11 / RT-12 boundary):** RT-12's scope begins at receipt of a CivilizationalDecisionProposal from RT-11 accompanied by a DeliberationRecord. The deliberation process itself — including CUM synthesis, Deliberation Architecture assembly, and the production of the CivilizationalDecisionProposal — falls entirely within RT-11's scope (A0 §3.12). RT-12 receives the output of deliberation; RT-12 does not conduct deliberation.

**Kernel boundary (RT-12 / RT-03 boundary):** RT-12 forms the CivilizationalDecision and submits it to RT-03 for six-gate evaluation. The gate evaluation itself is RT-03's scope. RT-12 is the submitting party; RT-03 is the evaluating party. Following gate passage, RT-12 creates the Open Action Register entry (via RT-03 Class B KOM, A0 §3.13 R4). This is the only Class B Kernel Manifest output owned by RT-12.

**Downstream boundary (RT-12 / RT-13 boundary):** RT-12's scope ends at delivery of the authorized CivilizationalDecision to RT-13 and creation of the corresponding Open Action Register entry. The Action Projection Lifecycle — including all stages of external reality projection — falls within RT-13's scope (A0 §3.14). RT-12 authorizes; RT-13 projects.

**Open Action Register lifecycle:** RT-12 owns the Open Action Register from entry creation through terminal state closure. However, terminal state assignment is performed exclusively by RT-14 (RT12-INV-5). RT-12 receives RT-14's terminal status assignments and updates the register; RT-12 does not independently assess or assign terminal states.

**Decision archive:** RT-12 maintains the CivilizationalDecision archive as a permanent constitutional record covering all decisions in all states (A0 §3.13 R9). This archive is permanent; no entry may be deleted (D8 PROH-5).

**Excluded from RT-12 scope:**
- Deliberation and CUM synthesis (RT-11 scope, A0 §3.12)
- Constitutional gate evaluation (RT-03 scope, A0 §3.3)
- Action Projection and Projection Boundary crossing (RT-13 scope, A0 §3.14)
- Terminal status assessment and consequence observation (RT-14 scope, A0 §3.15)
- Constitutional amendment initiation (RT-16 scope, A0 §3.16)

## RS-06 — Authority

**Authority Derivation Chain:**

RT-12's authority is derived through the following constitutional chain, read in order from highest to lowest authority:

**Step 1 — D6 §4.2–4.6 (five constitutional authority type definitions):**

The five authority types established by D6 are:
- AIR-1 (Observation Authority, D6 §4.2): right to initiate Observation Projections within a domain
- AIR-2 (Interpretation Authority, D6 §4.3): right to apply registered interpretation protocols to Observation Records
- AIR-3 (Decision Authority, D6 §4.4): right to form CivilizationalDecisions affecting a domain's ExternalRealitySegments
- AIR-4 (Projection Authority, D6 §4.5): right to authorize and execute Action Projections
- AIR-5 (Audit Authority, D6 §4.6): right to assess constitutional compliance; must be independent

**CRITICAL NOTE on D6 §4.7:** D6 §4.7 defines Authority Integrity Rules also labeled AIR-1 through AIR-5. These are a completely different constitutional system from the authority types at D6 §4.2–4.6. D6 §4.7 AIR-1 (Authority Separation), AIR-2 (No Unauthorized Projection), AIR-3 (No Knowledge Monopolization), AIR-4 (No Hidden Decisions), and AIR-5 (Audit Independence) are integrity rules, not authority type designations. They share the same notation but represent different constitutional content. They are never conflated in this specification.

**Step 2 — A0 §4.3 (authority relationship graph — RT-12's position):**

A0 §4.3 states:

> "RT-02 grants and holds: Decision Authority (for each domain) → granted to actors; validated at RT-12"

A0 §4.3 positions RT-12 as the **validation point** for Decision Authority — not as a holder of Decision Authority. Decision Authority (AIR-3, D6 §4.4) originates in RT-02 (which grants it to actors per domain). RT-12 validates that the actors submitting a CivilizationalDecisionProposal hold the requisite Decision Authority. RT-12 does not hold Decision Authority. RT-12 does not exercise Decision Authority. RT-12 validates it.

A0 §4.3 does not assign any of the five named authority types to RT-12 in the authority relationship graph. RT-12 appears in A0 §4.3 solely as the validation runtime for Decision Authority.

**Step 3 — A1 §5.1 (authority type distribution):**

A1 §5.1 assigns the following authority types to RT-12:

| AIR-1 (Observe) | AIR-2 (Interpret) | AIR-3 (Decide) | AIR-4 (Project) | AIR-5 (Audit) |
|-----------------|-------------------|----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| — | Compliance | — | — | — |

RT-12 holds **AIR-2/Compliance** (Interpretation Authority in the Compliance domain). This assigns RT-12 the right to apply registered interpretation protocols — specifically, the registered compliance verification protocols embodied in the DA-1 through DA-6 and ER-1 through ER-5 criteria — to assess the constitutional validity of CivilizationalDecisionProposals.

**Authority Gap Note (C-6):** A0 §4.3 does not explicitly assign a named authority type to RT-12. A1 §5.1 assigns AIR-2/Compliance. This specification uses A1 §5.1 as the lower authority filling in the A0 gap, with the caveat that A0 §4.3 does not directly confirm this assignment. See RS-12, Conflict C-6.

**Complete Authority Holdings for RT-12:**

RT-12 holds:
- **AIR-2/Compliance** (Interpretation Authority in the Compliance domain): derived from D6 §4.3 (definition) → A0 §4.3 (validation function) → A1 §5.1 (explicit assignment). This is the authority to apply the DA-1 through DA-6 and ER-1 through ER-5 compliance criteria to CivilizationalDecisionProposals.

RT-12 does NOT hold:
- **AIR-1 (Observation Authority):** Not assigned in A0 §4.3 or A1 §5.1 for RT-12.
- **AIR-3 (Decision Authority):** RT-12 validates Decision Authority; it does not hold it. Decision Authority is held by actors (registered in RT-15 domain registries, granted by RT-02). RT-12 forming CivilizationalDecision objects is a compliance and constitution function — the formal act of constituting a decision from a validated proposal — not an exercise of Decision Authority. This distinction is constitutionally critical (see RS-12, risk analysis in Phase 0 baseline).
- **AIR-4 (Projection Authority):** Not assigned. RT-12 does not cross the Projection Boundary.
- **AIR-5 (Audit Authority):** Not assigned. Audit Authority is held by RT-04 independently (A0 §4.3, D6 §4.6).

**Authority Grounding per CERT-03:** Every authority claim in RS-06 traces through D6 → A0 §4.3 → A1 §5.1. RT-12 holds AIR-2/Compliance. RT-12 holds no other authority type. This characterization satisfies CERT-03 (Authority Audit, R0 Part 7.3).

**D-2 §VIII Compliance:** D-2 §VIII (Philosophy of Governance: explicit authority, proportional authorization) requires that authority be explicitly held, not inferred. RT-12's AIR-2/Compliance authority is explicitly assigned in A1 §5.1. RT-12's validation function at the Decision Authority gate is explicitly stated in A0 §4.3. No authority is inferred beyond these explicit assignments.

## RS-10 — Managed Objects

**Objects managed by RT-12 throughout their lifecycle:**

**1. CivilizationalDecision**

The primary output object of RT-12. Formed from a CivilizationalDecisionProposal after DA-1 through DA-6 and ER-1 through ER-5 verification and RT-02 authority validation. Must satisfy RT12-INV-1 (grounded in DeliberationRecord) and RT12-INV-2 (satisfies all DA and ER requirements).

CivilizationalDecision Lifecycle States:

| State | Description | Entry Condition |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| `FORMING` | RT-12 actively constituting the decision from a validated proposal | CivilizationalDecisionProposal received with valid DeliberationRecord and AuthorityResolutionResult |
| `SUBMITTED` | CivilizationalDecision submitted to RT-03 for six-gate evaluation | A0 §3.13 R3 satisfied |
| `GATE_PROCESSING` | RT-03 executing all six gates | RT-03 gate sequence in progress |
| `AUTHORIZED` | CivilizationalDecision has passed all six Kernel gates and been admitted to RT-05 | RT12-INV-3 satisfied |
| `REJECTED` | RT-03 rejected the CivilizationalDecision at one or more gates | Gate evaluation failure |
| `ARCHIVED` | Decision in permanent archive (all states are archived) | Always — every CivilizationalDecision receives a DecisionArchiveRecord |

**2. OpenActionRegisterEntry**

Created by RT-12 for every authorized CivilizationalDecision (RT12-INV-4). Closed exclusively by RT-14 terminal status assignment (RT12-INV-5). Must reach one of four canonical terminal states (RT12-INV-6, D5 PI-12).

| State | Description | Entry Condition |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|

| `PARTIAL` | Terminal state — some but not all intended effects achieved | RT-14 terminal status assignment |
| `FAILED` | Terminal state — action not achieved | RT-14 terminal status assignment |
| `LOST` | Terminal state — consequence observation not possible within Effect Observation Window | RT-14 terminal status assignment; D5 PI-12; escalation required |

RT-12 may not self-assign any terminal state. Terminal states are assigned exclusively by RT-14 (RT12-INV-5).

**3. DecisionArchiveRecord**

A permanent record of every CivilizationalDecision in every state. Created by RT-12 for every CivilizationalDecision regardless of whether it is authorized or rejected. The archive is a constitutional record, not an operational log; no entry may be deleted (D8 PROH-5, A0 §3.13 R9).

**4. CivilizationalDecisionChainRecord**

Maintained by RT-12 to enforce the Civilizational Decision Chain (D7 Part 5.5, A0 §3.13 R7). Decisions are constitutionally sequenced, not arbitrary. The chain record establishes the constitutional ordering of decisions and their dependencies.

## RS-12 — Internal Processes and Constitutional Conflict Register

### Internal Processes

**Process 1: Decision Formation Process**

RT-12's primary process. Triggered on receipt of a CivilizationalDecisionProposal and accompanying DeliberationRecord from RT-11 (PAIR 40).

Steps:
1. Receive CivilizationalDecisionProposal and DeliberationRecord from RT-11 (A0 §3.13 R1)
2. Validate that the DeliberationRecord is present and complete (RT12-INV-1; 13-element record per D7 Part 4.6)
3. Request and receive AuthorityResolutionResult from RT-02 (A0 §3.13 R6)
4. Verify DA-1 through DA-6 (authority elements, D7 Part 5) are satisfied
5. Verify ER-1 through ER-5 (epistemic elements, D7 Part 5) are satisfied; if any ER blocking condition holds, return ComplianceFailureReturn to RT-11 (PAIR 40, Loop-Restarting; A1 §12.3 Step 8)
6. Verify position in Civilizational Decision Chain (A0 §3.13 R7, D7 Part 5.5)
7. Form CivilizationalDecision object (RT12-INV-2 satisfied: DA-1 through DA-6 and ER-1 through ER-5)
8. Submit CivilizationalDecision to RT-03 for six-gate Kernel processing (A0 §3.13 R3, RT12-INV-3)

Note on step 5: A0 §4.4 Step 19 describes RT-12 as forming the CivilizationalDecision and submitting it. A1 §12.3 Steps 6–9 describe RT-12 verifying and issuing a Compliance Verification Record before RT-11 submits to RT-03. A0 governs; the process described above follows A0 §4.4. See Conflict C-3 and C-2 below.

**Process 2: Post-Authorization Register Process**

Triggered upon receipt of six-gate passage confirmation from RT-03.

Steps:
1. Receive gate processing results from RT-03 (all six gates passed; RT12-INV-3 satisfied)
2. Create OpenActionRegisterEntry via RT-03 Class B KOM (A0 §3.13 R4, D4 Class B KOM)
3. Confirm RT-05 fabric admission of OpenActionRegisterEntry
4. Update CivilizationalDecisionChainRecord (A0 §3.13 R7)
5. Create DecisionArchiveRecord (A0 §3.13 R9)
6. Deliver authorized CivilizationalDecision to RT-13 (A0 §3.13 Outputs)

**Process 3: Open Action Register Maintenance Process**

Ongoing process maintaining the Open Action Register state.

Steps:
1. Receive TerminalStatusRecord from RT-14 (A0 §3.13 R8, RT12-INV-5)
2. Update OpenActionRegisterEntry to the assigned terminal state (COMPLETE, PARTIAL, FAILED, or LOST)
3. Verify terminal state is one of the four canonical states (RT12-INV-6, D5 PI-12)
4. Persist closed OpenActionRegisterEntry to RT-07 (A0 §3.13 Outputs)
5. Update DecisionArchiveRecord to reflect terminal state

**Process 4: Governance Compliance Alert Process (A1 §12.7 — lower authority)**

A1 §12.7 describes RT-12 identifying compliance gaps and generating compliance alerts. This process is based on A1's compliance verification model of RT-12 rather than A0's decision formation model. It is included for completeness but is noted as a lower-authority description. Where A1 §12.7 is consistent with A0 §3.13 responsibilities (particularly R10 — ensuring terminal state completion), it is incorporated. Where it conflicts with A0's characterization of RT-12 as a decision formation runtime rather than a compliance monitoring runtime, A0 governs.

## RS-15 — State Machine

```

  → [RECEIVING]: on CivilizationalDecisionProposal receipt from RT-11 (PAIR 40)

[SUBMITTING]
  → [GATE_PROCESSING]: on CivilizationalDecision delivered to RT-03 (A0 §3.13 R3)

[GATE_PROCESSING]

  → [REGISTER_ACTIVE]: on OpenActionRegisterEntry created (RT12-INV-4); Decision delivered to RT-13

[REGISTER_ACTIVE]
  → [TERMINAL_RECEIVED]: on TerminalStatusRecord received from RT-14 (RT12-INV-5)
```

## RS-22 — Recovery Behaviour

**Constitutional recovery behaviour for each RT-12 failure mode:**

**Recovery from FM-1 through FM-6 (ComplianceFailureReturn path):**

RT-12 issues ComplianceFailureReturn to RT-11 via PAIR 40 (Loop-Restarting, A1 §14.4). RT-11 receives the return, sets `compliance_failure_pending = true` (R11-v1.3 RS-13 PAIR 40), and re-deliberates (A1 §12.3 Step 8). RT-11 produces a new CivilizationalDecisionProposal addressing the failure elements and resubmits to RT-12 via PAIR 40. The re-deliberation cycle may repeat within constitutional bounds.

**Recovery from FM-5 (Gate Rejection path):**

RT-03 gate rejection is Loop-Terminating for the specific operation. RT-12 creates a DecisionArchiveRecord for the rejected CivilizationalDecision. No OpenActionRegisterEntry is created. RT-04 produces a ConstitutionalAuditRecord. If the gate rejection produces a ConstitutionalViolationRecord, human governance actors are notified. Recovery requires a new deliberation cycle initiated by RT-11.

**Recovery from FM-7 (Open Action Register Entry Not Closed):**

If RT-14 fails to assign terminal status within the Effect Observation Window, RT-04 detects the violation at periodic audit (A0 §4.4 Step 33). RT-04 generates a ConstitutionalViolationRecord. Human governance actors are notified. The LOST terminal state is applicable where consequence observation is permanently unavailable. RT-14 must assign the LOST state; RT-12 may not self-assign it (RT12-INV-5). Escalation to human governance is mandatory when a LOST state is reached (D5 PI-12).

**Recovery from FM-8 (Permanent Archive Deletion):**

The operation is constitutionally prohibited (D8 PROH-5). No recovery required — the operation must be rejected before execution. If an attempt is detected, RT-04 generates a ConstitutionalAuditRecord. The archive entry is preserved.

**Rollback Ownership (A1 §10.1):**

RT-12 owns rollback for objects it has produced: CivilizationalDecision, OpenActionRegisterEntry, DecisionArchiveRecord, CivilizationalDecisionChainRecord. RT-03 owns rollback for gate evaluation results. RT-11 owns rollback for CivilizationalDecisionProposal and DeliberationRecord.

## RS-31 — Phase Ownership

**RT-12 loop phase ownership per A1 §15.2:**

RT-12 holds SUPPORTING participation in two phases:
- **Deliberation phase:** Supporting. RT-11 is Primary. RT-12 supports deliberation by providing compliance determination capability via PAIR 40. RT-12 is available to receive a CivilizationalDecisionProposal during this phase.
- **Decision phase:** Supporting. RT-11 is Primary (per A1 §15.2, which retains RT-11 as primary — consistent with A1's compliance verification model). RT-12 supports by forming the CivilizationalDecision, submitting to RT-03, and creating the OpenActionRegisterEntry. This is the phase of RT-12's most intensive constitutional activity.

**RT-12 owns NO PRIMARY phases.** This is derived directly from A1 §15.2. No phase lists RT-12 as Primary Runtime.

**Reconciliation Note on Decision Phase Primary:** A1 §15.2 lists RT-11 as Primary in the Decision phase. This is consistent with A1's compliance verification model of RT-12 (RT-12 as a supporting compliance function to RT-11). Under A0's model (RT-12 as the decision formation runtime), one might expect RT-12 to be Primary in the Decision phase. However, A1 §15.2 governs loop participation classification (it is the source document for this section per the mandate). The conflict between A0's decision-formation model and A1's compliance model (C-2) extends to phase classification. This specification follows A1 §15.2 for loop participation (as required by RS-29 and RS-31 mandate), while noting the C-2 conflict in RS-12.
