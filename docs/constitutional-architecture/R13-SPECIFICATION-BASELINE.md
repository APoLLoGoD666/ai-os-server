## SECTION 3 — AUTHORITY MODEL (Task C)

### 3.1 D6 §4.2–4.6 Authority Types (Five Types)

From D6-v1.0-canonical.md §4.2–4.6:

- **AIR-1 — Observation Authority (§4.2):** The constitutional right to initiate Observation Projections within a domain and register results as constitutionally valid Observation Records.
- **AIR-2 — Interpretation Authority (§4.3):** The constitutional right to apply registered interpretation protocols to Observation Records, transforming them into Evidence.
- **AIR-3 — Decision Authority (§4.4):** The constitutional right to form CivilizationalDecisions that will result in Action Projections. Decision Authority does not automatically include Projection Authority.
- **AIR-4 — Projection Authority (§4.5):** The constitutional right to authorize and execute Action Projections in a domain's name — to cross the Projection Boundary and produce effects in external reality.
- **AIR-5 — Audit Authority (§4.6):** The constitutional right to assess a domain's constitutional compliance.

### 3.2 D6 §4.7 Authority Integrity Rules (Completely Different System)

From D6-v1.0-canonical.md §4.7:

**CRITICAL DISTINCTION:** The Authority Integrity Rules (AIR-1 through AIR-5) defined in D6 §4.7 are **NOT** the five authority types defined in §4.2–4.6. The AIR-N notation appears in both systems but refers to different content. The §4.7 rules are meta-level integrity rules governing HOW authority is exercised:

- **AIR-1 — Authority Separation:** The five authority types must not be collapsed into a single actor within any domain.
- **AIR-2 — No Unauthorized Projection:** No actor may execute an Action Projection without constitutionally registered Projection Authority.
- **AIR-3 — No Knowledge Monopolization:** Observation and Interpretation Authority must be held by at least two registered actors.
- **AIR-4 — No Hidden Decisions:** Decision Authority must produce Decision Records registered in the Open Action Register.
- **AIR-5 — Audit Independence:** Audit Authority holders must be constitutionally independent of the authorities they audit.

The specification author must never conflate these two systems. This is Pattern 10 of the Historical Failure Pattern Audit.
