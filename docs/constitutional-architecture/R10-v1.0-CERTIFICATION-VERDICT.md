## Verdict Basis

### Basis 1 — No Class I Findings

The audit identified zero (0) Class I findings. No critical constitutional defects were found that would indicate RT-10's constitutional role, authority type, input/output boundaries, loop participation, phase ownership, execution position, prohibited responsibilities, or constitutional invariants are incorrectly specified relative to the authoritative source documents (A0-v1.1.1, A1-v1.2, D-2-v1.2, D4-v2.0, D6-v1.0, D7-v1.0, D8-v1.0, R0-v1.0).

RT-10's core constitutional identity — transforming Knowledge Records into Domain Understanding Models using registered inference protocols, and delivering input to the Civilization Understanding Model for RT-11's ownership and synthesis — is correctly and consistently specified throughout RS-01 through RS-36.

### Basis 2 — No Boundary Violations

Six special verification checks were performed against the most constitutionally sensitive claims in R10. All six checks passed:

1. RT-10 correctly stops at DUM production; CUM assembly is assigned to RT-11. No boundary violation.
2. All five PAIRs (31, 32, 34, 38, 52) are present, correctly characterized, and complete per A1 §14.1 and §14.3.
3. All A1 §15.2 loop phase assignments for RT-10 are correctly reproduced in RS-29 and RS-31.
4. RT-10's A1 §12.2 execution position (Step 15) is correctly stated in RS-30.2.
5. All A1 §14.3 forbidden interactions are covered in RS-35.
6. RT-10's authority is limited to AIR-2 (Interpretation Authority) and does not claim unauthorized projection into RT-03, RT-09, or RT-11 domains.

### Basis 3 — One Class II Finding

Finding FAA-10-001 establishes that seven of nine validation checkpoint names in RS-24 diverge from the canonical names in A1 §8.1. This is a material defect because:

- Validation checkpoint names are constitutional identifiers defined by A1 §8.1.
- RS-24 cannot establish or rename checkpoints — it can only declare RT-10's role within them.
- Seven divergent names constitute a systemic transcription problem, not an isolated error.

This finding does not invalidate RT-10's constitutional role or any of its boundary, authority, or loop specifications. It affects only the RS-24 section's internal naming accuracy. Consequently, the verdict is CONDITIONALLY CERTIFIED rather than REJECTED.

### Basis 4 — Minor and Editorial Findings Do Not Alter Verdict

Finding FAA-10-002 (Class III — RS-30.2 Step 10 misattributes audit recording to RT-11 instead of RT-04) is a transcription error in a summary table. It does not affect RT-10's own execution obligations and does not alter any constitutional boundary.

Findings FAA-10-003 and FAA-10-004 (both Class IV) are citation precision errors. FAA-10-003 (D-2 §XI should be D-2 §VII) originated in A0 §3.11 and was faithfully reproduced by R10. FAA-10-004 is an overstatement of what A0 §4.3 explicitly contains. Neither affects substantive constitutional content.
