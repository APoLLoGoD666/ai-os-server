## Basis for Verdict

### Basis for CONDITIONAL (not REJECTED or REQUIRES REMEDIATION):

The specification demonstrates genuine constitutional derivation work:
- Constitutional seat (A0 §3.12), tier (T3), and naming hierarchy resolution are correct
- Core authority type (Decision Authority at civilizational scope) is correctly derived through A1 §5.1, even though the D6 source section is mis-cited
- All 7 PAIRs involving RT-11 are present and substantially correctly characterized
- The constitutional conflict register (CON-01 through CON-04) identifies real conflicts, even though disclosure is incomplete in some cases
- Boundary prohibitions (RS-32, RS-35) correctly define RT-11's scope
- No Class I constitutional role violation, no unauthorized authority creation, no fatal conflict
- Constitutional loop participation at Deliberation (PRIMARY) and Decision (PRIMARY) is correct

### Basis for CONDITIONAL (not UNCONDITIONALLY CERTIFIED):

Five Class II Material Certification Defects were independently confirmed:

1. **FAA-11-002** (Class II): RS-05 responsibilities are not verbatim from A0 §3.12. Six A0 responsibilities are omitted; five non-A0 responsibilities are added; multiple responsibilities are paraphrased. The verbatim claim is false. CERT-03 FAIL.

2. **FAA-11-003** (Class II): RS-20 invariants are not verbatim from A0 §3.12. All seven invariants have been completely rewritten with different constitutional content substituted for each. The verbatim claim is false. CERT-04 FAIL.

3. **FAA-11-004** (Class II): RS-07 incorrectly claims RT-11 owns "CivilizationalDecision," which A0 §3.13 assigns to RT-12. RT-11 produces "CivilizationalDecisionProposal." Six objects from A0 §3.12's Owned list are absent from RS-07. CERT-02 FAIL.

4. **FAA-11-008** (Class II): RS-29 classifies RT-11 as SUPPORTING at Updated Understanding. A1 §15.2 places RT-11 in the Primary Runtime column for that phase ("RT-09 → RT-10 → RT-11"). CERT-07 FAIL.

5. **FAA-11-009** (Class II): RT-15 is absent from RS-26 (A0 §3.12 explicitly lists RT-15 as a dependency). RT-12 and RT-16 are misclassified as dependencies — they are RT-11's dependents per A0 §3.12. CERT-04 FAIL.

**Condition 2 — Remediate FAA-11-003 (Class II):**

Additional constitutional constraints currently in RS-20 may be retained as RS-20 Supplementary Constraints clearly distinguished from verbatim A0 invariants. (a) Remove "CivilizationalDecision" from RS-07 owned objects. Correct to "CivilizationalDecisionProposal" which RT-11 produces per A0 §3.12.

(c) For objects in RS-07 not appearing in A0's owned list, either remove or provide constitutional citation for ownership claim.

**Condition 5 — Remediate FAA-11-009 (Class II):**

(b) Remove RT-12 and RT-16 from RS-26 (they are RT-11's dependents, not dependencies, per A0 §3.12). RT-12 and RT-16 should appear in RS-27 (Dependents) only.
