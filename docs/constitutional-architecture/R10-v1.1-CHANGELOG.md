### Change 4 — RS-06 Citation Precision (FAA-10-004, Sub-finding A)

**Section:** RS-06 (Authority)  
**Finding:** FAA-10-004 (Class IV — Editorial / Traceability Defect)  
**Nature:** Citation precision improvement  
**Constitutional basis:** A0-v1.1.1 §3.11 (RT-10 role definition); A0-v1.1.1 §4.3 (authority relationship graph, supporting context)

**Before (R10-v1.0 RS-06 primary citation):**
- Opening statement: "RT-10 holds exactly one authority type, instantiated through A0 §4.3 and confirmed by A1 §5.1."
- Authority derivation chain: listed A0 §4.3 as the instantiation step above A1 §5.1
- AIR-2 bullet: "A0 instantiation: A0 §4.3 authority relationship graph — RT-10 holds authority type AIR-2 in the Domain Understanding domain, granted by RT-02"

**After (R10-v1.1 RS-06 corrected citations):**
- Opening statement: Identifies A0 §3.11 as primary source for AIR-2 designation; A0 §4.3 as supporting context; A1 §5.1 as confirmation
- Authority derivation chain: A0 §3.11 (primary) precedes A0 §4.3 (supporting context) in the chain
- AIR-2 bullet: Cites A0 §3.11 as primary source with A0 §4.3 as supporting context

**Rationale:** A0 §4.3 contains the authority relationship graph but does not contain an explicit named grant "RT-10: AIR-2." RT-10's AIR-2 authority is derivable from its constitutional role as defined in A0 §3.11. A0 §4.3 provides supporting context. A0 §3.11 is the more accurate primary citation.
