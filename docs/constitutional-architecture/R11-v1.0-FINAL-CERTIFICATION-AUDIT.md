### Audit Finding 3: Authority Model — AIR Type and D6 Section Citation

**Source derivation (independent):**
D6 §4.2–§4.6 defines five authority types: Observation (§4.2), Interpretation (§4.3), Decision (§4.4), Projection (§4.5), Audit (§4.6). D6 §4.7 defines five Authority Integrity Rules: AIR-1 through AIR-5.

The five authority types in D6 are NOT numbered AIR-1 through AIR-5. The AIR-N notation in D6 belongs exclusively to the Authority Integrity Rules in §4.7.

A1 §5.1 Authority Graph assigns RT-11: "AIR-3 (Decide) — Civilizational scope." A1 independently renumbers the five authority types using the AIR-1 through AIR-5 convention (AIR-1 = Observe, AIR-2 = Interpret, AIR-3 = Decide, AIR-4 = Project, AIR-5 = Audit). This A1 numbering conflicts with D6 §4.7's AIR-1 through AIR-5 Authority Integrity Rules, which address different content entirely.

A0 §4.3 Authority Relationship Graph uses the five authority types by name (not by AIR-N number).

**Specification claim (RS-06):**
"AIR-3 (Decision Authority)" is cited as deriving from "D-6 §4.3." The specification states: "D-6 §4.3 establishes the five authority types (AIR-1 through AIR-5) and their constitutional definitions."

**Independent finding:**
D6 §4.3 is "Interpretation Authority" — not "Decision Authority" and not the section that establishes all five types. Decision Authority appears at D6 §4.4. The statement "D-6 §4.3 establishes the five authority types" is incorrect — §4.1 establishes that five types exist; each type is defined in its own section (§4.2–§4.6). Furthermore, D6 §4.3 does NOT use AIR-N numbering at all; the AIR-1 through AIR-5 labels for authority types come from A1, not from D6.

The specification conflates D6 §4.3 (Interpretation Authority) with D6 §4.4 (Decision Authority), and characterizes D6 as using AIR-N numbering for authority types, which D6 does not do (D6 reserves AIR-N for Authority Integrity Rules in §4.7).

**Classification:** Class IV Editorial/Traceability Defect (FAA-11-001). The authority claimed (Decision Authority at civilizational scope, assigned by A1 §5.1) is correct. The D6 section citation is wrong (§4.3 should be §4.4). The AIR-N origin attribution to D6 is misleading.
