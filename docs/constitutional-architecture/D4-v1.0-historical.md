## PART 3 — SIX CONSTITUTIONAL GATES

Every constitutional operation passes through six sequential gates before admission to the fabric.

**Gate 1 — Identity Resolution:** Validates the initiating actor's ActorProfile exists and is Active.

**Gate 2 — Ownership Gate:** Validates target object existence and operable state.

**Gate 3 — Authority Gate:** Validates the actor's authority chain, traceable to FoundingRatification without cycle or revocation.

**Gate 4 — Epistemic Gate:** Validates epistemic stage, prior chain objects, PossibilitySet requirements.

**Gate 5 — Constitutive Coherence:** Pre-admission check for constitutively invalid states: SC mutation, cycles, deletion, second FoundingRatification, type incompatibility, isolated nodes.

**Gate 6 — Temporal Gate:** Validates causal ordering, backdating prevention, forbidden transitions, era consistency, ChangeRecord immutability.
