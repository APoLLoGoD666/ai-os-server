## Part 1 — Remediation Summary

R10-v1.0-canonical.md was the CONDITIONALLY CERTIFIED canonical specification for RT-10 (Intelligence Runtime), subject to one Class II finding (FAA-10-001) blocking unconditional certification, plus three lower-class findings (FAA-10-002 Class III, FAA-10-003 Class IV, FAA-10-004 Class IV) recommended for correction.

R10-v1.1-canonical.md incorporates surgical remediation of all four FAA-10 findings:

1. **FAA-10-001 (Class II):** RS-24 validation checkpoint names corrected — all nine now use A1 §8.1 canonical names verbatim; RT-10 role descriptions updated relative to A1 §8.1 ownership assignments.
2. **FAA-10-002 (Class III):** RS-30.2 Step 10 audit attribution corrected from a misdescription to the canonical A1 §12.2 Step 10 text ("RT-04 records audit of reasoning operations").
3. **FAA-10-003 (Class IV):** All 33 occurrences of "D-2 §XI" replaced with "D-2 §VII" throughout; correction documented in RS-02; upstream root error in A0 §3.11 flagged for A-series maintainer.
4. **FAA-10-004 (Class IV):** RS-06 citation precision improved — A0 §3.11 cited as primary authority source for RT-10's AIR-2 designation; A0 §4.3 retained as supporting context; RS-09 RT10-OUT-01 domain count cited to A1 §6.1.

No constitutional content outside the four findings was altered. Surgical scope was maintained throughout.

### FAA-10-003 (Class IV)

**Status: RESOLVED**

**Finding summary:** All occurrences of "D-2 §XI" in R10-v1.0 were incorrect. D-2 §XI is "Philosophy of Agency." The correct section for the four constitutional intelligence principles (instrumental, fallible, context-dependent, interpretable) is D-2 §VII (Philosophy of Intelligence). This error originated in A0 §3.11 and was faithfully propagated into R10-v1.0.

**Implementation:**

- All 33 occurrences of "D-2 §XI" replaced with "D-2 §VII" throughout R10-v1.1-canonical.md.
- A correction note added to RS-02 documenting: the nature of the error, the correct section, the origin of the error in A0 §3.11, and the flagging of A0 §3.11 for separate A-series remediation.
- The four intelligence principles themselves are substantively correct — only the section number citation was wrong.

**Upstream note:** A0-v1.1.1 §3.11 carries the root citation error ("D-2 §XI" where "D-2 §VII" is correct). This error is beyond the scope of R10 remediation. It is flagged here and in RS-02 for attention by the A-series constitutional maintainer. No modification was made to A0 or any other upstream document.

**Verification:** Zero occurrences of "D-2 §XI" remain in R10-v1.1-canonical.md. 33 occurrences of "D-2 §VII" confirmed throughout the document.

### FAA-10-004 (Class IV)

**Status: RESOLVED**

**Finding summary (two sub-findings):**

Sub-finding A: RS-06 cited A0 §4.3 as the primary/explicit source of RT-10's AIR-2 authority. A0 §4.3 is the authority relationship graph and does not contain an explicit named grant "RT-10: AIR-2." The authority is derivable from A0 §3.11 (RT-10 role definition).

Sub-finding B: RS-09 referred to Domain Understanding Models covering "all 12 registered domains" without a specific authoritative citation for the count of 12.

**Implementation:**

Sub-finding A: RS-06 revised in three places:
1. Opening statement now identifies A0 §3.11 as primary source; A0 §4.3 as supporting context; A1 §5.1 as confirmation.
2. Authority derivation chain reordered: A0 §3.11 (primary) precedes A0 §4.3 (supporting context).
3. AIR-2 bullet updated to cite A0 §3.11 as primary source with A0 §4.3 as supporting context.

Sub-finding B: RS-09 RT10-OUT-01 row updated to cite A1 §6.1 as the authoritative source for the 12-domain count.

Both sub-findings are citation precision corrections. RT-10's authority type AIR-2 is unchanged; the authority itself was always correct.
