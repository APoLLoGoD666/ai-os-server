## RS-03 — Purpose

RT-11 exists to operationalize civilizational intelligence at the highest epistemic and decision-making level of the APEX constitutional loop. Its constitutionally assigned purpose, as derived from A0-v1.1.1 §3.12 and D-7-v1.0 Part 3, is:

**Primary Purpose:** To synthesize the Civilization Understanding Model (CUM) from twelve Domain Understanding Models (DUMs) delivered by RT-10, and to exercise Decision Authority (AIR-3) at civilizational scope by producing CivilizationalDecisionProposals that are constitutionally valid, epistemically grounded, and structurally irreversible where classified as such. RT-12 receives these proposals and forms the CivilizationalDecision after compliance verification and gate evaluation.

**Epistemic Purpose:** RT-11 transforms distributed domain knowledge into a unified, coherent understanding of civilizational reality, resolving cross-domain tensions and producing the single epistemic artifact — the CUM — upon which all civilizational decisions must be grounded.

**Decision Purpose:** RT-11 exercises the sole constitutionally authorized Decision Authority (AIR-3) at civilizational scope, producing CivilizationalDecisionProposal objects that satisfy all six decision authority requirements (DA-1 through DA-6) and all five epistemic requirements (ER-1 through ER-5). RT-12 owns and forms the CivilizationalDecision from these proposals.

**Constitutional Loop Purpose:** RT-11 occupies the Deliberation and Decision phases of the 10-phase Constitutional Loop as PRIMARY, transforming the epistemic pipeline output (DUMs → CUM) into constitutional action (CivilizationalDecisionProposal → RT-12 for compliance operationalization and decision formation).

**Civilizational Scope:** RT-11 operates exclusively at civilizational scope, meaning its decisions and understanding model address civilizational-scale phenomena, not domain-level or system-level matters.

## RS-10 — Managed Objects

**Objects managed by RT-11 throughout their lifecycle:**

**1. Civilization Understanding Model (CUM)**

The CUM is the primary managed object of RT-11. It is produced by the 9-step Constitutional Synthesis Process (D-7 Part 3.3) and must satisfy CUM requirements CUM-1 through CUM-5.

## RS-12 — Internal Processes and Constitutional Conflict Register

### Internal Processes

**Process 1: Constitutional Synthesis Process (CSP)**

Source: D-7-v1.0 Part 3.3 (verbatim steps)

The CSP transforms 12 DUMs into the CUM through 9 constitutional steps:

1. **Receive and Validate DUMs** — All 12 DUMs received from RT-10. Each DUM validated for provenance (VC-8), currency (VC-6), and identity (VC-1). Receipt logged to `dum_manifest`.

2. **Assess Domain Coherence** — Integrate DomainCoherenceStatus signals from RT-06. Identify cross-domain tensions and coherence failures requiring resolution prior to synthesis.

3. **Execute Cross-Domain Tension Resolution** — Apply constitutional conflict resolution protocols to identified cross-domain tensions. All unresolved tensions are registered as Knowledge Gaps (ER-5 compliance).

4. **Construct Civilizational Understanding Structure** — Assemble the structural framework of the CUM from the 12 validated and tension-resolved DUMs. Apply the 6-dimension Civilization Coherence Model assessment.

5. **Integrate Historical State** — Query RT-07 (PAIR 39) for historical state relevant to civilizational understanding. Integrate HistoricalStateQueryResult into the CUM structure.

6. **Assess CUM Coherence** — Produce the CUM Coherence Assessment (CCA) across all six coherence dimensions. CCA must pass all applicable thresholds before proceeding.

7. **Finalize and Version CUM** — Assign constitutional version identifier to the synthesized CUM. Update `cum_version`, `cum_state`, and `cum_currency_timestamp`. Store complete version history.

8. **Register CUM Synthesis Event** — Log CUM synthesis completion to constitutional audit trail via RT-03 (KRNL permission, Class A operation).

9. **Declare CUM Current** — Transition `cum_state` to `CURRENT`. Issue CUM availability signal. RT-11 is now ready to initiate constitutional deliberation.

**Process 2: Deliberation Architecture**

Source: D-7-v1.0 Part 4

Constitutional deliberation is governed by the Deliberation Architecture. For every CivilizationalDecision, RT-11 must:

1. Verify CUM is in `CURRENT` state (DA-3, ER-1, ER-2 compliance)
2. Initiate Deliberation Record with Question element populated
3. Assemble all evidence inputs with provenance verification (ER-4 compliance)
4. Identify all alternatives to be considered
5. Register all constitutional conflicts identified
6. Register all knowledge gaps identified (ER-5 compliance)
7. Register all competing objectives
8. Evaluate all alternatives against CUM and constitutional requirements
9. Resolve conflicts with constitutional reasoning registered
10. Identify sacrificed objectives and justification
11. Produce CivilizationalDecisionProposal
12. Assign confidence level with basis
13. Assign review requirement
14. Complete all 13 Deliberation Record elements

Per Evidence Standards (D-7 Part 4.4):
- **ESt-1:** All evidence must have verified provenance chain
- **ESt-2:** All evidence must be within constitutional currency thresholds
- **ESt-3:** Evidence uncertainty must be quantified and registered
- **ESt-4:** Contradictory evidence must be registered and resolution reasoning provided
- **ESt-5:** Evidence gaps must be registered as Knowledge Gaps

**Process 3: Decision Authority Execution**

Source: Internally authored constitutional elaboration grounded in A0 §3.12, A1 §12.3, and D7 Part 5

RT-11 executes decision authority through the following steps:

1. Confirm CUM is in CURRENT state and passes all epistemic requirements (ER-1 through ER-5)
2. Confirm Deliberation Record is complete with all 13 elements populated
3. Execute VC-3 (Authority Validation) — confirm RT-11 holds AIR-3 for the decision scope
4. Execute VC-4 (Epistemic Validation) — confirm CUM currency and grounding
5. Execute VC-5 (Constitutive Coherence Validation) — confirm Deliberation Record constitutional completeness

## RS-22 — Recovery Behaviour

**Recovery procedures for each classified failure mode:**

**FM-01 (Incomplete DUM Set) Recovery:**
- Maintain `dum_manifest` with received DUMs
- Issue RT-10 availability query via RT-03
- Do not timeout or discard received DUMs
- Resume CSP immediately when all 12 DUMs are available
- Log waiting state to audit trail at constitutional intervals

**FM-02/FM-03 (DUM Validation Failure) Recovery:**
- Reject affected DUM(s) with specific validation failure code
- Remove from `dum_manifest`
- Decrement `dum_receipt_count`
- Request re-delivery of specific DUM(s) from RT-10
- Do not re-validate previously validated DUMs in the same cycle

**FM-04 (CSP Internal Failure) Recovery:**
- Preserve all state at point of failure
- Log complete failure context to audit trail
- Transition `cum_state` to `UNINITIATED`
- Reset `dum_manifest` and `dum_receipt_count`
- Await RT-03 directive before retry
- Do not automatically retry CSP without RT-03 clearance

**FM-05 (CUM Coherence Failure) Recovery:**
- Register failing coherence dimension(s) as Knowledge Gaps
- Attempt targeted re-synthesis addressing identified gaps
- If two consecutive CSP attempts fail coherence, declare CDP (FM-11 path)

**FM-06 (CUM Expiry During Deliberation) Recovery:**
- Preserve Deliberation Record in progress (partial state)
- Halt deliberation
- Issue stale CUM refresh query to RT-10 (PAIR 32, QURY)
- Resume deliberation with preserved record once new CUM is CURRENT
- Register interruption in Deliberation Record element 7 (Knowledge Gaps)

**FM-07 (Epistemic Requirement Failure) Recovery:**
- Register specific ER failure in Deliberation Record
- Determine whether ER failure is resolvable (e.g., missing evidence can be obtained) or non-resolvable (e.g., CUM expired)
- For resolvable: obtain missing element and continue deliberation
- For non-resolvable: halt decision production; escalate per FM classification
