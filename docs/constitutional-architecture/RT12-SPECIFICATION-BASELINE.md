**Source:** A0-v1.1.1-canonical.md §3.13, Responsibilities block

---

### 2.1 Functional Character of Responsibilities

The ten A0 responsibilities characterize RT-12 as performing three constitutional functions:

- **Decision Formation** (Responsibilities 1, 2, 6, 7): RT-12 receives proposals, validates authority, and forms the formal CivilizationalDecision object.
- **Constitutional Gate Passage** (Responsibility 3): RT-12 submits decisions to RT-03 for six-gate evaluation.
- **Open Action Register Management** (Responsibilities 4, 5, 8, 9, 10): RT-12 creates, maintains, and tracks the permanent decision archive.

**Note:** These responsibilities do not align with A1's characterization of RT-12 as a "compliance verification" runtime that functions at Gate 5. A0 describes decision formation as a primary responsibility. This is a substantive conflict (see Section 10).

## SECTION 3 — AUTHORITY MODEL WITH FULL DERIVATION CHAIN (Task C)

### 3.1 D6 Authority Type Definitions (§4.2–4.4 and §4.7)

The five D6 constitutional authority types (D6-v1.0-canonical.md §4.2–4.6):

| Authority Type | D6 Section | Definition Summary |
|----------------|------------|-------------------|
| AIR-1 (Observation Authority) | §4.2 | Right to initiate Observation Projections within a domain |
| AIR-2 (Interpretation Authority) | §4.3 | Right to apply registered interpretation protocols to Observation Records |
| AIR-3 (Decision Authority) | §4.4 | Right to form CivilizationalDecisions affecting a domain's ExternalRealitySegments |
| AIR-4 (Projection Authority) | §4.5 | Right to authorize and execute Action Projections crossing the Projection Boundary |
| AIR-5 (Audit Authority) | §4.6 | Right to assess constitutional compliance, must be independent |

**D6 §4.7 Authority Integrity Rules (CRITICAL DISTINCTION):**

The AIR-N notation in §4.7 is a completely different system from the §4.2–4.6 authority types. D6 §4.7 defines:

- AIR-1 (§4.7): Authority Separation — the five authority types must not be collapsed into a single actor
- AIR-2 (§4.7): No Unauthorized Projection
- AIR-3 (§4.7): No Knowledge Monopolization
- AIR-4 (§4.7): No Hidden Decisions — Decision Authority must produce Decision Records in the Open Action Register
- AIR-5 (§4.7): Audit Independence — Audit Authority holders must be constitutionally independent

**SPECIFICATION AUTHOR WARNING:** The AIR-N notation in D6 §4.2–4.6 (authority types) and D6 §4.7 (integrity rules) share the same notation but represent entirely different constitutional content. They must never be conflated.

**Conclusion on Authority Model:**

- RT-12 explicitly does NOT hold: AIR-1 (Observation), AIR-3 (Decision), AIR-4 (Projection), AIR-5 (Audit).

**CRITICAL NOTE FOR SPECIFICATION AUTHOR:** RT-12 forms CivilizationalDecision objects (A0 §3.13 Responsibility 2) but does NOT hold Decision Authority. The formation of the formal decision object is a compliance function (verifying that proposals satisfy DA-1 through DA-6 and ER-1 through ER-5) — not an exercise of Decision Authority. Decision Authority is held by actors (granted by RT-02, validated at RT-12). The RS-06 section must precisely articulate this distinction to avoid decision authority inflation.

**OQ-2 — RT-14 Permission Matrix Row**

**OQ-3 — Open Action Register Relationship to D4 Class B KOM**
