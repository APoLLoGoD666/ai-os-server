**### 3.11 RT-10 — Intelligence Runtime**

**Constitutional Purpose:** To produce Domain Understanding Models from Knowledge States through registered inference protocols, provide reasoning services to all twelve civilization domains, and supply Domain Understanding Models to the Civilization Intelligence Runtime for constitutional synthesis.

**Constitutional Authority:** D6 DOM-000002 (Intelligence as a full civilization domain; reasoning, deliberation, cognitive processing; substrate for all domains requiring reasoning); D6 §5.3 (domain intelligence in the domain architecture); D7 Part 3 (Domain Understanding Models as inputs to CUM synthesis); D-2 §XI (Philosophy of Intelligence: instrumental, fallible, context-dependent, interpretable); D8 Phase 4 (Civilizational Intelligence Runtime implementation phase).

**Responsibilities:**
1. Receive Knowledge States from RT-09 and apply registered inference protocols to produce Domain Understanding Models
2. Produce a Domain Understanding Model for each of the twelve civilization domains
3. Provide reasoning services to all twelve domain instances (RT-15)
4. Apply registered inference protocols — never unregistered or ad hoc inference (D-2 §XI: intelligence is instrumentalized and interpretable)
5. Maintain the interpretability of all inference: how each Domain Understanding Model was formed must be traceable (D-2 §XI)
6. Acknowledge and preserve uncertainty in Domain Understanding Models — uncertainty is not collapsed into false certainty (D-2 §XI: intelligence is fallible)
7. Retrieve historical Understanding Models from RT-07 as required for temporal continuity
8. Provide Domain Understanding Models to RT-11 (Civilization Intelligence Runtime) for CUM synthesis
9. Flag Understanding Model degradation when relevant Knowledge States are DKS-3 (Contested) or DKS-4 (Degraded)
10. Coordinate with RT-15 on domain-level understanding requirements

**Owned Constitutional Objects:** DomainUnderstandingModel (twelve instances — one per domain); InferenceProtocol (registered inference protocols); UnderstandingDegradationFlag.

**Consumed Constitutional Objects:** KnowledgeState (from RT-09 — DKS-1 through DKS-4 for all twelve domains); HistoricalStateQueryResult (from RT-07 — historical Understanding Models).

**Produced Constitutional Objects:** DomainUnderstandingModel (twelve instances); UnderstandingDegradationFlag.

**Runtime Inputs:** KnowledgeState updates from RT-09 (via RT-03 and RT-05 after fabric admission); historical Understanding Model queries to RT-07; Understanding requirements from RT-15 domain instances.

**Runtime Outputs:** DomainUnderstandingModel (to RT-11 for CUM synthesis); DomainUnderstandingModel (to RT-15 for domain-level understanding); UnderstandingDegradationFlag (to RT-06 for coherence evaluation, to RT-11 for CUM Degradation Protocol).

**Invariants:**
- RT10-INV-1: Every Domain Understanding Model has traceable provenance to the Knowledge States from which it was formed
- RT10-INV-2: Uncertainty is preserved in Domain Understanding Models — false certainty is not introduced (D-2 §XI)
- RT10-INV-3: Inference protocols are always registered before use — no unregistered inference (D-2 §XI)
- RT10-INV-4: Domain Understanding Model degradation is always flagged when source Knowledge States are DKS-3 or DKS-4

**Dependencies:** RT-09 (Knowledge States), RT-07 (historical Understanding Models).

**Dependents:** RT-11 (receives Domain Understanding Models for CUM synthesis), RT-15 (receives domain-level understanding).

**Constitutional Traceability:** D6 DOM-000002 → RT-10 is the operational realization of the Intelligence domain. D-2 §XI (Philosophy of Intelligence) → RT-10 enforces intelligence as instrumental, fallible, interpretable. D7 Part 3 → RT-10 produces Domain Understanding Models as inputs to CUM synthesis. D8 Phase 4 → RT-10 is part of the mandatory Civilizational Intelligence Runtime phase.

## PART 10 — OPEN QUESTIONS

### OQ-1: CUM Ownership — A1 vs. A0 Conflict (REQUIRES DISCLOSURE IN RS-07 and RS-10)

**Nature:** A1 §6.1 lists "Civilization Understanding Model" with creating runtime "RT-10." A1 §9.2 canonical provenance chain anchors CUM to "RT-10 synthesis operation ID." A1 PAIR 32 P4 characterizes CUM as "provisionally owned by RT-10." However, A0 §3.12 (RT-11) lists CUM as RT-11's owned object. A0 §3.11 (RT-10) does NOT list CUM among RT-10's owned objects.

**Analysis:** A0 is the governing architectural document. Per A0, CUM ownership is assigned to RT-11 (A0 §3.12 owned objects: "CivilizationUnderstandingModel"). A1 characterizes RT-10 as the "creating" runtime for CUM in §6.1 but this appears to reflect the process of synthesis (RT-10 performs the Constitutional Synthesis Process) rather than ownership (RT-11 owns the completed CUM).

**Constitutional Resolution Path:** The synthesis operation is RT-10's (A0 §3.11 R8 and A1 §12.2 Steps 8-9 both confirm RT-10 initiates CUM synthesis). The completed CUM object, once admitted to the Reality Fabric via RT-03, becomes RT-11's owned object per A0 §3.12. A1 §6.1 "creating runtime: RT-10" reflects that RT-10 produces the CUM object; A0 §3.12 reflects that RT-11 is the constitutional owner once formed. This is consistent with the A1 PAIR 32 P4 language "provisionally owned by RT-10" — provisional during synthesis, transferred to RT-11 on delivery.

**Safe handling:** RS-07 must note that RT-10 produces CUM during synthesis but CUM ownership is assigned to RT-11 per A0 §3.12. RS-10 must characterize CUM as a Produced (not Owned) object for RT-10. Disclosure of the A1 §6.1 characterization required.

**Risk level:** MEDIUM. Incorrect characterization could claim RT-10 owns CUM (contradicting A0 §3.12) or fail to document CUM production (making RS-09 incomplete).

### OQ-2: InferenceProtocol Ownership and Registration Authority

**Nature:** A0 §3.11 lists "InferenceProtocol (registered inference protocols)" as an owned constitutional object of RT-10. This is not one of the 17 D8 §4.1 canonical object types. The constitutional actor with authority to register new InferenceProtocols is not explicitly assigned in A0 §3.11 or A1.

**Analysis:** D-2 §XI requires that intelligence be "instrumentalized and interpretable" — implying protocols must be registered. The parallel with RT-09's EpistemicProtocol (identified as an open question in R9 pre-specification) applies here. If new protocol registration constitutes a change to RT-10's operational scope, RT-16 amendment process may apply.

**Safe handling:** RS-07 must characterize InferenceProtocol as an owned governance object with "registration requires constitutional authorization process" citing D-2 §XI and D6 §4.3. The registration authority gap is flagged without invention.

**Risk level:** LOW to MEDIUM. The owned object is constitutionally established; registration process details are implementation-facing.

### OQ-3: "Twelve Domain Instances" Responsibility Scope

**Nature:** A0 §3.11 R2 states RT-10 must "Produce a Domain Understanding Model for each of the twelve civilization domains." A0 §3.11 R3 states RT-10 "Provide[s] reasoning services to all twelve domain instances (RT-15)." However, A1 PAIR 52 shows both RT-15 → RT-10 and RT-10 → RT-15 directions — RT-15 produces its own DUM which RT-10 synthesizes into CUM. This creates ambiguity: does RT-10 produce all 12 DUMs, or does it synthesize 12 DUMs (some of which come from RT-15)?

**Analysis:** A0 §3.12 (RT-11) confirms "RT-11 synthesizes CUM from twelve Domain Understanding Models." A1 §6.1 lists DUM creating runtime as "RT-10, RT-15" — indicating both contribute. The most constitutionally coherent reading is that RT-10 produces DUMs from Knowledge States (A0 R1) AND receives domain-specific DUM contributions from RT-15 (PAIR 52), synthesizes all inputs, and delivers the synthesized set to RT-11.

**Safe handling:** RS-05 and RS-12 must characterize RT-10 as both a DUM producer (from Knowledge States) and a DUM synthesizer (incorporating RT-15 contributions), with the final set submitted to RT-11. This reading is consistent with A0, A1, and D7.

**Risk level:** LOW. The ambiguity is resolvable from the constitutional record.

### OQ-4: CUM Synthesis as RT-10 Responsibility vs. RT-11 Responsibility

**Nature:** A0 §3.11 R8 states RT-10 "Provide[s] Domain Understanding Models to RT-11 (Civilization Intelligence Runtime) for CUM synthesis." A0 §3.12 R1 states RT-11 "Synthesize[s] the Civilization Understanding Model (CUM) from twelve Domain Understanding Models via the nine-step Constitutional Synthesis Process (D7 Part 3)." However, A1 §12.2 Steps 8-9 states RT-10 "initiates CUM synthesis (9-step CSP from D-7)" and "submits updated DUM/CUM as Class A through RT-03."

**Analysis:** There is a potential conflict between A0 (which attributes CUM synthesis to RT-11 in §3.12 R1) and A1 §12.2 (which attributes CUM synthesis initiation to RT-10 in Steps 8-9). This is significant for R10 RS-05 (Responsibility) and RS-12 (Internal Processes).

**Constitutional resolution path:** A0 is authoritative. A0 §3.12 R1 assigns CUM synthesis to RT-11. A1 §12.2 may describe an intermediate step where RT-10 triggers the nine-step CSP which is executed under RT-11's constitutional authority. Alternatively, A1 §12.2 Steps 8-9 may reflect RT-10 performing preliminary CUM construction (assembling DUMs into CUM form) before formal RT-11 synthesis. This conflict must not be silently reconciled.

**Safe handling:** RS-05 must note that RT-10's responsibility is DUM production and delivery; CUM synthesis ownership is RT-11's per A0 §3.12. RS-12 must document the A1 §12.2 Steps 8-9 RT-10 role with the caveat that formal CUM synthesis is constitutionally attributed to RT-11. The discrepancy between A0 §3.12 and A1 §12.2 must be disclosed, not silently resolved.

**Risk level:** HIGH. This is the most significant open question. If RS-05 claims CUM synthesis as an RT-10 responsibility, it conflicts with A0 §3.12 and R11's specification. The conflict must be disclosed and analyzed in RS-12 with safe handling following the non-invention principle.
