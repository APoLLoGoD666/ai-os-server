## PART 3 — CORE SPECIFICATION

### RS-03 — Purpose

RT-07 (Memory Runtime) durably persists all constitutional objects and their complete provenance chains, maintaining append-only historical records accessible to all runtimes for historical state queries, ensuring the civilization's complete constitutional history is preserved without modification, deletion, or gap.

RT-07 is the sole runtime that provides durable persistence for all constitutional objects produced by all other runtimes. RT-07 does not produce epistemic content, does not evaluate coherence, does not admit constitutional objects, and does not enforce temporal ordering. RT-07's function is archival: it receives everything that passes through the constitutional pipeline and preserves it permanently in append-only form.

**Authority:** A0 v1.1 §3.8 (line 743); D-2 §XIII; D6 DOM-000004; D8 §5.7

### RS-05 — Responsibilities and Obligations

The twelve obligations of RT-07, derived in full from A0 v1.1 §3.8:

**O7-1 — Universal Persistence:** RT-07 shall durably persist all constitutional objects produced by all runtimes. Every constitutional object that passes through the RT-03 → RT-05 write pipeline is also written to RT-07's durable store. No constitutional object may be excluded from persistence. The persistence obligation applies without exception to objects from all 16 runtimes.

**O7-2 — Append-Only Provenance Chain Maintenance:** RT-07 shall maintain append-only provenance chains for all persisted objects (D8 INV-2). A provenance chain records the complete formation history of an object: origin, transformations, validations, and constitutional events. No entry in a provenance chain may be modified or removed after creation. New entries are appended only. Truncation of any provenance chain is a constitutional violation (RT07-INV-3; D8 INV-2).

**O7-3 — Historical State Access Provision:** RT-07 shall provide historical state access to RT-09 (historical Knowledge States), RT-10 (historical Understanding Models), RT-11 (historical CUMs and Deliberation Records), RT-04 (all historical records), and all other runtimes that require historical context. Historical state is provided as HistoricalStateQueryResult objects. RT-07 shall respond to all constitutionally authorized historical state queries.

**O7-4 — Historical Record Modification Prohibition Enforcement:** RT-07 shall enforce the prohibition on historical record modification (D3 RF-A8). RT-07 shall reject any operation that attempts to modify, overwrite, or update a previously persisted historical record. The prohibition applies to all historical records regardless of their age, content, or requesting runtime. Only RT-04 may access historical records, and only in read mode. No other runtime may modify RT-07-persisted records.

**O7-5 — Provenance Suppression and Accountability Record Deletion Prohibition Enforcement:** RT-07 shall enforce D8 PROH-4 (No provenance suppression) and D8 PROH-5 (No accountability record deletion). RT-07 shall not suppress, truncate, or omit any provenance record. RT-07 shall not delete any accountability record. These prohibitions are absolute and admit no exceptions.

**O7-6 — Constitutional Memory Lifecycle Management:** RT-07 shall manage the constitutional memory lifecycle: Active → Archived (never deleted). All persisted records begin in Active status. Records may transition to Archived status through deliberate closure (O7-12) or automatic archiving on supersession. The Archived state is terminal: no Archived record may be deleted. The lifecycle is forward-only (Active → Archived; no reversal). This lifecycle is governed by MemoryLifecycleRecords (RS-07).

**O7-7 — Temporal Validity Metadata Maintenance:** RT-07 shall maintain temporal validity metadata for all persisted epistemic objects. Temporal validity metadata records: the temporal position at which the object was formed, the temporal bounds within which the object's claims were considered valid, the observation timestamp, and the formation timestamp. This is archival bookkeeping — recording when objects were valid — not temporal ordering authority. Temporal ordering is enforced by RT-03 Gate 6 (D4 §4.6; A0 §3.4). RT-07's temporal validity metadata maintenance implements CLI-4 (Temporal Coherence) across the constitutional system (A1 line 1783; D8 CLI-4).

**O7-8 — Collective Memory Reconciliation Support:** RT-07 shall support collective memory reconciliation when domain memory records diverge (D-2 §XIII line 300). When domain knowledge representations held in RT-07's store are found to be inconsistent across agents or subsystems, RT-07 provides the authoritative historical record against which reconciliation is performed. Reconciliation outcomes are recorded as CollectiveMemoryReconciliationRecords (RS-07). RT-07 does not itself adjudicate reconciliation disputes — it provides the record and receives the reconciliation outcome for persistence.

**O7-9 — Memory Audit Access Provision to RT-04:** RT-07 shall provide memory audit access to RT-04 at all times. All historical records persisted by RT-07 shall be auditable by RT-04 through PAIR 21. RT-04's AIR-5 audit authority over RT-07 is non-delegable (R0 ADR-3). RT-07 may not restrict, filter, or delay RT-04's access to any historical record. Audit access is unrestricted and always-available.

**O7-10 — Highest Provenance Protection for RT-03 Class B Outputs:** RT-07 shall persist all RT-03 Class B outputs with the highest available provenance protection. Class B outputs (Kernel Manifest operations) carry constitutional significance. RT-07 shall maintain their provenance chains with highest integrity guarantees and flag them with highest protection classification in their HistoricalStateRecords. This obligation implements RT07-INV-4.

**O7-11 — RT-04 Audit Record Protection:** RT-07 shall persist all RT-04 audit records with protection from modification by any other runtime. Once RT-04 audit records are persisted by RT-07, no runtime other than RT-04 may access them in write mode. This protection is enforced by RT-07 and cannot be overridden by any runtime other than RT-04. This obligation implements the constitutional independence of RT-04's audit function (D6 §3.4; R0 ADR-3).

**O7-12 — Deliberate Memory Closure Support:** RT-07 shall support deliberate memory closure operations (D-2 §XIII line 298). Memory closure is a constitutional lifecycle event, not a deletion. When a memory closure operation is constitutionally authorized and received by RT-07, the affected record transitions from Active to Archived status, a MemoryLifecycleRecord is generated recording the closure event, and the record remains accessible to RT-04 (RT07-INV-5). Closure does not remove the record from RT-07's store. Closed records are Archived records — permanently readable, never deletable.

### RS-10 — Managed Constitutional Objects

#### HistoricalStateRecord

**Type:** Constitutional archive record  
**Owner:** RT-07  
**Created:** On receipt of any constitutional object via the write pipeline  
**Content:** Full content of the persisted constitutional object at time of receipt; object type; object identifier; creation timestamp; source runtime; RT-03 provenance record (Stage 9); temporal validity metadata (O7-7); protection classification (standard or highest — per O7-10 for Class B outputs); lifecycle status (Active or Archived)  
**Transitions:** Active → Archived on memory closure event (O7-12) or supersession; Archived is terminal  
**Constitutional basis:** A0 v1.1 §3.8; D8 §5.7; D3 RF-A8

#### ProvenanceChain

**Type:** Append-only provenance record  
**Owner:** RT-07  
**Created:** At the first write of any constitutional object  
**Content:** Ordered sequence of provenance entries, each containing: object identifier reference; timestamp; operation type (creation, update, supersession, closure); authorizing runtime; RT-03 Stage 9 provenance record reference; IsComplete flag (must be true for all complete chains per D3 GCR-3)  
**Append rule:** New entries appended only; no prior entry may be modified or removed (RT07-INV-3; D8 INV-2)  
**Constitutional basis:** A0 v1.1 §3.8; D8 INV-2; D3 GCR-3; D3 RF-A8

#### MemoryLifecycleRecord

**Type:** Constitutional lifecycle event record  
**Owner:** RT-07  
**Created:** On each lifecycle transition for any HistoricalStateRecord  
**Content:** Record identifier; HistoricalStateRecord reference; prior lifecycle state; new lifecycle state; timestamp; triggering event description; authorizing source (constitutional authority or founding protocol reference)  
**Note:** Closure is an event, not a deletion. The HistoricalStateRecord remains in RT-07's store after closure. The MemoryLifecycleRecord documents the transition. (RT07-INV-5; D-2 §XIII line 298)  
**Constitutional basis:** A0 v1.1 §3.8; D-2 §XIII

#### CollectiveMemoryReconciliationRecord

**Type:** Constitutional reconciliation record  
**Owner:** RT-07  
**Created:** On completion of a collective memory reconciliation event  
**Content:** Reconciliation identifier; participating domains or agents; inconsistency identified; resolution applied; authoritative record reference (the RT-07 record that was treated as ground truth); reconciliation timestamp; authorizing source  
**Constitutional basis:** A0 v1.1 §3.8; D-2 §XIII line 300 (Collective memory requires reconciliation)

### RS-12 — Internal Processes

#### 12.1 Write Processing

Initiated by: RT-05 atomic commit notification (every commit)  
Sequence:
1. Receive constitutional object from RT-05 pipeline (after RT-03 gate processing and RT-05 atomic commit)
2. Validate object integrity (object type, identifier, RT-03 Stage 9 provenance record present)
3. Create HistoricalStateRecord with all required content (RS-10)
4. Assign protection classification: standard (default) or highest (if RT-03 Class B output per O7-10; if RT-04 audit record per O7-11)
5. Append new entry to the object's ProvenanceChain (or create ProvenanceChain if first write)
6. Record temporal validity metadata per O7-7
7. Register object in Active record index
8. Issue write confirmation
9. If object is RT-04 audit record: register in RT-04 protected record registry (write-protect from all other runtimes)
10. If object is RT-03 Class B output: register in highest-protection record registry

#### 12.2 Historical State Query Service

Initiated by: HistoricalStateQueryResult request from RT-09, RT-10, RT-11, RT-04, RT-08, RT-14, or any authorized runtime  
Sequence:
1. Receive query specifying requested object type, identifier, temporal scope, and requesting runtime
2. Locate matching HistoricalStateRecords in durable store
3. Retrieve relevant ProvenanceChain segments
4. Assemble HistoricalStateQueryResult containing: matching historical records, provenance chain segments, temporal validity metadata for returned records, query completeness attestation
5. Deliver HistoricalStateQueryResult to requesting runtime
6. Record query event in audit log (accessible to RT-04)

#### 12.3 Memory Lifecycle Management

Initiated by: memory closure request (constitutionally authorized)  
Sequence:
1. Receive memory closure request with target record identifier and authorizing source
2. Validate constitutional authorization for closure
3. Transition target HistoricalStateRecord from Active to Archived in Active record index
4. Move record to Archived record index
5. Create MemoryLifecycleRecord recording the closure event
6. Verify record remains accessible to RT-04 (RT07-INV-5)
7. Issue closure confirmation

Closed/Archived records are NOT deleted. They remain in RT-07's durable store permanently and remain accessible to RT-04.

#### 12.4 Provenance Chain Maintenance

RT-07 maintains provenance chains continuously:
- Every new write appends a new entry to the relevant ProvenanceChain
- ProvenanceChain completeness (IsComplete flag per D3 GCR-3) is maintained by RT-07 for all active chains
- No modification or removal of existing provenance chain entries is permitted (RT07-INV-3; D8 INV-2)

### RS-14 — Lifecycle

4. Establish query service availability
5. Confirm RT-04 audit access channel is open
6. Transition to ACTIVE state

- **Write Processing Service** (RS-12 §12.1): Receives all objects from RT-05 pipeline and persists them; runs continuously
- **Historical Query Service** (RS-12 §12.2): Responds to HistoricalStateQueryResult requests on demand
- **Lifecycle Management Service** (RS-12 §12.3): Processes memory closure requests and lifecycle transitions

#### 14.3 Memory Closure Processing

When constitutionally authorized memory closure requests arrive, RT-07 processes them per RS-12 §12.3 without interrupting the Write Processing or Historical Query services.

#### 14.4 Recovery

On restart after failure:
1. Re-enter Initialization Phase
2. Rebuild all indexes from stable storage
3. Validate RT07-INV-1 through RT07-INV-5 for all persisted records
4. If any historical record has been modified (RT07-INV-1 violation detected): enter RT07-FAIL-03 recovery

#### 14.5 Termination

RT-07 does not self-terminate. Shutdown is constitutional only, initiated through the founding termination protocol. Prior to shutdown:
1. Complete all in-progress write operations or record exact write queue position for recovery
2. Confirm all provenance chains are complete (IsComplete = true)
3. Write all index state to stable storage
4. Confirm RT-04 audit access is preserved
5. Record shutdown event as a MemoryLifecycleRecord

### RS-20 — Invariants

The following invariants are derived from A0 v1.1 §3.8 (RT07-INV-1 through RT07-INV-5) and constitutional D-series sources:

**RT07-INV-1 — Append-Only (A0 v1.1 §3.8 line 772; D3 RF-A8)**  
No historical record persisted by RT-07 is ever modified. Every update to a constitutional object produces a new HistoricalStateRecord and a new ProvenanceChain entry — the prior record is unchanged. Modification of any persisted record is a constitutional violation (RT07-FAIL-03). This invariant cannot be waived.

**RT07-INV-2 — No Deletion (A0 v1.1 §3.8 line 773; D8 PROH-5)**  
No historical record persisted by RT-07 is ever deleted. The terminal lifecycle state for any HistoricalStateRecord is Archived. Deletion of any record (Active or Archived) is a constitutional violation (RT07-FAIL-05). The Archived record set only grows — it never shrinks.

**RT07-INV-3 — Provenance Chain Completeness (A0 v1.1 §3.8 line 774; D8 INV-2; D3 GCR-3)**  
Provenance chains maintained by RT-07 are always complete and unbroken. For every constitutional object persisted by RT-07, the ProvenanceChain IsComplete flag must be true. A provenance chain with any gap, truncation, or missing entry is a constitutional violation (RT07-FAIL-02). RT-07 shall detect and escalate provenance chain gaps immediately.

**RT07-INV-4 — Highest Protection for Class B Outputs and Audit Records (A0 v1.1 §3.8 line 775)**  
All RT-03 Class B outputs (Kernel Manifest operations) and all RT-04 audit records are stored by RT-07 with the highest available provenance protection. These records are registered in separate protected indexes. The protection classification cannot be modified or downgraded by any operation.

**RT07-INV-5 — Closure is Not Deletion (A0 v1.1 §3.8 line 776; D-2 §XIII line 298)**  
Memory closure is a constitutional lifecycle event, not a deletion. Closed (Archived) records remain in RT-07's durable store permanently. Archived records must remain accessible to RT-04 at all times. A closed record that is subsequently inaccessible to RT-04 is a constitutional violation (RT07-FAIL-06).

**D8-INV-2 — Provenance Preservation**  
Append-only provenance chains may not be truncated. RT-07 enforces D8 INV-2 through RT07-INV-3. Every provenance chain maintained by RT-07 is governed by this invariant.

**D3-RF-A8 — Historical Inalienability**  
ChangeRecords, HistoricalRecords, and ProvenanceChains may only be appended to — never modified, backdated, or deleted. RT-07 operationalizes RF-A8 through RT07-INV-1 and RT07-INV-2. RF-A8 is the D3 basis from which RT07-INV-1 and RT07-INV-2 are derived.

**D8-TI-5 — Temporal Invariance**  
Temporal attributes on constitutional objects (validity bounds, formation timestamps, observation timestamps) must be preserved through all specification layers. RT-07 implements D8 TI-5 through O7-7 (temporal validity metadata maintenance). Temporal attributes on any persisted object must not be lost, modified, or removed.

**D8-TI-3 and TI-4 — Relationship and Constraint Invariance**  
Constitutional object relationships and constraints must be preserved through all layers. RT-07 enforces these invariants per A0 §7.x by ensuring that all relationship and constraint metadata on persisted objects is preserved unchanged.

**D8-CLI-4 — Temporal Coherence**  
Stages must execute in constitutional time order; timestamps are constitutional records. RT-07 implements CLI-4 through: (a) O7-7 temporal validity metadata maintenance on all persisted epistemic objects; (b) Foundation Layer participation at every constitutional loop phase; (c) temporal attributes on all HistoricalStateRecords (RT-07 ensures timestamps are preserved as constitutional records). ## PART 7 — COMPLIANCE AND AUDIT

### RS-23 — Audit Requirements

**RT-04 access:** RT-04 holds unrestricted read access to all RT-07 owned objects at all times (PAIR 21, A1 v1.0; R0 ADR-3; A0 v1.1 §3.8 Responsibility 9). This access is non-delegable and may not be restricted by RT-07 under any circumstances.

**Audit-addressable objects:** Every HistoricalStateRecord, ProvenanceChain, MemoryLifecycleRecord, and CollectiveMemoryReconciliationRecord created by RT-07 must be audit-addressable by RT-04. "Audit-addressable" means: identifiable by stable reference; readable in full; not modifiable after creation.

**Write operation records:** Every write operation must produce an audit-addressable record: the object received, the timestamp, the source runtime, the write confirmation issued. RT-04 must be able to reconstruct the complete write history of RT-07 from these records.

**Query service records:** Every HistoricalStateQueryResult delivered must be recorded: requesting runtime, query parameters, records returned, delivery timestamp. Accessible to RT-04.

**Failure and recovery records:** All RT07-FAIL-NN events and their recovery actions are permanently accessible to RT-04.

**RT-04 record protection:** All RT-04 audit records persisted by RT-07 are in a write-protected registry accessible only to RT-04 in write mode. RT-07 enforces this protection (O7-11). The protection cannot be overridden by any runtime other than RT-04.

**Retention:** RT-07 does not define retention periods. Retention is a constitutional founding matter. RT-07 obligations: (a) never modify historical records; (b) maintain audit access for all records in the retention window established at founding.

### RS-24 — Validation Criteria

**HistoricalStateRecord Validity:**
- Contains complete content of the persisted constitutional object at time of receipt
- Contains RT-03 Stage 9 provenance record reference
- Contains temporal validity metadata for epistemic objects
- Contains correct protection classification (standard or highest)
- Contains correct lifecycle status (Active)
- Is not modifiable after creation

**ProvenanceChain Validity:**
- IsComplete = true for all active chains
- Each entry contains: timestamp, operation type, authorizing runtime, RT-03 Stage 9 provenance reference
- Entry sequence is append-only; no gaps; no retroactive insertions
- Chain is complete from first write to current state

**MemoryLifecycleRecord Validity:**
- References a valid HistoricalStateRecord
- States prior and new lifecycle states
- Records triggering event and authorizing source

**Write Confirmation Validity:**
- Issued only after successful durable persistence (not optimistically pre-issued)

## PART 11 — IMPLEMENTATION CONSTRAINTS

### RS-33 — Translation Requirements

RT-07's durable persistence operates on the constitutional layer (D-2 §XIII, D3 RF-A8, D6 DOM-000004, D8 §5.7). When constitutional objects are translated to or from implementation representations per D8, the following translation requirements apply per D8 TI-1 through TI-5:

**D8 TI-1 (Translation Completeness):** Every constitutional property defined in D-series for persisted objects must appear in the implementation representation with no loss of constitutional meaning. No HistoricalStateRecord property (object content, RT-03 Stage 9 provenance reference, temporal validity metadata, protection classification, lifecycle status) may be lost in translation to an implementation representation.

**D8 TI-2 (Authority Preservation):** RT-07 holds no AIR-N authority; however, RT-03's gate authority over all objects RT-07 receives must be preserved through translation. Every implementation representation of a persisted object must reference the RT-03 Stage 9 provenance record, ensuring the constitutional authority chain (RT-03 processing) is preserved.

**D8 TI-3 (Relationship Invariance):** Object relationships recorded in persisted ProvenanceChains must be preserved through all translation operations. No translation may modify, reorder, or remove provenance chain entries. Relationship metadata on persisted objects must be preserved unchanged.

**D8 TI-4 (Constraint Invariance):** Constitutional constraints on persisted objects (protection classifications, lifecycle states, IsComplete flags on ProvenanceChains) must be preserved through translation. An implementation representation that loses a constraint (e.g., omits the highest-protection classification for a Class B output) is constitutionally invalid.

**D8 TI-5 (Temporal Invariance — primary for RT-07):** Temporal attributes on persisted epistemic objects (validity bounds, formation timestamps, observation timestamps) must be preserved with full precision through all translation operations. No translation may reduce temporal precision or omit temporal attributes. This is the primary TI requirement for RT-07, implemented through O7-7.

**Prohibited translation operations:** No translation operation may: lose a HistoricalStateRecord's content; lose a ProvenanceChain entry; substitute a synthetic provenance record for an actual RT-03 Stage 9 provenance record; reduce temporal attribute precision; merge distinct HistoricalStateRecords into a single implementation object.

**Constitutional basis:** D8 §1.1; D8 TI-1–5 (authoritative as stated in D8 canonical)
