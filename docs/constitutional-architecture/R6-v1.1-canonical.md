## PART 3 — CORE SPECIFICATION

### RS-03 — Purpose

RT-06 (Coherence Runtime) continuously evaluates all active URO objects and all relationships in the Reality Fabric against the seven Global Coherence Rules (GCR-1 through GCR-7) defined in D3 §4. RT-06 detects and records all coherence violations, generates CoherenceResolutionEvents (CREs) and CoherenceConflictRecords (CCRs), maintains seven CoherenceRegisters that reflect current constitutional truth, tracks the Mandatory Propagation Window (MPW) for every active CRE, evaluates domain and civilization coherence status, and escalates CUM Critical State to RT-11 when more than four domains are degraded.

RT-06 is the sole runtime authorized to detect and record coherence violations. RT-06 does not admit constitutional objects (RT-03), does not maintain the Reality Fabric (RT-05), and does not perform audit (RT-04). RT-06's function is evaluative — it reads the constitutional state established by other runtimes and renders coherence judgments against the GCR rules.

**Authority:** A0 v1.1 §3.7; D3 §4; D3 RF-A5

### RS-05 — Responsibilities and Obligations

The thirteen obligations of RT-06, derived in full from A0 v1.1 §3.7:

**O6-1 — GCR Object Evaluation:** RT-06 shall continuously evaluate all active URO objects against GCR-1 through GCR-7 (D3 §4). Evaluation is triggered both by the continuous scan cycle and by Stage 10 initiation upon atomic commit.

**O6-2 — GCR Relationship Evaluation:** RT-06 shall continuously evaluate all active relationships in the Reality Fabric against GCR-1 through GCR-7 (D3 §4). A relationship evaluation failure is a coherence violation equivalent in standing to an object evaluation failure.

**O6-3 — Violation Detection and Classification:** RT-06 shall detect and classify all coherence violations. Every classification shall identify the specific GCR violated (not a generic "coherence violation" — see RT06-INV-2 and D3 RF-A9), the specific object(s) in violation, and the violation type per D3 Part 9 classification.

**O6-4 — CRE Generation:** RT-06 shall generate a CoherenceResolutionEvent (CRE) for every detected coherence violation. A CRE shall specify: the admitted operation that triggered evaluation (or the scan cycle if not commit-triggered), the specific GCR or fabric invariant implicated, all constitutional objects involved, whether the violation is resolvable, and the CoherenceStatus flag to be set in D2 Layer 5 for the affected object(s). A CRE does NOT modify the admitted objects — it records the evaluation judgment (D4 Part 9).

**O6-5 — Open CoherenceViolations Register:** RT-06 shall maintain an Open CoherenceViolations register containing all CREs for violations that have not yet been resolved or converted to CCRs. An entry is removed from the Open register when: (a) the violation is resolved and a resolution record is appended to the CRE; or (b) a CCR is generated and the CRE is closed.

**O6-6 — Stage 10 Execution:** RT-06 shall execute Post-Commit Coherence Evaluation (Stage 10) for every atomic commit processed by RT-03. Stage 10 begins at RT-03 commit notification. Stage 10 is not part of the atomic commit (Stages 8–9); a kernel failure during Stage 10 does not un-commit the admitted object. On recovery, Stage 10 shall be re-executed from the last confirmed CRE stream position (idempotent per D4 §3 Stage 10). During Stage 10, RT-06 evaluates: the admitted object directly; all objects in the propagation frontier (objects whose CoherenceObligations depend on the admitted object, traversed via CoherenceObligations reverse chains, per D4 §9.5).

**O6-7 — MPW Tracking and Escalation:** RT-06 shall begin tracking the Mandatory Propagation Window (MPW) at Stage 10 initiation (D4 §9.4). RT-06 shall maintain the MPW timer for every active CRE. When the MPW is exceeded for any CRE: (a) register a constitutional violation record; (b) flag affected objects as Unresolved Propagation; (c) generate a CCR; (d) suspend the affected dependency stream (Type C suspension per D4 §9.4). MPW duration is established at founding (D4 §9.4) and is not specified in this document.

**O6-8 — CCR Generation:** RT-06 shall generate a CoherenceConflictRecord (CCR) for every coherence violation that is either (a) determined to be unresolvable, or (b) has exceeded the MPW without resolution. A CCR shall contain: a reference to the generating CRE, the basis for CCR generation (unresolvable or MPW breach), all affected objects, and escalation status. CCRs are transmitted to RT-11 for CUM coherence status tracking.

**O6-9 — Seven CoherenceRegister Maintenance:** RT-06 shall maintain the seven CoherenceRegisters (D3 Part 9 §9.2) in continuous alignment with the current Reality Fabric state. Each register corresponds to one GCR domain. The registers shall reflect current constitutional truth at all times (RT06-INV-4). On every evaluation cycle, the affected register(s) shall be updated to reflect the evaluation result.

**O6-10 — DomainCoherenceStatus Evaluation:** RT-06 shall evaluate and maintain DomainCoherenceStatus for each active domain against the six Domain Coherence Dimensions (D6 Part 9): Domain Identity Coherence (§9.2), Domain Knowledge Coherence (§9.3), Domain Actor Coherence (§9.4), Domain Authority Coherence (§9.5), Domain Projection Coherence (§9.6), and Domain Temporal Coherence (§9.7). DomainCoherenceStatus is provided to RT-15 for domain coherence management.

**O6-11 — Civilization-Level Coherence Evaluation:** RT-06 shall evaluate civilization-level coherence against the six Civilization Coherence Dimensions (D7 Part 9): Understanding Coherence (§9.2), Strategic Coherence (§9.3), Decision Coherence (§9.4), Domain Relationship Coherence (§9.5), Temporal Coherence (§9.6), and Constitutional Coherence (§9.7). Results are recorded in the relevant CoherenceRegisters and in CUMDegradationRecord when degradation is detected.

**O6-12 — CUM Critical State Escalation:** RT-06 shall escalate CUM Critical State to RT-11 when more than four active domains are degraded (RT06-INV-3). Escalation is expressed as DOM-000001. Escalation is triggered on every DomainCoherenceStatus update that brings the degraded-domain count above four. RT-06 shall not suppress or delay this escalation.

> **DEF-006 Disclosure:** A0 §3.7 Runtime Outputs specify "CUM Critical State escalation (to RT-15 DOM-000001 instance)" as a direct RT-06 → RT-15 pathway. A0 §4.2 flow graph specifies RT-06 → RT-11 → RT-15 (CUMDegradationEscalation). These two A0 sections are internally inconsistent. R6 v1.1 implements the A0 §4.2 flow graph (RT-06 transmits to RT-11; RT-11 escalates to RT-15 DOM-000001). A0 §3.15 (RT-15 description) notes RT-15 "responds to CUM Critical State escalations from RT-06 and RT-11" — suggesting both direct and mediated paths exist. The RT-11-mediated path is implemented here.

**O6-13 — On-Demand Event History:** RT-06 shall provide event history to RT-03 (for Gate 6 BLOCK evaluation, A1 PAIR 16) and to RT-05 (for LOST operation recovery, A1 PAIR 23) on demand. Event history shall be accurate and complete as of the request time.

### RS-06 — Authority

#### 6.1 Authority Class

RT-06 holds no D6 domain-actor authority type (AIR-1 through AIR-5). RT-06's constitutional authority is a direct mandate from D-series constitutional specifications, architecturally realized in A0 v1.1 §3.7:

**Coherence Evaluation Mandate (D3 §4):** D3 §4 establishes the seven Global Coherence Rules (GCR-1 through GCR-7) and mandates continuous evaluation of all active URO objects and relationships against these rules. RT-06 is constitutionally appointed as the runtime that executes this evaluation mandate. The appointment is architecturally specified in A0 v1.1 §3.7, which derives RT-06's thirteen responsibilities from D3 §4.

**Stage 10 Evaluation Mandate (D4 Stage 10):** D4 §3 Stage 10 (Post-Commit Coherence Evaluation) mandates that coherence evaluation occur for every atomic commit. RT-03 initiates Stage 10; RT-06 executes the Stage 10 evaluation within the Mandatory Propagation Window (D4 §9.4). RT-06's Stage 10 execution authority derives from this D4 mandate, as architecturally delegated in A0 §3.3 and §3.7.

The D6 authority type framework (AIR-1 through AIR-5) applies to domain actors in the epistemic chain. Infrastructure runtimes derive their authority from D-series constitutional mandates directly. The sole D6 authority type held by an infrastructure runtime is RT-04's AIR-5 (Audit Authority) — which D6 explicitly establishes as an infrastructure-level authority. RT-06 does not hold AIR-5 or any other D6 authority type.

> **Note on A1 §5.1:** A1 v1.0 §5.1 assigns RT-06 "AIR-1 (Observation)" in "Event domain" under the "Event Stream Runtime" conception. ### RS-12 — Internal Processes

RT-06 operates two concurrent evaluation modes and several supporting processes:

#### 12.1 Triggered Evaluation (Stage 10)

Initiated by: atomic commit notification from RT-03  
Sequence:
1. Receive Stage 10 trigger from RT-03
2. Begin MPW timer (D4 §9.4)
3. Read committed object from RT-05
4. Traverse propagation frontier: all objects whose CoherenceObligations depend on the admitted object, traversed via CoherenceObligations reverse chains (D4 §9.5)
5. Evaluate admitted object and all frontier objects against GCR-1 through GCR-7
6. For each violation detected: generate CoherenceViolationRecord; generate CRE; update relevant CoherenceRegister(s); set CoherenceStatus flag in D2 Layer 5
6a. Route generated CREs to RT-03 for Class B processing (per A0 §4.2; D4 Class B KOM); RT-03 admits CREs to Reality Fabric stream (RT-05); persistence to RT-07 follows via RT-05 pipeline
7. Update CRE stream position
8. Evaluate DomainCoherenceStatus for affected domain(s)
9. Check CUM Critical State (O6-12)
10. On MPW timer expiry for any open CRE: generate CCR; flag Unresolved Propagation; apply Type C suspension (O6-7); route CCR to RT-03 (Class B processing per A0 §4.2) and transmit to RT-11 (O6-8)

Idempotency guarantee: Stage 10 may be re-executed from last CRE stream position on recovery without producing duplicate CREs or register corruption (D4 §3 Stage 10).

#### 12.2 Continuous Scan

Initiated by: periodic cycle (cycle frequency established at founding; not specified in this document)  
Scope: all active URO objects and all active Reality Fabric relationships  
Sequence:
1. Select next evaluation subject from active object set
2. Evaluate against all seven GCR rules
3. Compare result against current CoherenceRegister state
4. If new violation detected: generate CoherenceViolationRecord; generate CRE; update register; set CoherenceStatus
5. If previously registered violation is now resolved: close CRE; update register; clear CoherenceStatus
6. Advance to next subject

#### 12.3 Register Maintenance

Registers are updated atomically with their triggering evaluation. No register update may be batched or deferred. A register must reflect the result of the most recently completed evaluation at all times (RT06-INV-4).

#### 12.4 CUM Critical State Check

After every DomainCoherenceStatus update by O6-10 or O6-11, RT-06 counts degraded domains. If count > 4: generate DOM-000001 escalation; transmit to RT-11. This check is not deferrable (RT06-INV-3).

#### 12.5 Event History Service

RT-06 maintains an indexed event history of all CRE stream positions. On demand from RT-03 (Gate 6 BLOCK) or RT-05 (LOST recovery), RT-06 queries the event history and returns the requested sequence. History is append-only; no modification of historical records is permitted.

## PART 5 — LIFECYCLE AND STATE

### RS-14 — Lifecycle

#### 14.1 Initialization Phase

1. Load all seven CoherenceRegisters from stable storage
2. Validate register integrity: each register must be internally consistent with Reality Fabric state as readable from RT-05
3. Confirm GCR-1 through GCR-7 are loaded from D3 §4
4. Establish event history index; determine last confirmed CRE stream position
5. Establish active MPW timers: for any CRE that was open at prior shutdown, restart MPW tracking; if MPW already exceeded, immediately generate CCR (O6-7)
6. Open connections to RT-05 (fabric read), RT-07 (temporal ordering), RT-01 and RT-02 (event capture)
7. Confirm RT-03 Stage 10 notification channel is active
8. Transition to ACTIVE state

#### 14.2 Active Operation

Once ACTIVE, RT-06 operates two concurrent modes:
- **Continuous Scan** (RS-12 §12.2): periodic evaluation of all active URO objects and relationships
- **Triggered Evaluation** (RS-12 §12.1): Stage 10 evaluation on every RT-03 commit notification

These modes may overlap. Stage 10 takes priority over continuous scan scheduling.

#### 14.3 Stage 10 Execution

See RS-12 §12.1 for the complete Stage 10 sequence. Key properties:
- MPW begins at Stage 10 initiation, not at CRE generation
- Stage 10 failure does not un-commit the Stage 8–9 admission
- Stage 10 must be re-executed from last CRE stream position on recovery (idempotent)

#### 14.4 Recovery

On restart after failure:
1. Re-enter Initialization Phase
2. All registers rebuilt or validated against current Reality Fabric
3. CRE stream position loaded from stable storage
4. Stage 10 resumes from last confirmed position (idempotent)
5. Any MPW timers that expired during downtime: generate CCR immediately upon recovery

#### 14.5 Termination

RT-06 does not self-terminate. Shutdown is constitutional only, initiated through the founding termination protocol. Prior to shutdown:
1. Complete all in-progress Stage 10 evaluations or record exact CRE stream position
2. Write all register state to stable storage
3. Write CRE stream position to stable storage
4. Transmit open CCR count and CUM Critical State to RT-11
5. Record active MPW timer states

### RS-15 — State Model

| State | Description | Entry Condition | Exit Condition |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|----------------|
| INITIALIZING | Loading registers, establishing channels, validating | System start or recovery | Initialization sequence complete |

### RS-18 — Preconditions

- The object must be in an active state in the Reality Fabric (RT-05 readable)
- RT-06 must be in ACTIVE state

After every Stage 10 execution:
- Every admitted object and every propagation frontier object has a completed Stage 10 evaluation record
- Every detected violation has a CRE in the Open CoherenceViolations register
- CoherenceStatus in D2 Layer 5 has been set for every object with an open CRE
- CRE stream position has been updated
- Affected CoherenceRegisters have been updated

After every evaluation cycle:
- CoherenceRegisters reflect the result of the most recent evaluation
- Any violation no longer present in the Reality Fabric has its CRE closed

### RS-20 — Invariants

The following invariants are derived from A0 v1.1 §3.7 (RT06-INV-1 through RT06-INV-5) and constitutional D-series sources:

**RT06-INV-1 — Stage 10 Universality (A0 v1.1 §3.7; D4 §3 Stage 10)**  
Every atomic commit processed by RT-03 (Stages 8–9) triggers MPW and Stage 10 evaluation by RT-06. No commit may be admitted to the Reality Fabric without a corresponding Stage 10 evaluation record. Violation of this invariant is a constitutional violation and must be recorded and escalated.

**RT06-INV-2 — Violation Specificity (A0 v1.1 §3.7; D3 RF-A9)**  
Every coherence violation detected and every CRE generated by RT-06 shall specify the exact GCR violated (GCR-1 through GCR-7) or the exact fabric invariant implicated. Generic "coherence violation" records without specific GCR citation are constitutionally invalid. This invariant cannot be waived.

**RT06-INV-3 — CUM Critical State Escalation (A0 v1.1 §3.7; D7 Part 9)**  
When the count of degraded active domains exceeds four, RT-06 shall generate DOM-000001 and escalate to RT-11. This escalation is mandatory and cannot be suppressed, deferred, or conditional on any other factor. The invariant is evaluated after every DomainCoherenceStatus update.

**RT06-INV-4 — Register Currency (A0 v1.1 §3.7; D3 Part 9 §9.2)**  
All seven CoherenceRegisters shall reflect current constitutional truth at all times. A register may not lag behind the most recently completed evaluation. Register state is not an approximation of the coherence state — it is the authoritative record of the coherence state as determined by RT-06. Staleness is a register corruption condition (RT06-FAIL-01).

**RT06-INV-5 — No Silent Absorption (A0 v1.1 §3.7)**  
No coherence violation detected by RT-06 may be silently absorbed, suppressed, or dismissed without a CRE being generated and added to the Open CoherenceViolations register. A detected violation that produces no CRE is a constitutional failure (RT06-FAIL-06) regardless of the violation's apparent severity.

**D3-EP-P1 through EP-P5 — Propagation Rules (D3 Part 9)**  
RT-06 observes all propagation rules established in D3 Part 9. These rules govern how violations propagate through the coherence fabric, which objects in the propagation frontier inherit violation status, and under what conditions propagation terminates. The full specification of EP-P1 through EP-P5 is authoritative as stated in D3-v1.0-canonical.md Part 9.

**D4-MPW — Mandatory Propagation Window (D4 §9.4)**  
The MPW is a constitutional maximum. RT-06 does not establish the MPW duration (established at founding). RT-06 enforces the MPW: a CRE that exceeds the MPW triggers the consequences specified in O6-7 without exception.

**D3-RF-A5 — Continuous Coherence Evaluability (D3 RF-A5)**  
The Reality Fabric must be continuously coherence-evaluable. RT-06's continuous scan obligation (O6-1, O6-2) is the implementation of RF-A5. The continuous scan may not be suspended or halted without constitutional authorization.

### RS-22 — Recovery Procedures

**RT06-FAIL-01 (Register Corruption):**
1. Halt evaluation; enter FAULT state
2. Read full Reality Fabric from RT-05
3. Rebuild affected CoherenceRegister(s) from Reality Fabric read
4. Generate a CoherenceViolationRecord for each register that required rebuild, recording the corruption event
5. For each violation discovered during rebuild that was not in the pre-corruption register: generate CRE
6. Return to INITIALIZING; complete initialization; re-enter ACTIVE

**RT06-FAIL-02 (MPW Timer Loss):**
1. On recovery, identify all CREs whose MPW state cannot be confirmed from stable storage
2. Mark all such CREs as MPW-Unknown
3. For each MPW-Unknown CRE: generate a CCR (MPW-Unknown is treated as MPW breach for safety); flag objects as Unresolved Propagation; apply Type C suspension
4. Restart MPW tracking from zero for any CREs whose timers had not expired at time of shutdown (where shutdown timestamp is determinable)
5. Record recovery event in event history

**RT06-FAIL-03 (CRE Stream Gap):**
1. Identify last confirmed CRE stream position from stable storage or event history
2. If position is determinable: resume Stage 10 from that position (idempotent; D4 §3 Stage 10)
3. If position is not determinable: treat as RT06-FAIL-01 register corruption; rebuild all registers

**RT06-FAIL-04 (CCR Generation Failure):**
1. Generate constitutional violation record identifying the failure
2. Generate CCR for the unresolvable CRE that did not receive a CCR
3. Escalate to RT-04 for audit
4. Continue operation; do not halt for CCR generation failure

**RT06-FAIL-05 (Escalation Suppression):**
1. Generate constitutional violation record
2. Immediately generate DOM-000001 and transmit to RT-11
3. Escalate to RT-04 for audit

**RT06-FAIL-06 (Silent Absorption):**
1. Generate constitutional violation record — FAIL-06 is a constitutional failure
2. Generate CRE for the absorbed violation retroactively; set CoherenceStatus in D2 Layer 5
3. Escalate to RT-04 for audit

**RT06-FAIL-07 (Stage 10 Skip):**
1. Generate constitutional violation record — FAIL-07 is a constitutional violation of RT06-INV-1
2. Execute Stage 10 retroactively for the skipped commit (idempotent)
3. Escalate to RT-04 for audit

**RT06-FAIL-08 (Generic Violation Record):**
1. Reject the CRE
2. Require re-generation of CRE with specific GCR citation
3. If specific GCR cannot be determined: halt evaluation; escalate to RT-04; do not generate a generic CRE

### RS-23 — Audit Requirements

**RT-04 access:** RT-04 holds read access to all RT-06 owned objects at all times (PAIR 20, A1 v1.0; ADR-3, R0 v1.0). This access is non-delegable and may not be restricted by RT-06.

"Audit-addressable" means: identifiable by a stable reference; readable in full; not modifiable after creation.

**Stage 10 records:** Every Stage 10 evaluation record must be retained and readable by RT-04. The Stage 10 record shall identify: the commit that triggered evaluation; the CRE stream position; the evaluation result for each object evaluated; register updates applied.

**Event history:** The event history maintained for RT-03 (Gate 6) and RT-05 (LOST recovery) service is also fully accessible to RT-04 for audit.

**Retention:** RT-06 does not define retention periods. Retention is a constitutional founding matter. RT-06 obligations are: (a) never modify historical records; (b) maintain audit access for all records in the retention window established at founding.

### RS-24 — Validation Criteria

**CRE Validity:**
- Specifies the admitted operation or scan cycle that triggered evaluation
- Specifies the exact GCR violated (GCR-1 through GCR-7) or exact fabric invariant (not generic)
- Lists all constitutional objects involved
- States resolvability
- States the CoherenceStatus flag to be set in D2 Layer 5
- Does not modify admitted objects

**CCR Validity:**
- References an existing, closed CRE
- States the basis (unresolvable or MPW breach — not generic)
- Lists all affected objects
- Contains Type C suspension flag where applicable
- Transmitted to RT-11

**Register Validity (per register):**
- Content matches the Reality Fabric truth for the register's GCR domain
- Last update timestamp matches the most recent evaluation that affected this register's domain

**DomainCoherenceStatus Validity:**
- Covers all six D6 Part 9 dimensions (§9.2–§9.7) for the domain
- Last evaluation timestamp is within the continuous scan window

**MPW Compliance:**
- Every open CRE has an active, correctly initialized MPW timer
- No open CRE exceeds the MPW without a corresponding CCR and Type C suspension
