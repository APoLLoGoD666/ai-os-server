### Condition 1: CERT-04 Extended Dependents (A0 §4.1 Amendment Pending)

**Criterion:** R0 CERT-04 requires RS-27 dependents to be in bijection with A0 §4.1.  
**Status:** Core Dependents (RT-09, RT-10, RT-11, RT-04) satisfy bijection with A0 §3.8/§4.1. Extended Dependents (RT-08, RT-14) are constitutionally grounded in A0 §3.9 and A0 §3.15 respectively but are not listed in A0 §4.1.

**Action required by:** Constitutional editor for A0 (separate task, lower priority than A1-AMEND).  
**Impact on this certification:** CERT-04 CONDITIONAL PASS. Does not block CONDITIONALLY CERTIFIED verdict.

### Condition 2: CERT-06 A1 Characterization (A1-AMEND-001/002 Pending)

**Criterion:** R0 CERT-06 requires RS-13 characterizations of A1 PAIRs to match A1.  
**Status:** A1 contains systematic errors for RT-07 (wrong identity at §3.0/§5.1; Temporal Sequence Record in §6.1/§7.1; PAIR 17 incorrect Gate 6 blocking direction; AIR-1 incorrectly assigned; RT-14 PAIR missing; §8.1 VC-6 not yet in amendment scope). R7-v1.1 RS-13 correctly adjudicates these as A1 errors and provides conflict disclosures. The CERT-06 condition reflects an irresolvable A1/R7 divergence until A1 is amended.  
**Resolution:** A1-AMEND-001/002 covering: RT-07 identity correction in §3.0/§5.1; AIR-1 removal from §5.1; Temporal Sequence Record removal from §6.1/§7.1; all 10 PAIR recharacterizations; new PAIR for RT-07 ↔ RT-14; revision of §15.2 and line 1783; and expansion of scope to include §8.1 VC-6.

**Impact on this certification:** CERT-06 CONDITIONAL PASS. Does not block CONDITIONALLY CERTIFIED verdict.
